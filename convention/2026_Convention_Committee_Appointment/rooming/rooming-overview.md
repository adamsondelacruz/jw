# Rooming Overview — Plain-Language Guide

## The role in one sentence

The rooming overseer helps the convention care for people who need accommodation, keeps rooming information accurate, and supervises a group of practical service departments so attendees can arrive, get help, use a clean and orderly facility, recover lost property, and resolve rooming problems.

This is both a **Convention Committee role** and a **department role**. Adamson Dela Cruz shares responsibility with the other two committee members for the whole convention, while taking the lead on the departments assigned to the rooming branch. (CO-1 1:6-12, 23-26.)

## What “Rooming” means in everyday language

Rooming is the convention’s accommodation support function. Before the convention, it helps make sure that approved accommodation information and special arrangements are ready. During the convention, it provides a visible help point for attendees who have a booking problem or an urgent last-minute need. It does not mean that the rooming overseer independently negotiates with hotels or personally assigns every complimentary room.

The role also includes practical oversight of several departments. In the standard CO-1 structure these are Cleaning, Information and Volunteer Service, Installation if needed, Lost & Found and Checkroom, Rooming, and Trucking and Equipment if needed. At this Assembly Hall convention, the local chart combines Information and Volunteer with Lost & Found, and does not show separate Installation or Trucking and Equipment departments.

## Who decides what

| Role | Plain-language responsibility | Important boundary |
|---|---|---|
| **Convention Committee** | Makes final convention decisions and shares responsibility for the entire event. | The committee—not the rooming overseer alone—decides who receives complimentary accommodation. |
| **Rooming overseer** | Convention Committee member; oversees the Rooming Department and the practical departments assigned to his branch. | Escalates unusual rooming matters and follows CO-80; he should not bypass the rooming coordinator or the committee. |
| **Rooming coordinator** | Branch-appointed brother involved in hotel surveys and rooming agreements for the convention city. | Hotel contacts may be available only on weekdays; the coordinator is the contact for unusual rooming situations. |
| **Rooming Department overseer** | Organises the on-site desk, assistants/keymen, and routine attendee help under the rooming overseer. | Routine desk matters stay with the department; matters needing authority or exception go to the rooming overseer. |
| **Contract representative** | Handles contracts and dealings with venue management, contractors, and related commercial parties. | Facility requests and contract matters go through him, not directly to venue management. |

Sources: CO-1 1:4-5, 8, 23-25, 27; 2:65-71; 3:78-79.

## Core responsibilities of the rooming overseer

- Carefully study and follow the current *Convention Rooming Guidelines* (CO-80). (1:23; Appendix E.)
- Be involved in the advance hotel rooming survey when directed, under the rooming coordinator’s arrangements. (1:4-5.)
- Keep the online Rooming List accurate. When information changes, notify affected congregations using a freshly downloaded congregation contact list and protect the confidentiality of that data. (1:24.)
- Ensure that an appropriately located Rooming desk is set up for routine problems and last-minute accommodation needs. A large convention may use an additional desk. (1:25; 3:78.)
- Oversee the assigned departments, know their status two to three months ahead, help resolve staffing gaps, and visit them with the rooming overseer assistant each convention day. (1:10-12.)
- Prepare and distribute simple convention department directories to the specified convention personnel. (1:26; 3:51; Appendix E.)
- Work with the cleaning overseer on any preconvention cleaning. (3:34; Appendix E.)
- If departments still need volunteers, arrange for the Information and Volunteer Service overseer to prepare one consolidated letter to the bodies of elders. (3:52-53; Appendix E.)
- Help ensure complimentary and private-home accommodation is available for approved special full-time servants, while leaving assignment decisions to the full Convention Committee. (2:58, 61; 3:79.)
- Consult the rooming coordinator when an unusual rooming situation occurs. (2:71.)
- Participate in convention-wide committee responsibilities, including hospitality for specifically assigned visiting brothers, daily committee coverage, and the postconvention personnel evaluation. (1:7-12; 2:8-11.)

## Accommodation work

### Ordinary attendee help

The Rooming desk should be easy for attendees to find. The assistant rooming overseer and keymen handle normal enquiries, booking problems, and last-minute accommodation needs. If they cannot resolve a matter within approved direction, they consult the rooming overseer. (1:25; 3:78.)

### Special full-time servants

Complimentary hotel rooms and, when necessary, private homes may accommodate missionaries, circuit overseers, Bethelites, special pioneers, and others in special full-time service. A Special Rooming Needs Request (CO-5a) starts the request. The entire Convention Committee decides the assignment. (2:58; 3:79.)

Circuit overseers may instead make their own accommodation arrangements. Appointed helpers assigned to the convention require suitable accommodation, transportation, and meals arranged by the committee. World headquarters makes different arrangements for Governing Body members as described in CO-1. (2:58-64.)

### What CO-1 does not supply

CO-1 does not give the full hotel-survey, hotel-agreement, reservation, room-block, complimentary-room calculation, private-home screening, or Rooming List procedure. It directs the rooming overseer to CO-80 for those details. Do not invent or localise those procedures without checking the current CO-80.

## Online Rooming List and congregation notices

If the online Rooming List changes:

1. Identify exactly which congregations are affected.
2. Use JW Hub Contact Search to obtain their current addresses.
3. Download a fresh spreadsheet for **each** mailing rather than reusing an old list.
4. Send only the necessary correction or updated information.
5. Treat the downloaded contact data as confidential and restrict it to the approved purpose.

Source: CO-1 1:24. Official letters to congregations require Convention Committee approval and should be brief and focused on vital information. (2:12-13.)

## Rooming desk at the venue

The desk should:

- be conveniently located for attendees;
- be staffed by the assistant rooming overseer(s) and keymen while service is needed;
- have current approved rooming information and escalation contacts;
- handle routine matters at desk level;
- avoid keeping unnecessary personal information;
- refer exceptions or unresolved problems to the rooming overseer; and
- refer unusual rooming situations onward to the rooming coordinator when appropriate.

Use the [rooming desk log template](templates/rooming-desk-log-template.md) only for operational follow-up. Record the minimum personal information needed and secure or dispose of it according to current organisational direction.

## Department directories

The rooming overseer prepares simple directories showing the location of every convention department **except areas dealing with accounts**. Supply them to:

- the attendant overseer;
- the information and volunteer service overseer, who gives one to each of that department’s volunteers;
- Convention Committee members and their assistants;
- the convention chairman;
- department overseers as needed.

Do not distribute these directories to attendees generally. (1:26; 3:51.) Use the [department directory template](templates/department-directory-template.md).

## When a problem occurs

| Situation | First contact | Escalation |
|---|---|---|
| Routine attendee rooming enquiry | Rooming desk assistant/keyman | Rooming Department overseer, then rooming overseer if needed |
| Unusual rooming situation | Rooming overseer | Rooming coordinator; resolution may wait for weekday hotel contacts |
| Complimentary-room decision | Rooming overseer gathers facts | Full Convention Committee decides |
| Rooming List correction | Rooming overseer | Notify affected congregations using fresh JW Hub contact data |
| Facility service or contract problem | Responsible department informs committee | Convention Committee coordinator works through contract representative |
| Additional facility use/service request | Committee | Contract representative; branch approval if cost/complication may result |
| Safety hazard | Department overseer / safety coordinator | Convention Committee coordinator |
| Security incident, threat, serious injury, near-injury, or property damage | Convention Committee | Complete TO-5 and submit to Branch Risk Management within 72 hours |

Sources: CO-1 1:25; 2:24, 33, 37, 68-71; 3:50, 78-79.

## Confidentiality and good judgement

- Rooming List congregation contact downloads are confidential. (1:24.)
- Contract terms are confidential. (2:67.)
- Use jwpub.org email for forms and official correspondence when possible. (2:5.)
- Do not store attendee details merely because they may be useful later. Local working registers should contain only the minimum needed and have a clear owner and disposal date.
- Official letters to congregations require full committee approval; other letters require approval from at least one committee member. (2:12.)
