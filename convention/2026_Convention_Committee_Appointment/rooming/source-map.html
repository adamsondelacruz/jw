Source boundary

The local source is Convention Organization Guidelines (CO-1-E BrAUS), February 2026. CO-1 is the organisational starting point, but it expressly sends the rooming overseer to Convention Rooming Guidelines (CO-80) for the full rooming procedure. CO-80 was not found anywhere in this workspace on 10 August 2026.

Verification rule

Before relying on any deadline, form, hotel procedure, or contact route:

  1. Check the current JW Hub convention domain.
  2. Confirm whether CO-1 or CO-80 has been revised.
  3. Follow the Assembly Hall’s current local instructions.
  4. Keep contract and personal information confidential.
  5. If an unusual rooming situation remains, contact the rooming coordinator through the approved route.
