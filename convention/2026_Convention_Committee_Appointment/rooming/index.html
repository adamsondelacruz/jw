2026 Auckland Tagalog Convention — Rooming A-Z Index

South Auckland Assembly Hall · Friday 30 October–Sunday 1 November 2026 · Estimated peak attendance: 451

This is the working home for the Rooming Overseer’s convention documentation. It begins with the controlling instructions in Convention Organization Guidelines (CO-1), February 2026, and the local appointment and organisation documents.

Important source boundary: CO-1 repeatedly requires the rooming overseer to study Convention Rooming Guidelines (CO-80). CO-80 is not present in this workspace. The pages here accurately capture CO-1, but they are not a replacement for CO-80. Obtain the current CO-80 from JW Hub before finalising hotel, private-home, complimentary-room, or Rooming List procedures.
