# Forms and Required Documents Register

## How to use this register

This register lists every coded form, report, badge/card, template, or plan expressly named in CO-1. “Required” means CO-1 expects it in an ordinary convention when the stated condition applies. “Conditional” means it is used only when the described situation or department exists.

Most current forms are downloaded from JW Hub. Always use the current version there; do not recreate an official form from this summary. (CO-1 2:15-17.)

These items do not have a form number in CO-1, but the responsible brothers may need to prepare, receive, or maintain them.

| Rooming List correction notice | Brief, committee-approved notice sent only to affected congregations when the online Rooming List changes. (1:24; 2:12-13.) | As needed |

- [ ] Limit access to brothers who need the form for their assignment.
- [ ] Use jwpub.org email for official forms/correspondence when possible.

- [ ] Apply current retention/destruction direction after the purpose is complete.
