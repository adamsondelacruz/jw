# Rooming Glossary — A-Z

## A <a class="back-top" href="#top">Back to A-Z</a>

**Accommodation** — A place to stay for the convention, such as an approved hotel room or an approved private home.

**Appointed helper** — A helper to a Governing Body committee assigned to the convention. World headquarters handles specified travel; the Convention Committee arranges suitable accommodation, local transportation, and meals. (2:61.)

**Assembly Hall** — A branch-operated venue. Some departments normally needed at commercial facilities may be unnecessary, and venue-specific operating arrangements come through the Assembly Hall overseer. (2:73-78.)

**Assistant rooming overseer** — The committee-member assistant who helps the rooming overseer care for his convention-wide and departmental responsibilities. Locally: Daveson Asuncion.

<a id="b"></a>
## B <a class="back-top" href="#top">Back to A-Z</a>

**Branch form** — An official coded document supplied through approved channels such as JW Hub. A local template must never be represented as a replacement.

**Bethelite** — A member of a Bethel family. CO-1 includes Bethelites among special full-time servants who may be considered for complimentary accommodation under the approved process. (3:79.)

<a id="c"></a>
## C <a class="back-top" href="#top">Back to A-Z</a>

**Checkroom** — A staffed service, used only where customary, that receives and identifies coats, luggage, and packages for later collection. (3:67.)

**Cleaning Department** — The team responsible for preconvention cleaning when needed, assigned seating sections, common areas, restrooms, supplies, rubbish, recycling, and safe daily cleaning. (3:33-41.)

**CO-5a** — *Special Rooming Needs Request*, used by circuit overseers and others in special full-time service to request accommodation assistance. (2:58; 3:79.)

**CO-5b** — *Convention Rooming Contact Information*, used where Internet access is limited or supplied by the branch when requested. (2:29.)

**CO-68** — *Convention Committee Acceptance and Rooming Information*, which confirms committee acceptance and supplies the rooming enquiry phone number. (2:29.)

**CO-80** — *Convention Rooming Guidelines*, the detailed rooming instructions that CO-1 requires the rooming overseer to study and follow. (1:23; Appendix E.)

**Complimentary room** — A hotel room made available without the ordinary charge under the rooming arrangement. It is used for approved special full-time servants; the Convention Committee makes assignments. (3:79.)

**Contract representative** — The branch-appointed brother who handles facility contracts and commercial dealings. Locally: Stuart Brown. Facility requests or contract problems must follow his channel. (2:65-70.)

**Convention and Assembly Desk** — The Service Department desk that gives convention direction, receives required reports, and approves certain actions such as equipment rental/purchase.

**Convention Committee** — Joshua Espiritu, Israel Vinuya, and Adamson Dela Cruz. They share responsibility for the whole convention and make decisions that CO-1 assigns to the committee. (1:6-12.)

**Convention department directory** — A simple internal guide showing department locations except Accounts areas. It is for specified convention workers, not general distribution. (1:26; 3:51.)

<a id="d"></a>
## D <a class="back-top" href="#top">Back to A-Z</a>

**Department overseer** — The brother responsible for organising and operating one convention department, including recruiting and directing approved volunteers. (1:13, 33-39, 42.)

**Desk log** — A local, nonofficial working aid for tracking unresolved rooming matters. It should contain only minimum necessary information and be secured.

<a id="e"></a>
## E <a class="back-top" href="#top">Back to A-Z</a>

**Emergency Response Team (ERT)** — The Convention Committee and attendant overseer, who coordinate during an emergency and maintain communication with the convention chairman. (2:22.)

**Exemplary** — Meeting the spiritual and conduct standard required for specified assignments. CO-1 requires exemplary personnel in a number of convention roles.

<a id="f"></a>
## F <a class="back-top" href="#top">Back to A-Z</a>

**Full-time special service** — In this context, service categories such as missionaries, circuit overseers, Bethelites, and special pioneers who may be considered for complimentary accommodation. (3:79.)

<a id="g"></a>
## G <a class="back-top" href="#top">Back to A-Z</a>

**Governing Body member** — A visiting brother for whom world headquarters handles the travel arrangements described in CO-1. The local committee cares for meals and hospitality as directed. (1:7; 2:59-60.)

<a id="h"></a>
## H <a class="back-top" href="#top">Back to A-Z</a>

**Hotel rooming survey** — The advance review of hotels for future conventions. The rooming coordinator leads it under CO-80, with appointed rooming overseers involved. (1:4-5.)

<a id="i"></a>
## I <a class="back-top" href="#top">Back to A-Z</a>

**Information and Volunteer Service Department** — The team that gives directions and helps departments obtain approved volunteers. At this convention it is combined on the chart with Lost & Found. (3:51-54.)

**Installation Department** — An optional team for setup, signs, maintenance, repairs, stage-related work, and move-out. It is likely unnecessary at an Assembly Hall and is not shown on the local chart. (3:55-62.)

<a id="j"></a>
## J <a class="back-top" href="#top">Back to A-Z</a>

**JW Drive** — A venue-series collaboration space for developing a unified operating plan. It is not a catchall store and does not replace JW Hub or official jwpub.org correspondence. (2:82-83.)

**JW Hub convention domain** — The controlled online area where authorised brothers obtain forms, announcements, assignments, and convention information. (2:15-17.)

<a id="k"></a>
## K <a class="back-top" href="#top">Back to A-Z</a>

**Keyman** — An experienced brother selected by a department overseer to supervise part of a department’s work, assist with recruiting, and keep the overseer informed. (1:38, 42.)

<a id="l"></a>
## L <a class="back-top" href="#top">Back to A-Z</a>

**Last-minute accommodation need** — An urgent need presented close to or during the convention. The Rooming desk helps within approved direction and escalates exceptions. (3:78.)

**Lost & Found** — The always-staffed function that secures found property, verifies claims, assists separated persons, and handles unclaimed items after the convention. (3:63-66.)

<a id="m"></a>
## M <a class="back-top" href="#top">Back to A-Z</a>

**Missionary** — A person in special full-time service who may be considered for complimentary accommodation through the approved process. (3:79.)

<a id="n"></a>
## N <a class="back-top" href="#top">Back to A-Z</a>

**Necessary personnel** — Only the people genuinely needed to operate a department should remain at its location during the program. (2:18.)

<a id="o"></a>
## O <a class="back-top" href="#top">Back to A-Z</a>

**Online Rooming List** — The current approved online accommodation information. When it changes, the rooming overseer notifies affected congregations using freshly downloaded, confidential contact data. (1:24.)

<a id="p"></a>
## P <a class="back-top" href="#top">Back to A-Z</a>

**Private-home accommodation** — An approved place in a local brother’s home used when needed for those qualifying under rooming direction. CO-80 supplies the fuller procedure. (2:58; 3:79.)

**Program overseer** — Convention Committee member overseeing program-related departments. Locally: Israel Vinuya.

<a id="q"></a>
## Q <a class="back-top" href="#top">Back to A-Z</a>

**Qualified brother** — A brother who meets the Scriptural, experience, age, availability, and conduct requirements for an assigned oversight role. (1:33-41.)

<a id="r"></a>
## R <a class="back-top" href="#top">Back to A-Z</a>

**Rooming coordinator** — The branch-appointed brother who conducts/coordinates hotel rooming survey work and helps with unusual rooming situations. Locally: Lewis Olding. (1:4-5; 2:71.)

**Rooming Department** — The team operating the on-site accommodation help point. Locally led by Meliton Soriano with Romel Lumiguid. (3:78-79.)

**Rooming desk** — The conveniently located on-site point for attendees with rooming problems or last-minute accommodation needs. (1:25; 3:78.)

**Rooming enquiry number** — The number submitted on CO-68 and made available to congregation elders through JW Hub. It should not be copied into general documentation without a need. (2:29.)

**Rooming overseer** — Convention Committee member who also oversees the Rooming Department and assigned practical departments. Locally: Adamson Dela Cruz. (1:23-26.)

<a id="s"></a>
## S <a class="back-top" href="#top">Back to A-Z</a>

**Special Rooming Needs Request** — See **CO-5a**.

**Special pioneer** — A person in special full-time service who may be considered for complimentary accommodation under the approved process. (3:79.)

<a id="t"></a>
## T <a class="back-top" href="#top">Back to A-Z</a>

**Trucking and Equipment Department** — An optional team that locates, records, transports, and returns convention equipment. It is likely unnecessary at an Assembly Hall and is not shown on the local chart. (3:80-85.)

<a id="u"></a>
## U <a class="back-top" href="#top">Back to A-Z</a>

**Unusual rooming situation** — A matter outside routine desk handling that may require the rooming overseer to consult the rooming coordinator. Hotel contacts may be unavailable until a weekday. (2:71.)

<a id="v"></a>
## V <a class="back-top" href="#top">Back to A-Z</a>

**Volunteer** — An approved person serving in a convention department. Personal recruiting is normal; Information and Volunteer Service assists when departments remain short. (1:42-43; 3:52-54.)

<a id="w"></a>
## W <a class="back-top" href="#top">Back to A-Z</a>

**Working aid** — A locally created checklist, register, or template. It helps organise work but is not an official branch form and cannot change official direction.
