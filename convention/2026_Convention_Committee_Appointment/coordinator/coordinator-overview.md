# Convention Committee Coordinator — Role Overview

## The role in one sentence

You chair the Convention Committee, keep the whole convention moving as one coordinated operation, directly supervise Accounts, Attendant, First Aid, and Parking, represent the committee on facility-related contract matters, and personally complete the coordinator’s required reports and follow-up.

## What the appointment letter requires now

1. Ask the program overseer and rooming overseer whether they accept.
2. If all three accept, complete and send CO-68 to the Convention and Assembly Desk **immediately**.
3. If either brother cannot accept, call the Desk immediately.
4. Each committee member corrects his own mailing address and phone details in JW Hub.
5. Use JW Hub Convention Information to assign convention personnel.

CO-68 is therefore the formal immediate response. A normal email can accompany it, but it is not a substitute for the form.

## Your own responsibilities

- Chair committee meetings and set a cooperative pace. (CO-1 1:15.)
- Represent the committee when handling facility matters included in the contract; route unresolved contract issues through the contract representative. (1:15; 2:68-70.)
- Before inviting department overseers or assistants, consult the convention chairman about Chairman’s Office personnel needs. (1:36.)
- Prepare and distribute the master CO-53 early; lead the postconvention review; personally submit it within one week. (2:8-11.)
- Submit CO-62 through JW Hub on the morning after the convention. (1:16.)
- Forward and retain accounting records exactly as CO-63 directs. (1:16-17.)
- Submit CO-100 if this committee is responsible for the first convention in the language and signs are actually needed. (2:77; 3:57.)
- Review property-damage TO-5 reports and verify all reportable incidents are submitted within 72 hours. (2:24, 37; 3:50; Appendix C.)
- Obtain CO-161 from the Assembly Hall overseer and make it available to the Event Response Team and assistants. (2:22, 73.)
- Provide an assigned media spokesman’s contact details to the Attendant and Information/Volunteer overseers and assistants. (2:84-88; Appendix C.)
- With your assistant, visit each department you supervise daily if possible. (1:12; Appendix C.)

## Full-committee work you chair and track

The committee collectively selects assistants, department overseers and safety personnel; confirms department locations and staffing; arranges the preconvention meeting; makes complimentary-room decisions; determines count timing; and keeps at least two committee members on site whenever the facility is open for convention use. See [the checklist](coordinator-checklist.md).

## Work that remains with department overseers

Each overseer organises and runs his department, recruits and trains approved personnel, completes a DC-85 before hazardous preconvention work, and gives the committee a reliable status. Your role is oversight and help—not taking over routine departmental decisions.

## Key timing

| When | Coordinator focus |
|---|---|
| **Immediately** | Acceptance; CO-68; contacts; first committee meeting; verify Convention Information. |
| **Now—2 to 3 months before** | All-department status, staffing help, safety appointments, preconvention meeting, CO-161, CO-53 distribution. |
| **Before move-in** | Facility/department arrangements, CO-100 decision, media contacts, emergency and safety briefings. |
| **During** | Daily visits; two committee members on site; CO-53 observations; incidents and escalation. |
| **Morning after** | Submit CO-62. |
| **Within 72 hours of incident** | Ensure TO-5 submission. |
| **Within one week after** | Personally submit CO-53; complete accounting actions under CO-63. |

## Source boundary

This pack summarises CO-1 and the appointment letter. It does not replace current JW Hub announcements, CO-63 accounting instructions, the completed CO-161 plan, department instructions, or Assembly Hall operating direction.
