## Committee-wide work you must chair or monitor

- [ ] Select/confirm committee assistants, department overseers/assistants, and safety coordinator/assistants.
- [ ] Confirm current CO-1 access and relevant department instructions.
- [ ] Verify the real status of every department now; help with shortages.
- [ ] Confirm elders’ approval and age/oversight requirements for volunteers.

### Preconvention meeting

- [ ] Invite all department overseers and assistants plus safety coordinator/assistants.
- [ ] Keep it to about one hour, opening and closing with prayer; no Bible talk or song is needed.
- [ ] Collect concise reports: staffing, training, equipment, locations, safety hazards, dependencies, and decisions needed.
- [ ] Record decisions, owners, and due dates in the [department tracker](templates/department-status-tracker-template.md).

- [ ] Confirm department locations, seating sections, mothers’ room, rubbish arrangements, parking arrangements, and any concurrent-language needs.
- [ ] Confirm current CO-161 distribution and Event Response Team assignments.
- [ ] Confirm every hazardous preconvention task has a DC-85 and worker briefing.
- [ ] Confirm Attendant count times and CO-24 arrangements.
- [ ] Confirm media contact/escalation arrangements if a spokesman is assigned.
- [ ] Confirm complimentary accommodation decisions with the full committee from CO-5a requests.
