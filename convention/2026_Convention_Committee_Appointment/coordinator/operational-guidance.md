<h2 id="answer">Baptism candidates</h2>

> **Decision:** The Convention Committee and coordinator do **not** need candidates’ names in advance. The committee needs assurance that the Baptism Department is ready. The coordinator needs the final number actually baptized after the baptism for the Postconvention Report (CO-62).

[CO-1 3:29](../CO-1.html#co1-3-29) expressly says that congregations should not be asked to provide a list of candidates’ names to the Baptism Department. It permits an inquiry about disabled or infirm candidates who will need special assistance.

An approximate total may be considered only when genuinely needed for operational planning. This is a practical inference from [CO-1 3:30](../CO-1.html#co1-3-30), which mentions an “anticipated number” when deciding whether a second pool is needed. CO-1 does not require an advance count and does not authorize creating a candidate masterlist.

<h2 id="responsibility-flow">Responsibility flow</h2>

| Stage | Responsible brother(s) | Information needed |
|---|---|---|
| Department preparation | Baptism Overseer | Staffing, changing rooms, candidate direction, seating coordination, pool readiness, First Aid coverage and any special-assistance needs. |
| Committee-member oversight | Program Overseer | Confirmation that the Baptism Department is ready and any operational matter requiring committee help. |
| Committee decision, if applicable | Convention Committee | Whether announcing candidates’ congregations is necessary and practical; whether capacity requires another pool. |
| During/after baptism | Baptism Overseer | Count everyone actually baptized and give the final figure to the Coordinator. |
| Morning after convention | Coordinator | Submit the final baptism figure with attendance figures in CO-62 through JW Hub. |


<h2 id="before-and-after">What the committee needs—and does not need</h2>

### Before the convention

The Program Overseer should confirm:

- The Baptism Overseer and assistants are prepared and adequately staffed.
- Seating, changing rooms, attendants, crowd control, First Aid coverage and pool arrangements are ready.
- Any disabled or infirm candidate needing special assistance can be accommodated.
- Any capacity issue requiring a committee decision has been raised.

The committee does **not** need:

- A list of candidates’ names.
- Confirmation of whether a person is being rebaptized.
- Confirmation that the elders approved a particular candidate.
- Personal information unrelated to a specific accommodation or safety need.

### After the baptism

The Baptism Overseer gives the Coordinator the final number actually baptized. The Coordinator includes that figure in CO-62 on the morning after the convention. The figure must also include applicable tie-in congregations as directed in [CO-1 1:16](../CO-1.html#co1-1-16).

<h2 id="safe-inquiry">Suggested limited inquiry</h2>

If the Baptism Overseer needs to contact congregations, use a limited request such as:

> Please advise the Baptism Department only if your congregation has a baptism candidate who is disabled or infirm and will require special assistance at the convention. Please do not submit a general list of baptism candidates.

If a genuine pool-capacity or staffing decision requires an estimate, request only an approximate aggregate number—without names—and retain no candidate masterlist.
