CO-162 Streaming Meeting — Presiding Notes

Auckland NS (TG) — 2026 · Controlled local working notes based on Instructions for Livestreaming Conventions (CO-162), March 2026

Chair: Adamson dela Cruz
Suggested attendees: Convention Committee, Program Overseer Assistant, Audio/Video Overseer, proposed Information Technology Overseer, and other brothers needed for decisions
Purpose: Confirm the approved streaming configuration, establish clear ownership, and agree on the preparation and day-of-convention workflow.

These notes highlight decisions and responsibilities. They do not replace CO-162, CO-160, CO-160a, or direction from the Service Department and Local Broadcasting Department.

Desired outcomes

By the end of the discussion, we should have:

  • Confirmed the written branch direction approving streaming and whether onsite, offsite, or both configurations are approved.
  • Confirmed the Information Technology Overseer and the brothers who will serve as stream technicians.
  • Agreed on equipment, network, testing, monitoring, attendance, link-distribution, and escalation arrangements.
  • Recorded an owner and due date for every remaining action.

Suggested opening

“The purpose of this discussion is not to decide independently whether to stream. The Service Department determines whether streaming is used and which configuration is permitted. Our responsibility is to confirm that direction and make arrangements that follow it closely.”

References: CO-162 Chapter 1, paragraphs 1-3; Chapter 2, paragraphs 1-3.

1. Confirm the approved configuration before organizing the crew

  • Onsite streaming supplies the program to isolated departments within the venue.
  • Offsite streaming supplies the program to publishers unable to attend in person.
  • A direct cable from the video desk remains the preferred method for an onsite department. Streaming is used when a direct connection is not practical.
  • The Service Department determines whether streaming is used and whether onsite, offsite, or both are permitted.
  • The committee must wait for that confirmation before making arrangements.
  • The Local Broadcasting Department manages the streaming configuration and receives technical questions.

Ask:

  1. What written direction confirms streaming for Auckland NS (TG)?
  2. Is our approval onsite, offsite, or both?
  3. Does every approved language/event appear correctly in JW Stream?
  4. Which departments genuinely need streaming because a direct cable is impractical?

Reference: CO-162 1:1-3 and 2:1-3.

The IT crew is not a separate convention department. It works under the Audio/Video Overseer, who works under the Program Overseer.

3. Use the correct stream method

  • A broadcast stream has a slight delay and should be used whenever possible because it costs the organization less.
  • A real-time stream has no noticeable delay but has a higher organizational cost.
  • Only 25 real-time connections are available for the convention.
  • Appropriate real-time uses include the Chairman’s Office or Convention Committee Office when a wired feed is impractical, and certain department locations where hearing the auditorium loudspeakers would conflict with a delayed broadcast.
  • Do not use a real-time connection merely for convenience.

4. Protect links and permissions

  • Give Manager permission only to brothers who need to manage the convention stream.
  • Give Streamer permission to technicians who start, monitor, and stop the stream, selecting only the language they support.
  • Create a unique real-time link for each approved department and require login.
  • Create a uniquely labelled broadcast link for each approved department.
  • Offsite links for publishers are distributed by congregation elders in harmony with current organizational direction.
  • Do not circulate links generally or reuse a department link for an unrelated audience.
  • Do not change JW Stream visibility or other configuration settings beyond the documented procedure unless directed by the Local Broadcasting Department.

References: CO-162 2:9-12; CO-162 3:2-5.

5. Follow the preparation timeline

After approval is received

  • AV Overseer confirms that the equipment needed to send the stream will be available.
  • AV Overseer assigns the IT Overseer the Manager role.
  • IT Overseer assigns Streamer permissions and prepares a full-day monitoring schedule.

No more than 30 days before the convention

  • AV Overseer confirms that every approved event and language is visible in JW Stream.
  • Do not alter other visibility settings unless directed.

One week before

  • IT Overseer confirms that all key personnel have the correct permissions.
  • Every stream technician verifies that he can log in and see the stream meeting interface.
  • IT Overseer creates and distributes only the approved real-time department links.

Setup day

  • Place the streaming computer close to the video desk when possible.
  • Prefer a wired Internet connection. If distance is an issue, prefer a long network cable over a long SDI cable.
  • If wired Internet is unavailable, use a private wireless network.
  • Test the stream using the first song—not the musical prelude—from the convention playlist.
  • Maintain proper decorum because anyone possessing the link may see the test.

Each convention day

  • Reset JW Stream attendance before starting the broadcast.
  • Start the stream well before the public enters.
  • Keep a brother monitoring the streaming computer throughout the day.
  • Periodically verify independently that the broadcast has both audio and video.

References: CO-162 2:10-14.

6. Agree on attendance handling

  • Volunteers viewing an internal broadcast stream enter 0 in the attendance field when connecting.
  • Congregations permitted to tie in at a Kingdom Hall or other remote location do not use JW Stream to report their tied-in attendance through the individual connection count.
  • At the normal attendance-count time, the IT Overseer gives the JW Stream attendance figure to the Attendant Overseer.
  • The Convention Committee Coordinator ensures that the applicable JW Stream attendance is included on CO-62.
  • Reset the JW Stream attendance to zero before starting the broadcast each morning.

Confirm the reporting chain:

JW Stream analytics → IT Overseer → Attendant Overseer → Convention Committee Coordinator → CO-62

References: CO-162 2:6-7 and 2:14; CO-162 3:9-12.

7. Prepare for technical failure

  • Direct technical questions to the Local Broadcasting Department.
  • Where provided, configure a primary and backup streaming computer.
  • Confirm beforehand who takes action if the primary computer, ingest device, or network fails.
  • Minor intermittent network issues may sometimes be tolerable; persistent hardware or network failure requires the CO-162 troubleshooting procedure.
  • Do not improvise configuration changes that fall outside the approved design.

Ask:

  • What is our primary computer and ingest path?
  • What backup equipment is available?
  • Who contacts the Local Broadcasting Department?
  • How will the IT crew communicate with AV and the committee during an incident?

Reference: CO-162 3:13-17.

Suggested closing summary

“Streaming is an Audio/Video responsibility carried out by the IT crew under the approved branch configuration. Our priorities are to protect access, use the least costly suitable stream method, prepare and test early, monitor continuously, report attendance correctly, and contact the Local Broadcasting Department rather than independently changing the design.”
