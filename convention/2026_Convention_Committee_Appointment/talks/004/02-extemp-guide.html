Extemporaneous Guide — Talk No. 4

“He Will Not Quarrel” — 17 minutes

One sentence: Jesus fulfilled Isaiah’s prophecy by exercising justice with quiet humility and tender compassion; we imitate him by directing attention to Jehovah, reasoning peacefully, and caring for people.

Main memory path: SEE → READ → JUSTICE → QUIET → TENDER → JEHOVAH → REASON → CARE → HANDOFF

Delivery rule: Do not memorise every sentence. Know the path, the exact video and reading cues, the section endings, and the final handoff.

WHAT WAS PROPHESIED? — 3:00

SEE — Authority under control

  • Link to symposium: quality easy to overlook because it is quiet.
  • Messiah establishes justice without making himself centre.
  • Set Video 1 question: What authority did Jesus have, and how did he use it?

VIDEO 1 — 02-CO-v26_E_041
Outline 0:46; actual file 0:50. Allow the final frame to settle.

  • Spirits recognise / fall before Jesus.
  • He orders them not to make him known.
  • Key line: “That was power under perfect control.”

READ — Isaiah’s balance

READ ISAIAH 42:1-4.

  • Justice to nations.
  • Not shout / crush damaged reed / extinguish weak flame.
  • Key line: “True justice in Messiah’s hands: faithful, quiet, compassionate.”
  • Bridge: How was every part fulfilled?

HOW WAS THE PROPHECY FULFILLED? — 8:00

JUSTICE — Person, not merely a case

  • Matthew 12:15-18 links prophecy to Jesus.
  • Leaders’ legalism could judge instead of help.
  • Jesus’ justice = compassion + mercy for poor, ill, discouraged.
  • Key contrast: “Where legalism saw a case, Jesus saw a person.”

QUIET — No personal publicity

  • Matthew 12:19: no self-promotion or sensation.
  • Cures often followed by instruction not to advertise.
  • Set Video 2: instruction + consequence when ignored.

VIDEO 2 — 04-CO-v26_E_042
Outline 0:52; actual file 0:56.

  • Mark 1:40-44: tell no one; follow Law arrangement.
  • Publicity forced Jesus into isolated places.
  • Bethzatha, John 5:2-13: healed man did not even know who Jesus was.
  • Mark 3:11, 12 note: demons were rebels; Jesus rejected their witness.
  • Key thought: Jehovah’s way and Jehovah’s time.

TENDER — Bruised reed / smouldering wick

  • Matthew 12:20, 21.
  • Bent by hardship / almost lost hope.
  • Jesus did not treat fragile people as disposable.
  • Balance:
    • would not amplify own name;
    • would not overlook another’s quiet pain.
  • Section landing: justice without quarrelling; hope without spectacle; strength without crushing.

WHAT CAN WE LEARN FROM THE MESSIAH? — 6:00

JEHOVAH — Direct the spotlight correctly

  • Matthew 6:2, 5: not like attention-seeking hypocrites.
  • Matthew 9:2-8: crowd glorified God.
  • John 7:16-18: teaching from the One who sent him.
  • Use Bible evidence; help listener conclude.
  • Light line: “If they remember our illustration but forget the scripture, the spotlight landed wrong.”

REASON — No scorecard

  • 2 Timothy 2:24 study note: no verbal fighting/contentious debate.
  • Light line: “A conversation is not a contest with a scorecard.”
  • Mildness / thoughtful question / Bible evidence.

CARE — Give the spark air

  • Mark 1:41: pity before help.
  • Love People, lesson 3: sense our care → more likely to listen.
  • Listen / understand / speak kindly / offer help.
  • Reuse controlling image:
    • bruised reed = bent by life;
    • smouldering wick = weak spark of interest;
    • pressure could extinguish; kindness gives air.
  • Matthew 5:3: help person express spiritual need.

LANDING + HANDOFF

  • Final three: Give Jehovah the attention; people the evidence; discouraged tender care.
