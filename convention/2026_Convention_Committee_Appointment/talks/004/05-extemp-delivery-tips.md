# Delivery Tips — Talk No. 4

## Core idea

Jesus’ authority was never self-promoting or crushing. It was quiet, just, and tender. The delivery should feel the same: confident, unhurried, and warm.

## What to memorise

1. The memory path: **SEE → READ → JUSTICE → QUIET → TENDER → JEHOVAH → REASON → CARE → HANDOFF**.
2. Both video setup questions and the first sentence after each video.
3. The exact reading cue for Isaiah 42:1-4.
4. The “bruised reed / smouldering wick” explanation.
5. The three-part landing and exact symposium handoff.

## Rehearsal passes

1. **Content pass:** Deliver from the guide without a timer. Check that every outline point is present.
2. **Media pass:** Practise the exact video cues, allow the final frame to settle, then resume without repeating the whole clip.
3. **Timing pass:** Use the actual English file runtimes, not only the rounded outline times.
4. **Warmth pass:** Record yourself. The bruised-reed portion should sound tender, not clinical.
5. **Platform pass:** Practise turning naturally toward the next speaker for the handoff.

## Voice and body

- Opening/video authority: firm and expectant.
- Isaiah reading: slower than ordinary speech; stress **justice**, **crushed reed**, **smoldering wick**, and **faithfulness**.
- Religious leaders / Jesus contrast: do not sound contemptuous.
- Applications: increase eye contact and shorten sentences.
- Humour: use only one of the two light lines in the manuscript if it comes naturally. Do not wait for laughter.

## Recovery bridges

- After Video 1: “That was power under perfect control.”
- Into fulfilment: “How was every part of Isaiah’s prophecy fulfilled?”
- Into applications: “What does the Messiah’s manner teach us?”
- If a thought is lost: “Notice again the balance in Isaiah’s picture.”
- Final recovery: “Give Jehovah the attention, people the evidence, and the discouraged tender care.”

## Final checklist

- [ ] Video operator has the two exact English files in order.
- [ ] Isaiah 42:1-4 is marked and opens quickly.
- [ ] No other scripture is presented as a formal audience reading.
- [ ] The videos and Isaiah reading are included in the rehearsal clock.
- [ ] The next speaker’s name and readiness are confirmed.
- [ ] Finish at 17:00 without adding comments after the handoff.
