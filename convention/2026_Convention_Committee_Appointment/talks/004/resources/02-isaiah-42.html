Isaiah 42:1-4 — Required Reading

Official text: Isaiah 42, Study Bible

Outline use

  • This is the talk’s only designated audience reading.
  • It belongs after Video 1 and before the fulfilment section.
  • Read all four verses without interruption.

Main thought

Jehovah’s approved servant establishes justice faithfully. His manner is neither noisy nor crushing. The servant’s strength, quietness, and tenderness belong together.

REIA preparation

  • Read: Isaiah 42:1-4 in full.
  • Explain: Justice is not weak, but neither is it harsh or self-promoting in the Messiah’s hands.
  • Illustrate: Use Isaiah’s own two-part picture—a bruised reed and a smouldering wick—as the controlling image for the talk.
  • Apply: Jesus’ followers do not promote themselves, quarrel, or put extra pressure on people already bent by life.

Reading emphasis

Stress the movement from justice, to no crushed reed, to in faithfulness, and finally to establishes justice. Do not rush the imagery.
