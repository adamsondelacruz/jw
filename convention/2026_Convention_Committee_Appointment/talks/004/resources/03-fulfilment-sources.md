# How the Prophecy Was Fulfilled

## Matthew 12:15-21

- [Matthew 12, Study Bible](https://www.jw.org/en/library/bible/study-bible/books/matthew/12/)
- [Isaiah’s Prophecy II, chapter 3](https://wol.jw.org/en/wol/d/r1/lp-e/1102001023)
- [Jesus—The Way, chapter 80](https://wol.jw.org/en/wol/d/r1/lp-e/1102014636)

**Use:** Matthew explicitly applies Isaiah’s prophecy to Jesus. The cited commentary contrasts harsh, legalistic treatment with Jesus’ compassionate and merciful justice. Jesus did not advertise himself noisily; he comforted people who were lowly or discouraged.

**Talk line:** “Where legalism saw a case, Jesus saw a person.”

## Mark 1:40-44

[Mark 1, Study Bible](https://www.jw.org/en/library/bible/study-bible/books/mark/1/)

**Use:** Jesus cures a man with leprosy, directs him to the Law’s arrangement, and firmly tells him not to publicise the cure. Video 2 carries this event beyond verse 44 and shows the effect of the publicity.

## John 5:2-13

[John 5, Study Bible](https://www.jw.org/en/library/bible/study-bible/books/john/5/)

**Use:** The man healed at Bethzatha did not even know Jesus’ identity because Jesus had moved away into the crowd. This supports the lack of self-promotion in a single sentence.

## Mark 3:11, 12 study note

[Mark 3, Study Bible](https://www.jw.org/en/library/bible/study-bible/books/mark/3/)

**Use:** The demons were rebels and enemies of Jehovah. Jesus would not accept wicked spirits as witnesses who publicised his identity. Connect this directly to Video 1.
