# Reason and Show Personal Interest

## 2 Timothy 2:24 — “fight”

[2 Timothy 2, Study Bible](https://www.jw.org/en/library/bible/study-bible/books/2-timothy/2/)

**Use:** The study note allows the sense of verbal fighting, quarrels, and contentious debate. Jesus’ follower remains mild and qualified to teach.

**Application:** The objective is not to win an exchange. Ask, listen, reason, and let the Scriptures do their work.

## Mark 1:41

[Mark 1, Study Bible](https://www.jw.org/en/library/bible/study-bible/books/mark/1/)

**Use:** Jesus is moved with pity before helping the man with leprosy. Correct teaching and sincere feeling are not alternatives.
