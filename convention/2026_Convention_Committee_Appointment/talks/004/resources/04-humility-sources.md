# Direct Attention to Jehovah

## “Street”

[Insight on the Scriptures—“Street”](https://www.jw.org/en/library/books/Insight-on-the-Scriptures/Street/)

**Use:** Jesus taught and healed publicly, but did not wrangle, create a sensation, magnify his own name, or draw attention away from Jehovah and the Kingdom.

## Matthew 6:2, 5

[Matthew 6, Study Bible](https://www.jw.org/en/library/bible/study-bible/books/matthew/6/)

**Use:** The hypocrites practised good works and prayer to be noticed. This is a brief contrast, not a separate exposition.

## Matthew 9:2-8

[Matthew 9, Study Bible](https://www.jw.org/en/library/bible/study-bible/books/matthew/9/)

**Use:** After Jesus heals the paralysed man, the crowd glorifies God. Jesus’ ministry directs attention upward.

## John 7:16-18

[John 7, Study Bible](https://www.jw.org/en/library/bible/study-bible/books/john/7/)

**Use:** Jesus identifies the source of his teaching as the One who sent him. A teacher seeking Jehovah’s glory is truthful and free of unrighteousness.

**Application:** Let Bible evidence carry the thought. Help the listener reach a sound conclusion rather than admire the speaker.
