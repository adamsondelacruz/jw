Delivery Tips — Talk No. 13

Core idea

This is not primarily a catalogue of six rules. It is a guided look at Jehovah’s personality. The audience should leave asking, “What does this principle teach me about him?”

What to memorise

  1. The memory path: HAND → HEART → PEACE → PURITY → LOYALTY → TRUTH → JUSTICE → LOVE → PERSON.
  2. The recurring question: “What does this teach me about Jehovah, and how should it shape my choices?”
  3. One principle sentence and one application for each of the six laws.
  4. Both video setup and exit lines.
  5. The final road-crossing callback and final sentence.

Rehearsal passes

  1. Hierarchy pass: Name all six laws in order without notes.
  2. Principle pass: For each, state Jehovah’s quality before giving the application.
  3. Media pass: Rehearse with the actual files. Video 2 is the major illustration; do not retell it scene by scene afterward.
  4. Clock pass: The two videos consume about 8:26. Rehearse the full assignment, not narration alone.
  5. Warmth pass: Avoid sounding like a list of prohibitions. Emphasise peace, purity, loyalty, truth, justice, and love.

Scripture handling

  • The outline designates no formal audience readings.
  • Cite each scripture naturally and paraphrase only the point assigned in the outline.
  • Do not ask the audience to turn to six passages; that would consume time and alter the outline’s delivery design.
  • Do not quote more of a verse than needed to identify Jesus’ example.

Video 2 handling

  • Before: ask listeners to notice the move from minimum compliance to love.
  • After: give only four beats—unfairness, initial reaction, meditation/prayer, proactive help.
  • Land on: “He did not merely suppress revenge; he replaced it with goodness.”
  • Avoid speculating about the manager, colleague, or employment outcome beyond what the video shows.

Recovery bridges

  • Into examples: “What does this teach me about Jehovah, and how should it shape my choices?”
  • If the order is lost: peace, purity, loyalty, truth, justice, love.
  • After Video 2: “What changed was not the unfair situation first; it was his understanding of love.”
  • Into conclusion: “All six examples reveal the same Person.”
  • Final recovery: “A principle shows the reason, and the reason reveals Jehovah’s thinking.”
