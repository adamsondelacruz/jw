# Love Your Enemies

## Matthew 5:43-48

- [Matthew 5, Study Bible](https://www.jw.org/en/library/bible/study-bible/books/matthew/5/)
- [What Does It Mean to Love Your Enemies?](https://www.jw.org/en/bible-teachings/questions/love-your-enemies-meaning/)

Outline publication: *Come Be My Follower*, p. 103 ¶14.

**Source distinction:** The Hebrew Scriptures commanded love of neighbour; they did not command hatred of enemies. Jesus points to the Father’s impartial gifts of sun and rain.

## 1 John 4:8, 19

[1 John 4, Study Bible](https://www.jw.org/en/library/bible/study-bible/books/1-john/4/)

**Principle:** Jehovah is love. We learn to love because he loved first, so another person’s hostility does not have to dictate our character.

**Application:** Avoid partiality, speak respectfully, remain kind, and leave open the possibility that an opposer may later respond. Love does not approve wrongdoing.

## Romans 13:8-10 and Matthew 28:19

- [Romans 13, Study Bible](https://www.jw.org/en/library/bible/study-bible/books/romans/13/)
- [Matthew 28, Study Bible](https://www.jw.org/en/library/bible/study-bible/books/matthew/28/)

**Use:** Love fulfils the Law because it does no harm. Christian commands, including making disciples, reflect Jehovah’s love and are best obeyed when we understand that loving purpose.
