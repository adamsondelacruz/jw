# Love the Person Behind the Principle

## Matthew 11:27

[Matthew 11, Study Bible](https://www.jw.org/en/library/bible/study-bible/books/matthew/11/)

**Use:** No one knows the Father better than Jesus, and Jesus reveals him. The six Sermon on the Mount examples reveal Jehovah’s personality, not merely stricter rules.

## Galatians 6:2

[Galatians 6, Study Bible](https://www.jw.org/en/library/bible/study-bible/books/galatians/6/)

**Use:** The law of the Christ also contains commands and principles that reveal a loving Lawgiver.

## Proverbs 2:4, 9

[Proverbs 2, Study Bible](https://www.jw.org/en/library/bible/study-bible/books/proverbs/2/)
