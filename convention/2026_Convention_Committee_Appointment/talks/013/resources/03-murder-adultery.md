# Murder and Adultery

## Murder — Matthew 5:21-26

[Matthew 5, Study Bible](https://www.jw.org/en/library/bible/study-bible/books/matthew/5/)

Outline publications: *The Watchtower*, February 15, 2006, p. 31 ¶6-7; *Come Be My Follower*, p. 103 ¶13.

**Principle:** Jehovah, the God of peace, cares about the anger, hatred, and contempt from which murder begins. He holds a worshipper accountable for heart attitudes, not only the final violent act.

**Application:** Make peace quickly. Take the initiative, speak privately and calmly, listen, and apologise where needed. One person cannot force reconciliation, but can remove fuel for hatred from his own heart.

## Adultery — Matthew 5:27-30

- [Matthew 5, Study Bible](https://www.jw.org/en/library/bible/study-bible/books/matthew/5/)
- [“Wisdom for the Eye,” Awake! February 2012](https://www.jw.org/en/library/magazines/g201202/Wisdom-for-the-Eye/)

**Principle:** Jehovah is holy and values loyal love. Deliberately cultivating sexual desire outside marriage is disloyal even before a physical act occurs.

**Application:** Take decisive measures against pornography and sources of temptation. Positively cultivate contentment, affection, and loyal attention toward one’s own mate.

**Boundary:** Jesus’ eye/hand language calls for decisive action, not literal injury.
