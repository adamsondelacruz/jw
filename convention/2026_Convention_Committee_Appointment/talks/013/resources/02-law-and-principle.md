# Law and Principle

## Psalm 119:104

[Psalm 119, Study Bible](https://www.jw.org/en/library/bible/study-bible/books/psalms/119/)

**Use:** Understanding gained from Jehovah’s orders helps a worshipper hate and reject false paths. Knowing the reason supports willing obedience.

## Matthew 5:17-48

[Matthew 5, Study Bible](https://www.jw.org/en/library/bible/study-bible/books/matthew/5/)

**Use:** Jesus did not abolish or criticise the Mosaic Law. In the six “you heard . . . however, I say” examples, he exposed fundamental truths and heart attitudes behind commands.

## Colossians 2:13, 14

[Colossians 2, Study Bible](https://www.jw.org/en/library/bible/study-bible/books/colossians/2/)

**Use:** The written Law covenant was taken out of the way. Do not imply that Christians remain under that covenant.

## Malachi 3:6

[Malachi 3, Study Bible](https://www.jw.org/en/library/bible/study-bible/books/malachi/3/)

**Use:** Jehovah does not change. The Law covenant ended, but the righteous qualities and timeless truths it reflected did not.

## Video 1 concept

A particular rule—hold a parent’s hand while crossing—may cease when the child matures. The principles behind it, the value of life and parental responsibility, remain. Principles reveal the reason and dignify a mature person to make loving choices.
