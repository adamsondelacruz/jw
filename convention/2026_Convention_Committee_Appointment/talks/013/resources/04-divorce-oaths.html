Divorce and Oaths

Divorce — Matthew 5:31, 32

  • Matthew 5, Study Bible
  • “Love and Justice in Ancient Israel,” The Watchtower, February 2019

Other outline sources: The Watchtower, August 15, 1993, pp. 4-5; October 1, 1990, p. 13 ¶15.

Source thought: The certificate provided a formal process and protection; the arrangement was not approval of selfish, casual divorce. Jehovah sees the tears and harm disloyalty creates.

Principle: A selfish decision can expose oneself and others to serious sin and lasting harm.

Application: Do not give up quickly on a difficult marriage. Seek help, communicate, forgive, make needed changes, imitate loyal love, and consider the family’s spiritual growth.

Oaths — Matthew 5:33-37

  • Matthew 5, Study Bible
  • The Watchtower, October 15, 2012
  • Insight on the Scriptures—“Oath”

Other outline source: The Watchtower, October 1, 1990, p. 14 ¶16.

Principle: Jehovah is the God of truth. Impossible promises may be immodest; promises made without intent to keep them are dishonest.

Application: Think realistically before committing. Let ordinary “Yes” and “No” be dependable in the home, congregation, work, and financial matters.
