“Eye for Eye” and Video 2

Matthew 5:38-42

  • Matthew 5, Study Bible
  • “Eye for an Eye”—What Does It Mean?

Outline publications: Awake!, September 2010, p. 11 ¶2; Bible Questions Answered, article 149.

Source thought: “Eye for eye” was a judicial standard for measured, proportionate justice, not permission for personal revenge.

Principle: Jehovah values true justice and is the final Judge. Wounded pride must not turn a desire for justice into retaliation.

1 Peter 2:23

1 Peter 2, Study Bible

Use: Jesus did not threaten under suffering; he entrusted himself to the One who judges righteously.

Boundary: Refusing revenge does not forbid seeking proper protection or using an appropriate arrangement to address serious wrong. Focus on motive and spirit.

Video 2

The brother suffers workplace unfairness and loses an opportunity. He first interprets non-retaliation as giving only the minimum required cooperation. Meditation and prayer help him discern love and the “second mile.” He gives the selected colleague his notes and offers help.

Exit line: “He did not merely suppress revenge; he replaced it with goodness.”
