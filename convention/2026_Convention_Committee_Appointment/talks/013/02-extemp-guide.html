Extemporaneous Guide — Talk No. 13

Can You Discern the Principle Behind the Law? — 24 minutes

One sentence: Mature obedience looks beneath a command to Jehovah’s timeless thinking, then chooses the course that imitates the loving Person behind it.

Main memory path: HAND → HEART → PEACE → PURITY → LOYALTY → TRUTH → JUSTICE → LOVE → PERSON

Delivery rule: None of the cited scriptures is designated “Read” in the outline. Refer to and explain them briefly; do not create extra formal readings.

LAW AND PRINCIPLE — 3:00

HAND — What remains after the rule?

  • Question: law tells what; what does principle do?
  • Set Video 1: notice what remains when particular rule no longer applies.

VIDEO 1 — 08-CO-v26_E_131
Outline 2:04; actual file 2:05.

  • Law = specific requirement.
  • Principle = timeless truth / Jehovah’s thinking.
  • Child: avoid displeasing → understand and share parent’s values.
  • Psalm 119:104: understanding helps reject wrong course.
  • Landing: Knowing and loving Person makes heartfelt obedience easier.

“YOU HEARD . . . HOWEVER, I SAY” — 18:00

HEART — Set the six-example lens

  • Matthew 5:17-48: Jesus reveals truths behind Mosaic commands.
  • Colossians 2:13, 14: Law covenant removed.
  • Malachi 3:6: principles remain because Jehovah does not change.
  • Repeated question: What does this teach about Jehovah, and how should it shape me?

1. PEACE — Murder

  • Matthew 5:21-26: beneath murder → anger / contempt.
  • Jehovah = God of peace; accountable for hatred where murder begins.
  • Application: make peace quickly; initiate / private / calm / listen / apologise.
  • Key question: Not “Am I technically guilty?” but “How can I restore peace?”

2. PURITY — Adultery

  • Matthew 5:27-30: passionate disloyal look cultivates heart adultery.
  • Jehovah = holy; marriage = loyal love.
  • Decisive measures: reject pornography and temptation source.
  • Positive side: contentment and affection for own mate.

3. LOYALTY — Divorce

  • Matthew 5:31, 32: formal protection could be selfishly misused.
  • Principle: selfish choice can endanger self/others spiritually.
  • Application: don’t give up quickly; seek help / communicate / forgive / make changes.
  • Honour Creator of marriage; help family grow spiritually.

4. TRUTH — Oaths

  • Matthew 5:33-37: impressive promise can hide unreliability.
  • Impossible promise = immodest; false promise = dishonest.
  • Jehovah = God of truth.
  • Think before commitment; let simple Yes carry weight everywhere.

5. JUSTICE — Eye for eye

  • Matthew 5:38-42: judicial proportional justice, not personal revenge.
  • Jehovah = final Judge; wounded pride is not.
  • 1 Peter 2:23: Jesus entrusted himself to righteous Judge.
  • Set Video 2: minimum requirement → what love would do.

VIDEO 2 — 10-CO-v26_E_132
Outline 6:29; actual file 6:21.

  • Unfair treatment / lost opportunity.
  • First thought: minimum cooperation, no overt revenge.
  • Meditates: love / second mile.
  • Prays / gives notes / offers help / entrusts outcome.
  • Key line: “He did not merely suppress revenge; he replaced it with goodness.”

6. LOVE — Enemies

  • Matthew 5:43-48: “love neighbour” Scriptural; “hate enemy” was not.
  • Father gives sun/rain even to opposers.
  • 1 John 4:8, 19: Jehovah is love; we love because he loved first.
  • No partiality / respectful / kind; do not let hostility dictate character.
  • Romans 13:8-10: love fulfils Law.
  • Matthew 28:19: Christian commands reflect Jehovah’s love.

LOVE THE PERSON BEHIND THE PRINCIPLE — 3:00

PERSON — Carry Jehovah’s thinking with you

  • Matthew 11:27: Jesus knows and reveals Father.
  • Six examples reveal personality, not merely harder rules.
  • Galatians 6:2: law of Christ also reveals loving Lawgiver.
  • Key line: “A principle shows the reason; the reason opens a window into Jehovah’s thinking.”
  • Return to road-crossing image:
    • grown child no longer needs hand rule;
    • respect for life and guidance remains.
  • Maturity ≠ independence from Jehovah.
  • Maturity = carry his thinking where no detailed rule gives every step.
  • Pray; Proverbs 2:4, 9; ask:
    1. What does this reveal about Jehovah?
    2. Which choice would imitate him?
  • Final line: “Discern the principle, imitate the Person behind it, and enjoy the happiness of thinking like Jehovah.”
