Part 7: Jehovah Gives His Servants Hope

These are audience-style answer options for the discussion and video question.

Discussion Point

How does hope help Jehovah’s servants?

Answer option 1: Hope helps us because it gives pain an end point. The Jews in Babylon were still captives, but Jehovah’s promise meant their misery would not last forever. That can help us too. Even when our situation has not changed yet, hope tells us that Jehovah has not forgotten us and that better conditions are ahead.

Answer option 2: Hope also stabilizes us. Hebrews 6:19 compares hope to an anchor, and that is a good picture. An anchor does not remove the storm, but it keeps the ship from drifting. In the same way, our hope does not remove every problem now, but it keeps our heart from being carried away by fear or discouragement.

Video Question

What lessons did you learn from the video?

Answer option 1: One lesson is that hope is not just for the future; it helps us now. When we picture Jehovah’s promised peace as real, it can strengthen us emotionally and help us endure grief, loneliness, or discouragement. The video reminded me that Jehovah’s promises are meant to comfort us while we are waiting, not only after they are fulfilled.

Answer option 2: I also learned that we can help others keep their hope strong. Sometimes a scripture, a visit, or reading with someone can remind them that Jehovah’s future is real. The elderly sister in the video received emotional support, and that shows me that hope is something we can share. We can help one another keep looking forward.

Answer option 3: The video helped me see that universal peace is certain because Jehovah has promised it. Human peace efforts often fail, but Jehovah’s Kingdom will succeed. That gives me confidence in the ministry. When we talk about peace, we are not sharing a wish. We are sharing a guarantee from the God who cannot lie.
