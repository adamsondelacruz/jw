**Life and Ministry Meeting | Jeremiah 16-17**

---

## Opening Comments (~1 min)

Brothers and sisters, this week's meeting has a very clear thread: *It matters whom we trust.* Jeremiah 17 contrasts two lives. The person who trusts in humans becomes like a lonely shrub in dry country, but the one who trusts in Jehovah is like a tree planted near waters, still fruitful even when heat comes.

Tonight we will see that trust from several angles: in our Bible reading, in our ministry, and especially in the part for young ones on trusting God's Word for practical advice. So as the meeting progresses, maybe keep this question in mind: *When pressure comes, where do I instinctively look first for guidance?* Let us be helped tonight to make Jehovah and his Word our first place of trust.

## Closing Comments (~1 min)

Tonight we were reminded that trust is not just a feeling. It shows in whose counsel we follow, how we respond to direction, how we handle people in the ministry, and how our young ones face pressure at school.

Jeremiah 17 gave us that strong picture of the tree planted near water. The heat still comes, but the tree does not stop producing fruit. That is what Jehovah can help us become if we keep trusting him, his Word, and the loving direction he provides. So this week, when we need advice or reassurance, let us go first to Jehovah. Read, pray, research, and let his thinking guide ours.
