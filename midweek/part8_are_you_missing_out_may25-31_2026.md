# Part 8 — "Are You Missing Out?"
**Living as Christians | 15 minutes (conductor) | Week of May 25–31, 2026 (Isaiah 65-66)**
**Features video: *What Will You Do "for the Sake of the Good News"?* (~9:54)**

> **Part scriptures (use these first):** Psalm 84:11, 12 (setup) · Malachi 3:10 (conclusion)
> **Official audience question (the only one):** *"What blessings have these young ones experienced as a result of making themselves available?"*
> Timing: Setup ~3 min → Video ~10 min → Discussion + Conclusion ~2 min.

---

## Introduction / Setup (~3 min)

<span style="color:grey">*(engaging, to the audience)*</span> How many of you have seen a SpaceX or a Rocket Lab launch? <span style="color:grey">*(pause)*</span> Those clips are everywhere now — that moment the engines ignite and this enormous machine claws its way up off the pad. <span style="color:grey">*(conversational)*</span> Here's something most people don't realize: in just the first few minutes after liftoff, a rocket burns almost *half* of its entire fuel supply.

<span style="color:grey">*(slowing)*</span> Now imagine a rocket that decided to play it safe — to hold back its fuel, save some for later, not give everything at launch. <span style="color:grey">*(humor)*</span> That rocket isn't being careful. <span style="color:grey">*(beat)*</span> That rocket is a very expensive lawn ornament. <span style="color:grey">*(pause)*</span> It never leaves the ground. The only way it reaches orbit — the only way it does what it was built for — is by giving everything at the start.

<span style="color:grey">*(warm, transition)*</span> Hold that picture, because tonight's question is a fair one: **"Are you missing out?"**

<span style="color:grey">*(conversational)*</span> The world insists you are. It says, *"If you give your best years to the full-time ministry — if you pioneer, if you go where the need is greater — you'll miss out. Miss out on the career, the money, the experiences."* Maybe you've even wondered, *'Why make sacrifices to do more, if all of Jehovah's worshippers will receive everlasting life anyway?'*

<span style="color:grey">*(read with conviction)*</span> But notice what Jehovah promises at **Psalm 84:11, 12**:

> *"For Jehovah God is a sun and a shield; Jehovah gives favor and glory. He does not **hold back anything good** from those walking in integrity. O Jehovah of armies, happy is the man who trusts in you."*

<span style="color:grey">*(reflective)*</span> Did you catch that? Jehovah *"does not hold back anything good."* <span style="color:grey">*(pause)*</span> So here is the truth that turns the world's thinking upside down: you don't miss out by giving Jehovah your best — <span style="color:grey">*(slow)*</span> you only miss out when you **hold back**. Just like that rocket.

<span style="color:grey">*(set up the video)*</span> The young ones in tonight's video made themselves available — they gave Jehovah their best. As we watch, look for the answer to one question: *What blessings have these young ones experienced as a result of making themselves available?* Let's watch.

## Conclusion (~1 min)

<span style="color:grey">*(sincere)*</span> Maybe you're thinking, *'But I don't have the circumstances to pioneer or serve full-time right now.'* <span style="color:grey">*(warm)*</span> That's all right — the principle is the same for every one of us: Jehovah blesses those who give him their best, whatever that best looks like for you today.

<span style="color:grey">*(read)*</span> Listen to his promise at **Malachi 3:10**:

> *"...Test me out, please, in this regard... whether I will not open to you the floodgates of the heavens and **pour out on you a blessing until there is nothing lacking**."*

<span style="color:grey">*(building)*</span> That is the one place in the Bible where Jehovah actually invites us to *test* him. <span style="color:grey">*(pause)*</span> Give him your best — don't hold back — and watch the floodgates open.

<span style="color:grey">*(conclude, callback)*</span> So, are you missing out? <span style="color:grey">*(slow)*</span> Only if you hold back. <span style="color:grey">*(warm)*</span> Like the rocket — give Jehovah everything at the launch, and there is no limit to where he will take you.
