Part 8 — "Are You Missing Out?"

Living as Christians | 15 minutes (conductor) | Week of May 25–31, 2026 (Isaiah 65-66)
Features video: What Will You Do "for the Sake of the Good News"? (~9:54)

Part scriptures (use these first): Psalm 84:11, 12 (setup) · Malachi 3:10 (conclusion)
Official audience question (the only one): "What blessings have these young ones experienced as a result of making themselves available?"
Timing: Setup ~3 min → Video ~10 min → Discussion + Conclusion ~2 min.

Introduction / Setup (~3 min)

(engaging, to the audience) How many of you have seen a SpaceX or a Rocket Lab launch? (pause) Those clips are everywhere now — that moment the engines ignite and this enormous machine claws its way up off the pad. (conversational) Here's something most people don't realize: in just the first few minutes after liftoff, a rocket burns almost half of its entire fuel supply.

(slowing) Now imagine a rocket that decided to play it safe — to hold back its fuel, save some for later, not give everything at launch. (humor) That rocket isn't being careful. (beat) That rocket is a very expensive lawn ornament. (pause) It never leaves the ground. The only way it reaches orbit — the only way it does what it was built for — is by giving everything at the start.

(warm, transition) Hold that picture, because tonight's question is a fair one: "Are you missing out?"

(conversational) The world insists you are. It says, "If you give your best years to the full-time ministry — if you pioneer, if you go where the need is greater — you'll miss out. Miss out on the career, the money, the experiences." Maybe you've even wondered, 'Why make sacrifices to do more, if all of Jehovah's worshippers will receive everlasting life anyway?'

(read with conviction) But notice what Jehovah promises at Psalm 84:11, 12:

"For Jehovah God is a sun and a shield; Jehovah gives favor and glory. He does not hold back anything good from those walking in integrity. O Jehovah of armies, happy is the man who trusts in you."

(reflective) Did you catch that? Jehovah "does not hold back anything good." (pause) So here is the truth that turns the world's thinking upside down: you don't miss out by giving Jehovah your best — (slow) you only miss out when you hold back. Just like that rocket.

(set up the video) The young ones in tonight's video made themselves available — they gave Jehovah their best. As we watch, look for the answer to one question: What blessings have these young ones experienced as a result of making themselves available? Let's watch.

▶ PLAY VIDEO — What Will You Do "for the Sake of the Good News"? (~10 min)

Discussion (~1 min)

(warm) Welcome back.

Question (the part's question): What blessings have these young ones experienced as a result of making themselves available?
(take several comments)

OPTIONAL follow-up — only if discussion is lively and time allows; drop without hesitation if tight:
Which of those blessings could a young person start enjoying even now, before full-time service?

Conclusion (~1 min)

(sincere) Maybe you're thinking, 'But I don't have the circumstances to pioneer or serve full-time right now.' (warm) That's all right — the principle is the same for every one of us: Jehovah blesses those who give him their best, whatever that best looks like for you today.

(read) Listen to his promise at Malachi 3:10:

"...Test me out, please, in this regard... whether I will not open to you the floodgates of the heavens and pour out on you a blessing until there is nothing lacking."

(building) That is the one place in the Bible where Jehovah actually invites us to test him. (pause) Give him your best — don't hold back — and watch the floodgates open.

(conclude, callback) So, are you missing out? (slow) Only if you hold back. (warm) Like the rocket — give Jehovah everything at the launch, and there is no limit to where he will take you.
