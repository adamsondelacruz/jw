## Introduction (~1:15)

<span style="color:grey">*(warm, conversational)*</span> Brothers and sisters, we live in a time when more information reaches us in a single day than reached our grandparents in a whole year. <span style="color:grey">*(pause)*</span> But here is the difficult question — how much of it is actually *true*?

<span style="color:grey">*(thoughtful)*</span> Most of us have experienced it. We see a story online, we share it, and later we find out it wasn't true at all. Or we hear something repeated so many times that we simply start to believe it.

<span style="color:grey">*(serious)*</span> Misinformation isn't new. Jehovah saw it in his own people thousands of years ago. Notice what the Israelites told their prophets at **Isaiah 30:10:**

> *"Tell us **flattering** things; envision **deceptive illusions**."*

<span style="color:grey">*(pause)*</span> They didn't want the truth. They wanted what felt good. And **Jeremiah 5:31** — right in this week's Bible reading — adds this heartbreaking detail:

> *"The prophets prophesy lies... and my own people **love it that way**."*

<span style="color:grey">*(reflective)*</span> Misinformation succeeds not because it overpowers us — it succeeds because it *appeals* to us. To our fears. To our preferences. To our pride. <span style="color:grey">*(transition)*</span> The video we're about to watch will help us see how to protect our hearts. Let's watch.

## Closing reflection (~25 sec)

<span style="color:grey">*(slow, sincere)*</span> Then **ask yourself** — and this one stings a little: <span style="color:grey">*(pause)*</span> *"Do I tend to trust only information that confirms what I want to believe? Or do I keep my biases in check?"*

<span style="color:grey">*(conclude, warm)*</span> Remember — our feeds and social media are tailored to *our* likes, so we often need to dig deeper. <span style="color:grey">*(slow)*</span> Jeremiah's people didn't fall because they couldn't see lies. They fell because they *loved* the lies. <span style="color:grey">*(pause)*</span> Test what you hear — and protect your heart.
