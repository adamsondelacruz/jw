Opening Comments (~1 min)

Brothers and sisters, this week’s Bible reading in Jeremiah 22 and 23 helps us think about shepherding. Jehovah strongly corrected shepherds who neglected his people, but he also promised that his people would be cared for by good shepherds.

That is a comforting thought. Jehovah sees when people are hurt by bad leadership, and he also provides loving care through his organization. Tonight we will see how good shepherds protect unity, strengthen faith, and help Jehovah’s people feel safe. So as we listen, maybe we can ask ourselves: How can I show appreciation for Jehovah’s shepherding, and how can I help others feel cared for?

Closing Comments (~1 min)

Tonight we saw Jehovah as a caring Shepherd. He does not ignore neglect or false teaching, and he does not leave his people without help. He gives direction, good examples, and loving shepherds who work hard to keep the congregation united.

The lesson is not only for appointed brothers. All of us can help make the congregation feel safe and cared for. We can speak kindly in the ministry, show empathy when following up, and strengthen others by our words and example. This week, let us keep thanking Jehovah for the way he shepherds us and look for practical ways to care for one another.
