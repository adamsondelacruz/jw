Show Insight and You Will Find Success

Show Insight and You “Will Find Success”

The Watchtower - Study Edition | May 25-31, 2026
“The one who shows insight in a matter will find success.” - Proverbs 16:20

Source article: https://www.jw.org/en/library/magazines/watchtower-study-march-2026/Show-Insight-and-You-Will-Find-Success/

Study Questions

1-2. What is insight, and how can it help us?

Direct answer - English: Insight is the ability to see into a situation and look beyond what is obvious. It helps us understand why something happened or why someone acted a certain way. Because of that, insight helps us act wisely and sensibly, such as controlling our lips, knowing when to keep silent, keeping anger in check, overlooking transgression, and accepting advice or correction.

Tagalog: Ang insight ay kakayahang makita ang mas malalim sa isang sitwasyon, hindi lang yung obvious. Tinutulungan tayo nito na maintindihan kung bakit nangyari ang isang bagay o bakit ganoon kumilos ang isang tao. Dahil dito, mas nagiging marunong tayo sa pagsasalita, sa pananahimik, sa pagkontrol ng galit, sa pagpapalampas ng pagkakamali, at sa pagtanggap ng payo.

Deep spiritual answer - English: This paragraph teaches me that Jehovah does not want me to react only by emotion. Through Proverbs 10:19, Psalm 4:4, and Proverbs 19:20, he trains me to pause, think, and choose words that will please him. Insight protects my relationship with Jehovah because it helps me respond in a way that benefits myself and others instead of making a tense situation worse.

Tagalog: Itinuturo nito sa akin na ayaw ni Jehovah na emosyon lang ang magdikta ng reaction ko. Sa Proverbs 10:19, Psalm 4:4, at Proverbs 19:20, tinuturuan niya akong huminto muna, mag-isip, at pumili ng salita na magpapasaya sa kaniya. Pinoprotektahan ng insight ang kaugnayan ko kay Jehovah dahil tinutulungan ako nitong maging maingat at mapayapa.

3. Who was Naaman?

Direct answer - English: Naaman was a prominent man in Syria, the nation north of Israel. He was the chief of the Syrian army, so he had high position and influence. But despite his status, he had a serious problem: he suffered from leprosy. His situation shows that even a respected and powerful person can face a painful weakness that he cannot solve on his own.

Tagalog: Si Naaman ay kilalang tao sa Syria, isang bansa sa hilaga ng Israel. Siya ang pinuno ng hukbo ng Syria, kaya mataas ang posisyon at respeto sa kaniya. Pero kahit ganoon, mayroon siyang mabigat na problema: may ketong siya. Ipinapakita nito na kahit makapangyarihan o respetadong tao, puwedeng magkaroon ng problemang hindi niya kayang solusyunan mag-isa.

Deep spiritual answer - English: The paragraph connects Naaman’s example with 1 Peter 5:5, which says we need humility, not pride. This teaches me that Jehovah values humility more than position or reputation. Naaman had rank, but his rank could not cure him. In daily life, I should not let experience, age, assignment, or ability make me too proud to accept help from Jehovah.

Tagalog: Inuugnay ng paragraph ang halimbawa ni Naaman sa 1 Peter 5:5, na nagpapakita na kailangan natin ang kapakumbabaan, hindi pride. Itinuturo nito na mas pinapahalagahan ni Jehovah ang humility kaysa posisyon o reputasyon. Mataas ang ranggo ni Naaman, pero hindi siya napagaling ng ranggo niya. Kaya hindi ko dapat hayaang maging hadlang ang experience, edad, assignment, o kakayahan para tumanggap ng tulong mula kay Jehovah.

4. How did Naaman show a measure of perception?

Direct answer - English: Naaman showed perception by not dismissing the words of the Israelite servant girl. He could have proudly ignored her because she was a lowly servant from an enemy nation. Instead, he took her words seriously, got permission from the king of Syria, and traveled to Israel to seek help from Jehovah’s prophet.

Tagalog: Nagpakita si Naaman ng perception dahil hindi niya binalewala ang sinabi ng batang aliping Israelita. Puwede niya sanang isipin na mababa lang ito at mula pa sa kaaway na bansa. Pero pinakinggan niya ang sinabi nito, humingi siya ng pahintulot sa hari ng Syria, at pumunta sa Israel para humingi ng tulong sa propeta ni Jehovah.

Deep spiritual answer - English: This teaches me that Jehovah can use unexpected people to give helpful direction. The servant girl mentioned in 2 Kings 5:2, 3 had no high status, but her words opened the way for Naaman to receive help. In my life, I should listen humbly even when counsel or a good reminder comes from someone younger, quieter, or less prominent.

Tagalog: Itinuturo nito sa akin na puwedeng gamitin ni Jehovah ang mga taong hindi natin inaasahan para magbigay ng kapaki-pakinabang na paalala. Ang batang aliping binanggit sa 2 Kings 5:2, 3 ay walang mataas na posisyon, pero naging daan ang sinabi niya para matulungan si Naaman. Kaya dapat akong makinig nang may humility kahit ang payo ay galing sa mas bata, tahimik, o hindi kilala.

5. What happened to Naaman when he got to Israel?

Direct answer - English: When Naaman arrived in Israel, King Jehoram misunderstood the situation and thought Syria’s king was trying to start trouble with him. Then Elisha told the king to send Naaman to him. But even there, things did not happen as Naaman expected. Elisha did not personally come out; he sent a messenger with instructions for Naaman to follow.

Tagalog: Pagdating ni Naaman sa Israel, mali ang naging reaction ni Haring Jehoram. Akala niya gusto lang ng hari ng Syria na makipag-away. Pagkatapos, sinabi ni Eliseo na ipadala sa kaniya si Naaman. Pero hindi pa rin nangyari ang inaasahan ni Naaman. Hindi personal na lumabas si Eliseo; nagpadala lang siya ng mensahero na may mga tagubilin.

Deep spiritual answer - English: This paragraph reminds me that Jehovah’s help may not arrive in the form I expect. Naaman likely expected honor and ceremony, but the answer came through simple direction from Elisha’s messenger. If I ask Jehovah for help, I should be careful not to reject the answer just because it comes in a humble or ordinary way.

Tagalog: Ipinaaalala nito sa akin na ang tulong ni Jehovah ay maaaring dumating sa paraan na hindi ko inaasahan. Posibleng inasahan ni Naaman na tatanggapin siya nang may malaking pagpaparangal, pero dumating ang sagot sa simpleng tagubilin mula sa mensahero ni Eliseo. Kaya kapag humihingi ako ng tulong kay Jehovah, hindi ko dapat tanggihan ang sagot dahil lang simple o ordinaryo ang paraan.

6a. What may have caused Naaman to react with pride to the messenger’s instructions?

Direct answer - English: Naaman may have reacted with pride because he felt he had not been treated with the respect he deserved as a high-ranking army officer. He may also have felt that Elisha’s instructions did not show respect for Syria. Because of those feelings, he became indignant, went away in rage, and was ready to abandon the chance to be healed.

Tagalog: Posibleng nag-react si Naaman nang may pride dahil pakiramdam niya hindi siya binigyan ng respeto bilang mataas na opisyal ng hukbo. Baka naisip din niya na walang respeto sa Syria ang tagubilin ni Eliseo. Dahil dito, nagalit siya, umalis nang galit na galit, at muntik nang isuko ang pagkakataong gumaling.

Deep spiritual answer - English: This teaches me that pride can make a person focus more on how he was treated than on the help Jehovah is providing. In 2 Kings 5:11, 12, Naaman’s anger almost cost him his cure. I can apply this by asking, “Am I offended because the direction is wrong, or because it did not come the way I preferred?”

Tagalog: Itinuturo nito sa akin na dahil sa pride, puwedeng mas mag-focus ang tao sa kung paano siya trinato kaysa sa tulong na ibinibigay ni Jehovah. Sa 2 Kings 5:11, 12, muntik nang mawala kay Naaman ang pagkakataong gumaling dahil sa galit. Kaya puwede kong tanungin ang sarili ko, “Mali ba talaga ang direction, o nasaktan lang ako dahil hindi ito dumating sa paraang gusto ko?”

6b. How did Naaman’s servants show insight, and with what result? (2 Kings 5:13, 14)

Direct answer - English: Naaman’s servants showed insight by helping him look past his anger and reconsider Elisha’s instructions. They reasoned with him respectfully instead of adding to the tension. Naaman listened, set aside his pride, and obeyed. According to 2 Kings 5:13, 14, he dipped himself in the Jordan as instructed and was completely healed.

Tagalog: Nagpakita ng insight ang mga lingkod ni Naaman dahil tinulungan nila siyang makita ang sitwasyon nang lampas sa galit niya. Kinausap nila siya nang may respeto, hindi nila pinalala ang tensiyon. Nakinig si Naaman, ibinaba ang pride, at sumunod. Ayon sa 2 Kings 5:13, 14, lumubog siya sa Jordan gaya ng tagubilin at lubusan siyang gumaling.

Deep spiritual answer - English: This shows that Jehovah can bless humble second thoughts. Naaman’s first reaction was wrong, but when he accepted help and obeyed, the result was success. That encourages me because if I initially react badly to counsel, I can still recover by listening, praying, and obeying. Jehovah values humility more than a perfect first reaction.

Tagalog: Ipinapakita nito na puwedeng pagpalain ni Jehovah ang mapagpakumbabang pagbabago ng isip. Mali ang unang reaction ni Naaman, pero nang tumanggap siya ng tulong at sumunod, naging matagumpay ang resulta. Nakakapagpatibay ito dahil kung mali man ang una kong reaction sa payo, puwede pa rin akong bumawi sa pamamagitan ng pakikinig, pananalangin, at pagsunod.

7. What can we learn from Naaman’s experience? (Proverbs 22:4) (See also pictures.)

Direct answer - English: We learn that insight helps us look beyond outward appearances and not allow emotions to control us. It moves us to be humble and admit that we do not have all the answers. Like Naaman, we may need help from others, especially from Jehovah. Proverbs 22:4 shows that humility and fear of Jehovah lead to rich blessings.

Tagalog: Natutuhan natin na ang insight ay tumutulong sa atin na tumingin lampas sa panlabas na anyo at huwag magpakontrol sa emosyon. Tinutulungan tayo nitong maging mapagpakumbaba at amining hindi natin alam ang lahat. Gaya ni Naaman, kailangan natin ang tulong ng iba, lalo na ni Jehovah. Ipinapakita ng Proverbs 22:4 na ang humility at pagkatakot kay Jehovah ay nagdadala ng maraming pagpapala.

Deep spiritual answer - English: This paragraph teaches me that Jehovah is patient with imperfect people who are willing to become humble. Naaman was not yet a worshipper of Jehovah, yet Jehovah allowed him to benefit when he listened to the right direction. In my daily life, before reacting to counsel or theocratic direction, I should pause and ask whether my response shows humility or wounded pride.

Tagalog: Itinuturo nito sa akin na matiisin si Jehovah sa mga imperfect na taong handang magpakumbaba. Hindi pa man mananamba ni Jehovah si Naaman, nakinabang siya nang makinig siya sa tamang tagubilin. Sa daily life, bago ako mag-react sa payo o theocratic direction, dapat huminto muna ako at tanungin kung humility ba ang ipinapakita ko o nasaktang pride.

8. In what circumstances may we find it difficult to remain calm?

Direct answer - English: We may find it difficult to remain calm when others say or do things that are unkind or unjust. In those moments, we may feel that our anger is understandable. Ephesians 4:26 shows that a person may feel wrath, but the paragraph reminds us that insight can help us avoid responding to frustrating situations in an angry way.

Tagalog: Mahirap manatiling kalmado kapag may nagsalita o gumawa ng hindi mabait o hindi makatarungan. Sa ganoong pagkakataon, baka maramdaman nating natural lang na magalit. Ipinapakita ng Ephesians 4:26 na maaaring makadama ng galit ang isang tao, pero ipinapaalala ng paragraph na ang insight ay tutulong para hindi tayo mag-react nang may galit.

Deep spiritual answer - English: This teaches me that Jehovah understands hurt feelings, but he also wants me to handle them with self-control. Ephesians 4:26 helps me see that feeling upset is not the same as giving anger control. In daily life, when I feel provoked, I can ask Jehovah to help me slow down before I speak, text, or make a decision.

Tagalog: Itinuturo nito sa akin na naiintindihan ni Jehovah ang nasasaktang damdamin, pero gusto rin niyang kontrolado natin ang sarili. Tinutulungan ako ng Ephesians 4:26 na makita na iba ang makadama ng galit at iba ang magpakontrol sa galit. Kapag naiinis ako, puwede akong humingi kay Jehovah ng tulong para huminto muna bago magsalita, mag-text, o magdesisyon.

9. How was David treated by Nabal?

Direct answer - English: David and his men had been kind to Nabal’s shepherds and had protected his flocks while living in the wilderness. During sheepshearing season, David sent messengers to greet Nabal respectfully and ask for any provisions he could spare. But Nabal showed no gratitude. Instead, he answered rudely and insulted David and his men.

Tagalog: Naging mabait si David at ang mga tauhan niya sa mga pastol ni Nabal at pinrotektahan nila ang mga kawan nito habang nasa ilang. Noong panahon ng paggugupit ng tupa, nagpadala si David ng mga mensahero para magbigay-galang at humingi ng anumang maibibigay na pagkain. Pero hindi nagpakita ng pasasalamat si Nabal. Sa halip, sumagot siya nang bastos at ininsulto si David at ang mga tauhan nito.

Deep spiritual answer - English: This paragraph teaches me that even when I have done good, I may still be treated unfairly. David had shown kindness, but Nabal responded with disrespect. The account in 1 Samuel 25 reminds me not to base my conduct on the other person’s gratitude. I should keep doing what is right and let Jehovah’s thinking guide my next step.

Tagalog: Itinuturo nito sa akin na kahit gumawa ako ng mabuti, puwede pa rin akong tratuhin nang hindi patas. Naging mabait si David, pero walang respeto ang sagot ni Nabal. Ipinaaalala ng 1 Samuel 25 na hindi dapat nakadepende ang mabuting paggawi ko sa pasasalamat ng iba. Dapat kong patuloy na gawin ang tama at hayaan ang kaisipan ni Jehovah ang gumabay sa susunod kong hakbang.

10. How did both David and Abigail show insight? (1 Samuel 25:32, 33) (See also picture.)

Direct answer - English: Abigail showed insight by recognizing that David was a good man even though he was reacting extremely. She acted quickly, brought a generous gift, and humbly gave wise advice. David showed insight by listening to Abigail and recognizing that her words reflected Jehovah’s view. As 1 Samuel 25:32, 33 shows, he calmed down and avoided bloodguilt.

Tagalog: Nagpakita ng insight si Abigail dahil nakita niya na mabuting tao si David kahit sobra ang reaction nito. Kumilos siya agad, nagdala ng maraming regalo, at nagbigay ng matalinong payo nang may humility. Nagpakita naman ng insight si David dahil nakinig siya kay Abigail at nakita niyang galing kay Jehovah ang paalala. Ayon sa 1 Samuel 25:32, 33, kumalma siya at naiwasan ang pagkakasala sa dugo.

Deep spiritual answer - English: This teaches me that Jehovah can use a humble, courageous person to stop someone from making a serious mistake. Abigail did not excuse David’s anger, but she appealed to the good in him. David’s response also teaches me humility: when correction reflects Jehovah’s thinking, I should accept it even if it interrupts what I strongly intended to do.

Tagalog: Itinuturo nito na puwedeng gamitin ni Jehovah ang isang mapagpakumbaba at matapang na tao para pigilan ang iba sa seryosong pagkakamali. Hindi kinunsinti ni Abigail ang galit ni David, pero kinausap niya ang mabuting puso nito. Ang reaction ni David ay nagtuturo rin ng humility: kapag ang correction ay kaayon ng kaisipan ni Jehovah, dapat ko itong tanggapin kahit salungat ito sa gusto kong gawin.

11. How can showing insight help us when we are provoked? (Proverbs 19:11)

Direct answer - English: Showing insight helps us respond peacefully even when we have a legitimate reason to be angry. Proverbs 19:11 shows that insight can slow down anger and help a person overlook an offense. The paragraph says we should consider the consequences of our words and actions, reject thoughtless responses, pray to Jehovah, and give ourselves time to seek his thinking.

Tagalog: Ang insight ay tumutulong sa atin na manatiling mapayapa kahit may dahilan tayong magalit. Ipinapakita ng Proverbs 19:11 na ang insight ay nakakapagpabagal ng galit at nakakatulong para palampasin ang pagkakamali. Sinasabi ng paragraph na dapat nating isipin ang magiging resulta ng salita at gawa natin, iwasan ang padalos-dalos na reaction, manalangin kay Jehovah, at bigyan ang sarili ng panahon para makita ang kaisipan niya.

Deep spiritual answer - English: This paragraph teaches me that Jehovah’s way is not just to win an argument but to prevent damage. James 1:19 reminds me to be slow about speaking and slow about wrath. In daily life, I can apply this by delaying a reply when I am upset. A pause can give prayer and insight enough room to protect my peace.

Tagalog: Itinuturo nito sa akin na ang paraan ni Jehovah ay hindi lang manalo sa argumento kundi maiwasan ang pinsala. Ipinaaalala ng James 1:19 na maging mabagal sa pagsasalita at mabagal sa pagkagalit. Sa daily life, puwede ko itong gawin sa pamamagitan ng pagpapaliban ng sagot kapag galit ako. Ang pause ay nagbibigay ng pagkakataon sa prayer at insight na protektahan ang kapayapaan ko.

12. How can others help us show insight and remain calm?

Direct answer - English: Others can help us show insight by helping us see beyond the immediate situation, just as Jehovah used Abigail to help David. Before reacting to something frustrating, we can speak with a mature Christian who can help us view matters more objectively. Proverbs 12:15 and 20:18 show the value of listening to advice and seeking counsel before acting.

Tagalog: Matutulungan tayo ng iba na magpakita ng insight kung tutulungan nila tayong makita ang sitwasyon nang mas malawak, gaya ng paggamit ni Jehovah kay Abigail para tulungan si David. Bago tayo mag-react sa nakakainis na sitwasyon, puwede tayong makipag-usap sa mature na Kristiyano na makakatulong makita ang bagay nang mas objective. Ipinapakita ng Proverbs 12:15 at 20:18 ang halaga ng pakikinig sa payo bago kumilos.

Deep spiritual answer - English: This teaches me that Jehovah often helps us through the spiritual maturity of others. Seeking counsel is not weakness; it is insight. If I am the one helping a friend, I can imitate Abigail by being respectful, calm, and focused on Jehovah’s perspective. My goal should not be to control my friend, but to help him regain spiritual balance.

Tagalog: Itinuturo nito sa akin na madalas ginagamit ni Jehovah ang spiritual maturity ng iba para tulungan tayo. Ang paghingi ng payo ay hindi kahinaan; ito ay insight. Kung ako naman ang tutulong sa kaibigan, puwede kong tularan si Abigail: magalang, kalmado, at nakapokus sa pananaw ni Jehovah. Ang goal ko ay hindi kontrolin siya, kundi tulungan siyang maibalik ang spiritual balance.

13. How can insight help us see beyond our fears?

Direct answer - English: Insight helps us see beyond fear by reminding us that frightening events in this world are small compared with Jehovah’s power. Psalm 27:1 helps us focus on Jehovah as our light and salvation. Even when a situation seems hopeless, Jehovah can help us. The paragraph introduces Jonah as someone who needed more insight to succeed in a difficult assignment.

Tagalog: Tinutulungan tayo ng insight na makita ang lampas sa takot sa pamamagitan ng pag-alala na maliit ang nakakatakot na pangyayari sa mundong ito kumpara sa kapangyarihan ni Jehovah. Tinutulungan tayo ng Psalm 27:1 na magpokus kay Jehovah bilang ating liwanag at kaligtasan. Kahit parang wala nang pag-asa ang sitwasyon, kaya tayong tulungan ni Jehovah. Ipinakilala ng paragraph si Jonas bilang taong kinailangang magkaroon ng higit pang insight para magtagumpay sa mahirap na atas.

Deep spiritual answer - English: This paragraph teaches me that Jehovah does not minimize my fears, but he helps me put them in the right scale. Psalm 27:1 turns attention from danger to Jehovah. In daily life, when fear feels bigger than my faith, I can deliberately ask, “How does this look next to Jehovah’s power?” That question helps fear lose its control.

Tagalog: Itinuturo nito sa akin na hindi binabalewala ni Jehovah ang takot ko, pero tinutulungan niya akong ilagay ito sa tamang sukat. Inililipat ng Psalm 27:1 ang focus mula sa panganib papunta kay Jehovah. Sa daily life, kapag parang mas malaki ang takot kaysa pananampalataya ko, puwede kong itanong, “Ano ang itsura ng problemang ito kung ikukumpara sa kapangyarihan ni Jehovah?” Nakakatulong iyon para humina ang kontrol ng takot.

14. Why may Jonah have been reluctant to accept the assignment Jehovah gave him?

Direct answer - English: Jonah may have been reluctant because the assignment was daunting. Jehovah told him to go to Nineveh and proclaim a judgment message. The trip from Israel to Nineveh would have taken about a month on foot, and the Assyrians were known as aggressive and violent people. Nineveh was even called “the city of bloodshed.” So Jonah ran away.

Tagalog: Maaaring nag-atubili si Jonas dahil nakakatakot ang atas. Sinabi ni Jehovah na pumunta siya sa Nineve at ihayag ang mensahe ng paghatol. Ang biyahe mula Israel papuntang Nineve ay aabutin ng mga isang buwan kung lalakarin, at kilala ang mga Asiryano bilang marahas at agresibo. Tinawag pa ang Nineve na “lunsod ng pagbububo ng dugo.” Kaya tumakas si Jonas.

Deep spiritual answer - English: This paragraph shows that fear can make even a spiritual person avoid an assignment from Jehovah. Jonah 1:1-3 does not present Jonah as faithless forever, but as someone overwhelmed by what he saw ahead. That helps me be honest about my own fears. If an assignment feels intimidating, I should not run from Jehovah; I should run to him.

Tagalog: Ipinapakita nito na ang takot ay puwedeng magtulak kahit sa isang spiritual na tao na umiwas sa atas ni Jehovah. Sa Jonah 1:1-3, hindi ipinapakita si Jonas na walang pananampalataya magpakailanman, kundi isang taong natakot sa nakikita niyang mangyayari. Tinutulungan ako nitong maging honest sa sarili kong takot. Kapag nakakatakot ang isang assignment, hindi ako dapat tumakbo palayo kay Jehovah; dapat akong lumapit sa kaniya.

15. How did Jonah develop greater confidence in Jehovah? (Jonah 2:6-9)

Direct answer - English: Jonah developed greater confidence when Jehovah reminded him of His power by miraculously saving his life. Jonah began to see beyond the fearful parts of the Nineveh assignment and focused on the fact that Jehovah could protect him. After that, when Jehovah gave him a second chance, Jonah accepted the assignment and went to Nineveh, where his ministry succeeded.

Tagalog: Nagkaroon si Jonas ng higit na tiwala nang ipaalala ni Jehovah ang Kaniyang kapangyarihan sa pamamagitan ng makahimalang pagliligtas sa buhay niya. Unti-unti niyang nakita ang lampas sa nakakatakot na bahagi ng assignment sa Nineve at nag-focus siya sa katotohanang kaya siyang protektahan ni Jehovah. Pagkatapos, nang bigyan siya ni Jehovah ng ikalawang pagkakataon, tinanggap niya ang atas at pumunta sa Nineve, kung saan naging matagumpay ang ministeryo niya.

Deep spiritual answer - English: Jonah 2:6-9 teaches me that Jehovah can rebuild confidence after failure. Jonah had run away, but Jehovah did not discard him; he saved him, taught him, and gave him another opportunity. That tells me Jehovah is not only powerful but merciful. When fear has made me hesitate, I can still pray, learn, and move forward with renewed trust.

Tagalog: Itinuturo ng Jonah 2:6-9 na kayang buuin muli ni Jehovah ang tiwala natin kahit matapos tayong magkamali. Tumakas si Jonas, pero hindi siya itinapon ni Jehovah; iniligtas siya, tinuruan, at binigyan muli ng pagkakataon. Ipinapakita nito na si Jehovah ay hindi lang makapangyarihan kundi maawain din. Kapag napaatras ako dahil sa takot, puwede pa rin akong manalangin, matuto, at sumulong na may panibagong tiwala.

16. How can insight help us in fearful situations? (Proverbs 29:25) (See also pictures.)

Direct answer - English: Insight helps us not let fear, especially fear of man, hold us back in our worship of Jehovah. Proverbs 29:25 shows that trembling at men is a snare, but trusting in Jehovah brings protection. Like Jonah, we can look past the challenge and focus on Jehovah’s support. We can also remember how Jehovah has helped us and others before.

Tagalog: Tinutulungan tayo ng insight na huwag hayaang pigilan tayo ng takot, lalo na ng fear of man, sa pagsamba kay Jehovah. Ipinapakita ng Proverbs 29:25 na ang panginginig sa tao ay isang bitag, pero ang pagtitiwala kay Jehovah ay nagbibigay ng proteksiyon. Gaya ni Jonas, puwede nating tingnan ang lampas sa hamon at mag-focus sa suporta ni Jehovah. Puwede rin nating alalahanin kung paano tayo at ang iba ay natulungan ni Jehovah noon.

Deep spiritual answer - English: This paragraph teaches me that Jehovah wants my trust to become stronger than my fear. Hebrews 13:6 helps me say with confidence that Jehovah is my helper. In daily life, that may mean speaking up at school or work, accepting a difficult assignment, or continuing in worship even when others disapprove. Insight makes Jehovah’s support more real than people’s pressure.

Tagalog: Itinuturo nito sa akin na gusto ni Jehovah na maging mas malakas ang tiwala ko kaysa sa takot ko. Tinutulungan ako ng Hebrews 13:6 na sabihin nang may confidence na si Jehovah ang helper ko. Sa daily life, maaaring ibig sabihin nito ay magsalita nang tama sa school o work, tanggapin ang mahirap na assignment, o magpatuloy sa pagsamba kahit hindi sang-ayon ang iba. Pinaparamdam ng insight na mas totoo ang suporta ni Jehovah kaysa pressure ng tao.

17. How can we gain more insight?

Direct answer - English: We gain more insight from Jehovah, the most important Source of insight. He gives it through his Word and holy spirit. Nehemiah 9:20 and Psalm 32:8 show that Jehovah instructs and guides his people. By carefully considering the Bible and prayerfully asking for holy spirit, we can understand matters more clearly and handle them in a way that reflects Jehovah’s thinking.

Tagalog: Nagkakaroon tayo ng higit pang insight mula kay Jehovah, ang pinakamahalagang Pinagmumulan ng insight. Ibinibigay niya ito sa pamamagitan ng kaniyang Salita at banal na espiritu. Ipinapakita ng Nehemiah 9:20 at Psalm 32:8 na tinuturuan at ginagabayan ni Jehovah ang kaniyang bayan. Sa maingat na pag-aaral ng Bibliya at pananalangin para sa banal na espiritu, mas malinaw nating mauunawaan ang mga bagay at mahaharap ito sa paraang nagpapakita ng kaisipan ni Jehovah.

Deep spiritual answer - English: This paragraph teaches me that insight is not just personality or natural intelligence; it is something Jehovah can give and train. Psalm 119:97-101 shows the value of loving and meditating on Jehovah’s reminders. So if I want better judgment, I need more than quick opinions. I need Bible thinking, prayer, and holy spirit before I speak or act.

Tagalog: Itinuturo nito sa akin na ang insight ay hindi lang personality o natural na talino; ito ay bagay na maibibigay at maituturo ni Jehovah. Ipinapakita ng Psalm 119:97-101 ang halaga ng pagmamahal at pagbubulay-bulay sa mga paalala ni Jehovah. Kaya kung gusto ko ng mas mabuting judgment, hindi sapat ang mabilis na opinyon. Kailangan ko ang kaisipan ng Bibliya, panalangin, at banal na espiritu bago magsalita o kumilos.

18. What are you determined to do?

Direct answer - English: I am determined to keep seeking and valuing the insight that comes from Jehovah. Psalm 14:2 shows that Jehovah looks for those who seek him, and Proverbs 21:16 warns against straying from the way of insight. So I want to keep showing insight at all times and in every situation, trusting that this will help me find success.

Tagalog: Determinado akong patuloy na hanapin at pahalagahan ang insight na galing kay Jehovah. Ipinapakita ng Psalm 14:2 na tinitingnan ni Jehovah ang mga humahanap sa kaniya, at nagbababala ang Proverbs 21:16 laban sa paglayo sa daan ng insight. Kaya gusto kong patuloy na magpakita ng insight sa lahat ng panahon at sa bawat sitwasyon, na nagtitiwalang tutulong ito para magtagumpay ako.

Deep spiritual answer - English: This final paragraph helps me see insight as something to safeguard, not just something to admire. Jehovah gives insight because he wants me to stay close to him and avoid damaging choices. My determination should be practical: pause before reacting, pray before deciding, listen before rejecting counsel, and keep asking, “What thinking would please Jehovah here?”

Tagalog: Tinutulungan ako ng huling paragraph na makita ang insight bilang bagay na dapat bantayan, hindi lang hangaan. Ibinibigay ni Jehovah ang insight dahil gusto niyang manatili akong malapit sa kaniya at maiwasan ang maling desisyon. Dapat practical ang determinasyon ko: huminto bago mag-react, manalangin bago magdesisyon, makinig bago tanggihan ang payo, at patuloy na itanong, “Anong kaisipan ang magpapasaya kay Jehovah dito?”
