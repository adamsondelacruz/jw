1-2. Why do our personal decisions sometimes differ from those of other Christians?

1-2. Bakit may mga desisyon tayo na iba sa desisyon ng mga kapatid?

ANS1 — Direct

EN

Our personal decisions sometimes differ because we are not all the same. Each Christian has his own viewpoint, and our thinking is affected by our culture, family background, and personal experiences. So even faithful Christians may make different choices in personal matters. But those differences should not damage congregation peace and unity. (Ephesians 4:3)

TL

Minsan iba-iba ang personal na desisyon natin dahil magkakaiba tayo. May kani-kaniya tayong pananaw, at naaapektuhan tayo ng kultura, pamilya, at mga karanasan natin. Kaya kahit tapat na Kristiyano, puwedeng magkaiba ang piliin sa personal na bagay. Pero hindi dapat masira ng pagkakaibang iyon ang kapayapaan at pagkakaisa ng kongregasyon. (Efeso 4:3)

ANS2 — Deeper

EN

Ephesians 4:3 teaches me that Jehovah values unity even when personalities and backgrounds differ. He does not expect every servant to have the same preference or life experience. That draws me closer to Jehovah because his congregation has room for variety, but not for pride or division. Unity is something we actively protect.

TL

Itinuturo ng Efeso 4:3 na mahalaga kay Jehova ang pagkakaisa kahit magkakaiba ang personalidad at background natin. Hindi niya inaasahang pare-pareho ang preference o experience ng mga lingkod niya. Mas napapalapit ako kay Jehova dahil may lugar sa kongregasyon niya ang variety, pero walang lugar ang pride at division.

3. What might cause us to judge a fellow Christian for a decision he has made?

3. Bakit posibleng mahirapan tayong igalang ang desisyon ng isang kapatid?

ANS1 — Direct

EN

We might judge a fellow Christian because we love him and fear that his decision could hurt him spiritually. Proverbs 17:17 shows that real friends care. So our motive may be good. But even with good motives, we may wrongly feel that we must convince him, criticize his decision, or talk about our concerns to others.

TL

Posibleng mahusgahan natin ang kapatid dahil mahal natin siya at nag-aalala tayo na baka makasira sa kaniya spiritually ang desisyon niya. Ipinapakita ng Kawikaan 17:17 na nagmamalasakit ang tunay na kaibigan. Kaya maaaring maganda ang motive natin. Pero kahit maganda ang motive, baka mali nating isipin na kailangan natin siyang kumbinsihin o punahin sa iba.

ANS2 — Deeper

EN

Proverbs 17:17 reminds me that love is loyal, but love also needs wisdom. Jehovah does not want concern to turn into control. This helps me ask, "Am I truly helping my brother, or am I trying to make his decision match mine?" Love should protect, not pressure.

TL

Ipinaaalala ng Kawikaan 17:17 na loyal ang pag-ibig, pero kailangan din nitong samahan ng karunungan. Ayaw ni Jehova na ang concern ay maging control. Kaya magandang itanong, "Tinutulungan ko ba talaga ang kapatid, o pinipilit ko lang siyang gayahin ang desisyon ko?" Ang pag-ibig ay proteksiyon, hindi pressure.

4-5. What should we do if a fellow Christian decides to do something that differs from what we would have done?

4-5. Ano ang dapat nating gawin kung hindi tayo sang-ayon sa desisyon ng isang kapatid?

ANS1 — Direct

EN

If a Christian is about to violate a Bible command, love should move us to help him correct his thinking. But if his decision does not break God's law and is simply different from what we would do, we should not judge him. Romans 14:1 says not to pass judgment on differing opinions. (Proverbs 27:5, 6; Romans 14:1)

TL

Kung may malalabag na utos sa Bibliya ang isang kapatid, dapat tayong pakilusin ng pag-ibig na tulungan siyang ituwid ang kaisipan niya. Pero kung wala naman siyang nilalabag na utos ng Diyos at iba lang ang desisyon niya sa atin, hindi natin siya dapat hatulan. Sinasabi ng Roma 14:1 na huwag humatol dahil lang iba ang opinyon niya. (Kawikaan 27:5, 6; Roma 14:1)

ANS2 — Deeper

EN

Romans 14:1 teaches me that Jehovah makes a difference between Bible commands and personal opinions. That protects the peace of the congregation. Jehovah gives clear standards, but he does not make rules for every preference. Respecting that helps me imitate his reasonableness.

TL

Itinuturo ng Roma 14:1 na may pagkakaiba sa paningin ni Jehova ang utos sa Bibliya at personal na opinyon. Pinoprotektahan nito ang kapayapaan ng kongregasyon. Nagbibigay si Jehova ng malinaw na pamantayan, pero hindi siya gumagawa ng rules para sa bawat preference. Kapag iginagalang ko iyan, natutularan ko ang pagiging makatuwiran niya.
