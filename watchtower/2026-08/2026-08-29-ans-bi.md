## Jehovah’s Witnesses / Mga Saksi ni Jehova

> *<sub>**1-2.** How do many feel about the field service group arrangement?</sub>*  
> *<sub>**1-2.** Ano ang nararamdaman ng marami sa pagkakaroon ng mga grupo sa paglilingkod sa larangan?</sub>*

**ANS1 - Direct (EN):** Many feel warmly about the field service group arrangement because it helps them in practical and spiritual ways. The paragraph mentions publishers who learned new ministry skills, received help to reach spiritual goals, made lifelong friends, and came to view their group like family.

**ANS1 - Direct (TL):** Marami ang nagpapahalaga sa field service group kasi malaking tulong ito sa kanila. Sa parapo, may mga kapatid na natutong mangaral at magturo nang mas mahusay, natulungang umabot ng spiritual goals, nagkaroon ng tunay na mga kaibigan, at itinuring ang grupo nila na parang pamilya.

**ANS2 - Deeper (EN):** This teaches me that Jehovah helps us serve him not only as individuals but as part of a loving spiritual family. Psalm 148 shows that all kinds of people praise Jehovah together, and Matthew 24:14 reminds me that our small field service group supports a worldwide preaching work.

**ANS2 - Deeper (TL):** Natututuhan ko na tinutulungan tayo ni Jehova hindi lang bilang indibidwal, kundi bilang bahagi ng isang mapagmahal na spiritual family. Ipinapakita ng Awit 148 na sama-sama tayong pumupuri kay Jehova, at sa Mateo 24:14, nakikita natin na ang maliit nating grupo ay bahagi ng pandaigdig na pangangaral.

**ANS1 - Direct (TL):** Tatalakayin ng artikulo ang tatlong bagay: ang papel ng tagapangasiwa ng grupo at ng assistant niya, kung paano tayo makakatulong sa grupo sa paglilingkod, at kung paano tayo makikinabang sa mapagmahal na kaayusang ito.

Kaya puwede kong itanong, “Nakakatulong ba ako sa magandang espiritu ng grupo?”

## THE GROUP OVERSEER AND HIS ASSISTANT / ANG TAGAPANGASIWA NG GRUPO AT ANG ASSISTANT NIYA

> *<sub>**4.** (a) What is a field service group overseer’s main responsibility? (b) How does a group overseer care for those in his group? (See the box “ Tips for the Group Overseer .”)</sub>*  
> *<sub>**4.** (a) Ano ang pangunahing pananagutan ng tagapangasiwa ng grupo? (b) Paano pinangangalagaan ng tagapangasiwa ng grupo ang mga kagrupo niya? (Tingnan ang kahong “ Mga Tip Para sa Tagapangasiwa ng Grupo .”)</sub>*

**ANS1 - Direct (EN):** His main responsibility is to help the publishers in his group make spiritual advancement. He does that by taking personal interest in each one, giving encouragement and practical help, helping them have a full share in the ministry, and training baptized brothers to qualify for responsibility.

**ANS1 - Direct (TL):** Ang pangunahing pananagutan niya ay tulungan ang mga kagrupo niya na sumulong sa espirituwal. Ginagawa niya ito sa pamamagitan ng pagpapakita ng personal na interes, pagbibigay ng pampatibay at praktikal na tulong, pagtulong sa kanila na maging regular sa ministeryo, at pagsasanay sa bautisadong mga brother.

**ANS2 - Deeper (EN):** The scriptures show Jehovah’s kind way of shepherding. Proverbs 27:23 encourages knowing the flock well, Isaiah 32:2 pictures shelter and refreshment, and James 2:15-17 reminds us that care includes practical help. Jehovah’s organization reflects that personal and tender concern.

**ANS2 - Deeper (TL):** Makikita sa mga teksto ang mabait na paraan ni Jehova ng pagpapastol. Sinasabi ng Kawikaan 27:23 na kilalaning mabuti ang kawan, inilalarawan ng Isaias 32:2 ang proteksiyon at kaginhawahan, at ipinapaalala ng Santiago 2:15-17 na kasama sa pag-aalaga ang praktikal na tulong.

## Tips for the Group Overseer / Mga Tip Para sa Tagapangasiwa ng Grupo

> *<sub>**5.** What challenges do some group overseers face?</sub>*  
> *<sub>**5.** Anong mga hamon ang napapaharap sa mga tagapangasiwa ng grupo?</sub>*

**ANS1 - Direct (EN):** Some group overseers have to balance several congregation responsibilities, care for large groups when there are few appointed brothers, and find time to train others. So even when they are willing, their assignment can require a lot of time, energy, and patience.

**ANS1 - Direct (TL):** May mga tagapangasiwa ng grupo na kailangang pagsabayin ang iba’t ibang atas, alagaan ang malaking grupo kapag kaunti ang appointed brothers, at maglaan ng panahon para magsanay ng iba. Kaya kahit willing sila, kailangan ng maraming oras, lakas, at pasensiya.

**ANS2 - Deeper (EN):** This reminds me to be reasonable with brothers who carry responsibility. They may love the congregation deeply, but they also have limited time and energy. I can support Jehovah’s arrangement by being cooperative, appreciative, and easy to care for.

**ANS2 - Deeper (TL):** Paalala ito na maging makatuwiran sa mga brother na may mabibigat na atas. Mahal nila ang kongregasyon, pero limitado rin ang oras at lakas nila. Masusuportahan ko ang kaayusan ni Jehova kung magiging cooperative, appreciative, at madaling alagaan.

---

> *<sub>**6.** How does keeping field service groups small help group overseers to care for those in their group?</sub>*  
> *<sub>**6.** Bakit makakabuting panatilihing maliit ang mga grupo?</sub>*

**ANS1 - Direct (EN):** Smaller field service groups help the overseer know the publishers well and give better spiritual care. If there are not enough elders, the body of elders may temporarily assign a ministerial servant to care for a group instead of making the groups too large.

**ANS1 - Direct (TL):** Kapag mas maliit ang grupo, mas madaling makilala ng tagapangasiwa ang bawat publisher at maalagaan sila sa espirituwal. Kung kulang ang elders, puwedeng pansamantalang mag-atas ang lupon ng matatanda ng ministeryal na lingkod para mangalaga sa isang grupo.

**ANS2 - Deeper (EN):** I see Jehovah’s wisdom in keeping care personal. A smaller group makes it easier for shepherds to know the publishers, notice needs, and give timely help. That teaches me that Jehovah values people, not just activity or numbers.

**ANS2 - Deeper (TL):** Nakikita ko ang karunungan ni Jehova sa personal na pangangalaga. Kapag maliit ang grupo, mas madaling makilala ng mga pastol ang bawat publisher, makita ang pangangailangan nila, at makatulong agad. Ipinapakita nito na mahalaga kay Jehova ang tao, hindi lang ang dami ng ginagawa.

> *<sub>**7.** In congregations with few elders, what can group overseers do to ensure that all in the group are well cared for? ( 1 Peter 5:2 ) (See also picture.)</sub>*  
> *<sub>**7.** Kapag kaunti ang mga elder, ano ang magagawa ng mga tagapangasiwa ng grupo para masiguradong maaalagaan pa rin ang bawat kagrupo nila? ( 1 Pedro 5:2 ) (Tingnan din ang larawan.)</sub>*

**ANS1 - Direct (EN):** In congregations with few elders, group overseers can train their assistants and delegate appropriate tasks. They can let the assistant take the lead in field service, discuss the group’s needs with him, bring him on shepherding visits, and also use other mature publishers to help train others.

**ANS1 - Direct (TL):** Kapag kaunti ang elders, puwedeng sanayin ng tagapangasiwa ang assistant niya at magbigay ng angkop na mga atas. Puwede niya itong pag-conduct-in sa service, kausapin tungkol sa pangangailangan ng grupo, isama sa shepherding visits, at gamitin din ang mature publishers para tumulong sa training.

**ANS2 - Deeper (EN):** First Peter 5:2 helps me see that Jehovah wants his flock shepherded willingly and lovingly. Training others is part of that love. It protects the congregation because care does not depend on only one person, and it helps more brothers become useful in Jehovah’s service.

**ANS2 - Deeper (TL):** Ipinapakita ng 1 Pedro 5:2 na gusto ni Jehova na ang kawan niya ay maalagaan nang kusa at may pagmamahal. Bahagi ng pag-ibig na iyon ang pagsasanay sa iba. Napoprotektahan ang kongregasyon kasi hindi nakaasa sa isang tao lang ang pag-aalaga.

## CONTRIBUTE TO THE SUCCESS OF YOUR FIELD SERVICE GROUP / MAY MAITUTULONG KA SA GRUPO MO

> *<sub>**8.** How can we contribute to the success of our group? ( Romans 1:12 )</sub>*  
> *<sub>**8.** Paano tayo makakatulong sa grupo natin? ( Roma 1:12 )</sub>*

**ANS1 - Direct (EN):** We can contribute by being present with our group in the ministry. When we preach with others, Romans 1:12 says there is “an interchange of encouragement.” The paragraph also suggests adjusting work schedules, joining by video, letter writing, phone witnessing, or planning regular service with someone.

**ANS1 - Direct (TL):** Makakatulong tayo kung lagi tayong sasama sa grupo sa ministeryo. Sinasabi ng Roma 1:12 na may “pagpapatibayan” kapag magkasama tayong naglilingkod. Binanggit din ang pag-adjust ng schedule, pag-tie in online, letter writing, phone witnessing, o regular na paglilingkod kasama ng iba.

**ANS2 - Deeper (EN):** This teaches me that my presence matters more than I may realize. Jehovah can use my effort to encourage someone else, and Philippians 2:13 assures me that he can give both the desire and the power to act when I ask for his help.

**ANS2 - Deeper (TL):** Natututuhan ko na mahalaga ang presensiya ko, kahit minsan pakiramdam ko maliit lang iyon. Puwede akong gamitin ni Jehova para mapatibay ang iba, at sa Filipos 2:13, kaya niyang ibigay ang pagnanais at lakas na kumilos kapag humihingi ako ng tulong.

---

> *<sub>**9.** How might we contribute to a warm, loving atmosphere in our field service group? ( Romans 12:13 )</sub>*  
> *<sub>**9.** Paano natin maipaparamdam sa mga kagrupo natin na mahal natin sila? ( Roma 12:13 )</sub>*

**ANS1 - Direct (EN):** We can contribute by being hospitable. Romans 12:13 encourages us to follow “the course of hospitality.” The paragraph gives practical examples, such as offering our home for a field service meeting, helping care for a visiting speaker, or keeping hospitality simple and focused on knowing others.

**ANS1 - Direct (TL):** Makakatulong tayo kung magiging mapagpatuloy. Hinihimok tayo ng Roma 12:13 na “maging mapagpatuloy.” Puwede nating ialok ang bahay natin bilang tagpuan, tumulong sa pag-aasikaso sa visiting speaker, o gawing simple ang hospitality at magpokus sa pagkilala sa iba.

**ANS2 - Deeper (EN):** This helps me see that Jehovah values warmth more than impressive arrangements. Nehemiah 8:10 and Acts 20:35 show that giving and sharing bring joy. Even simple hospitality can draw us closer to Jehovah and to our brothers and sisters.

**ANS2 - Deeper (TL):** Nakikita ko na mas pinapahalagahan ni Jehova ang init ng pagmamahal kaysa magarbong paghahanda. Ipinapakita ng Nehemias 8:10 at Gawa 20:35 na may saya sa pagbibigay. Kahit simple lang ang hospitality, mas napapalapít tayo kay Jehova at sa mga kapatid.

> *<sub>**10.** What might we do if we want to improve in some aspect of the ministry? ( Proverbs 1:5; 27:17 ) (See also picture.)</sub>*  
> *<sub>**10.** Ano ang puwede nating gawin kung gusto nating humusay sa ministeryo? ( Kawikaan 1:5; 27:17 ) (Tingnan din ang larawan.)</sub>*

**ANS1 - Direct (EN):** If we want to improve in the ministry, we can take the initiative to ask for help. The paragraph suggests asking a pioneer or experienced publisher, telling the group overseer about our goal, and working with others so they can help us develop needed skills.

**ANS1 - Direct (TL):** Puwede tayong magkusa at humingi ng tulong sa pioneer, experienced publisher, o sa tagapangasiwa ng grupo. Kung may gusto tayong pasulungin sa ministeryo, puwede tayong magpatulong, magpasama sa paglilingkod, at magpa-train para mas ma-enjoy natin ang pangangaral.

**ANS2 - Deeper (EN):** Proverbs 1:5 and 27:17 teach me that a wise person keeps learning and lets others sharpen him. Jehovah does not expect me to improve alone. He lovingly gives me experienced brothers and sisters who can help me enjoy the ministry more.

**ANS2 - Deeper (TL):** Itinuturo ng Kawikaan 1:5 at 27:17 na ang marunong ay patuloy na natututo at nagpapatalas sa tulong ng iba. Hindi inaasahan ni Jehova na mag-improve ako nang mag-isa. Maibigin niyang ibinibigay ang experienced brothers and sisters para matulungan akong mas ma-enjoy ang ministeryo.

## HOW YOU CAN BENEFIT FROM YOUR FIELD SERVICE GROUP / MGA PAKINABANG NG PAGIGING BAHAGI NG ISANG GRUPO

> *<sub>**11-12.** What spiritual benefits can you receive from the field service group arrangement? Relate an experience.</sub>*  
> *<sub>**11-12.** Paano tayo nakakapanatiling malapít kay Jehova dahil bahagi tayo ng isang grupo? Maglahad ng karanasan.</sub>*

**ANS1 - Direct (EN):** We receive spiritual benefits through timely shepherding, training in the preaching and teaching work, and help for baptized brothers to qualify for congregation responsibilities. The experience of Wendy also shows how a group can provide loving advice and training that helps someone for many years.

**ANS1 - Direct (TL):** Nakakatanggap tayo ng spiritual benefits gaya ng napapanahong shepherding, pagsasanay sa pangangaral at pagtuturo, at tulong sa bautisadong mga brother para maging kuwalipikado sa mga atas. Makikita rin sa karanasan ni Wendy na ang grupo ay puwedeng magbigay ng payo at training na makakatulong nang maraming taon.

**ANS2 - Deeper (EN):** This paragraph shows Jehovah’s tender concern for those who may need extra care. First Thessalonians 2:8 speaks of giving ourselves, and James 1:27 highlights care for widows and orphans. Jehovah’s arrangement helps vulnerable ones feel noticed, valued, and spiritually protected.

**ANS2 - Deeper (TL):** Ipinapakita ng parapo ang malambing na malasakit ni Jehova sa mga nangangailangan ng dagdag na pag-aalaga. Sinasabi ng 1 Tesalonica 2:8 ang pagbibigay ng sarili, at ipinapakita ng Santiago 1:27 ang pag-aalaga sa mga biyuda at ulila. Sa kaayusang ito, nararamdaman nilang mahalaga sila.

> *<sub>**13.** What are some emotional benefits that we can receive when we support our field service group? (See also picture.)</sub>*  
> *<sub>**13.** Ano pa ang isang pakinabang ng pagiging bahagi ng grupo sa paglilingkod? (Tingnan din ang larawan.)</sub>*

**ANS1 - Direct (EN):** We receive emotional benefits because we form close bonds as we work “side by side” in the ministry. The paragraph says smaller groups can help shy or anxious ones develop friendships, and those friends can support us when we are sick, discouraged, grieving, or facing hardship.

**ANS1 - Direct (TL):** Nakakatanggap tayo ng emotional benefits kasi nagiging close tayo sa mga kagrupo natin habang “nagtutulungan” sa ministeryo. Sinasabi ng parapo na nakakatulong ang maliit na grupo sa mahiyain o anxious na magkaroon ng kaibigan, at nandiyan sila kapag may sakit, lungkot, pagdadalamhati, o problema.

**ANS2 - Deeper (EN):** Philippians 1:27 and Proverbs 17:17 help me see that Jehovah wants us to stand together, not struggle alone. A field service group can become a place where Christian love is felt in real situations, especially when someone is weak, anxious, or grieving.

**ANS2 - Deeper (TL):** Ipinapakita ng Filipos 1:27 at Kawikaan 17:17 na gusto ni Jehova na magtulungan tayo, hindi lumaban nang mag-isa. Ang service group ay puwedeng maging lugar kung saan ramdam ang tunay na pag-ibig, lalo na kapag ang isa ay nanghihina, anxious, o nagdadalamhati.

> *<sub>**14.** How do we benefit from our field service group during times of disaster or emergency?</sub>*  
> *<sub>**14.** Paano tayo natutulungan ng grupo natin kapag may sakuna o emergency?</sub>*

**ANS1 - Direct (EN):** During disasters or emergencies, our field service group helps us receive practical help quickly. The paragraph mentions situations like disease outbreaks, natural disasters, medical emergencies, persecution, or financial crises. Group overseers can help carry out plans to care for those with special needs.

**ANS1 - Direct (TL):** Kapag may sakuna o emergency, nakakatulong ang grupo para mabilis tayong makatanggap ng praktikal na tulong. Binanggit ang epidemya, likas na sakuna, medical emergency, pag-uusig, o financial crisis. Tumutulong ang mga tagapangasiwa ng grupo sa pag-aalaga sa may special needs.

**ANS2 - Deeper (EN):** This shows that Jehovah’s love is organized and practical. He knows emergencies can come suddenly, so he provides arrangements that help elders know who needs assistance. I can apply this by staying connected with my group and by being ready to help others when a need arises.

**ANS2 - Deeper (TL):** Makikita dito na ang pag-ibig ni Jehova ay maayos at praktikal. Alam niyang biglang dumarating ang emergencies, kaya may kaayusan para malaman ng mga elder kung sino ang kailangang tulungan. Maia-apply ko ito kung mananatili akong connected sa grupo at handang tumulong.

> *<sub>**15.** Sa anong gawain nagkakaisa ang organisasyon ni Jehova?</sub>*

**ANS1 - Direct (EN):** All of us in Jehovah’s organization are united in praising Jehovah and preaching the good news. The paragraph shows that Jehovah’s organization includes a vast heavenly part and millions on earth, but each field service group still has a share in the same worldwide work.

**ANS1 - Direct (TL):** Nagkakaisa tayong lahat sa organisasyon ni Jehova sa pagpuri sa kaniya at pangangaral ng mabuting balita. Ipinapakita ng parapo na napakalaki ng organisasyon, may makalangit na bahagi at milyon-milyon sa lupa, pero ang bawat service group ay may bahagi pa rin sa iisang gawain.

**ANS2 - Deeper (EN):** Zechariah 8:23, Revelation 14:6, 7, and Psalm 111:1 help me see the dignity of even a small service group. When we meet and preach together, we are connected to Jehovah’s universal organization and to the worldwide praise of his name.

**ANS2 - Deeper (TL):** Tinutulungan ako ng Zacarias 8:23, Apocalipsis 14:6, 7, at Awit 111:1 na makita ang halaga ng kahit maliit na service group. Kapag nagtitipon at nangangaral tayo, konektado tayo sa buong organisasyon ni Jehova at sa pandaigdig na pagpuri sa pangalan niya.
