Every scripture below is taken only from the paragraph that the question covers. ANS1 = direct answer from the paragraph; ANS2 = a deeper spiritual insight drawn from that same paragraph.

1-2. What happened as Jesus was teaching in Capernaum?

1-2. Ano ang nangyari noong nagtuturo si Jesus sa Capernaum?

ANS1 — Direct

EN

While Jesus was teaching in Capernaum, many disciples were shocked by his words about eating his flesh and drinking his blood. They had previously been impressed by his works and even recognized him as the Prophet who was to come. But when they faced a test of faith, they were offended and stopped following him. (John 6:60; John 6:53, 54; John 6:14)

TL

Noong nagtuturo si Jesus sa Capernaum, marami sa mga alagad niya ang nagulat sa sinabi niya tungkol sa pagkain ng kaniyang laman at pag-inom ng kaniyang dugo. Dati, hanga sila sa ginawa ni Jesus at kinilala pa siyang Propeta na darating. Pero nang masubok ang pananampalataya nila, hindi nila ito natanggap at tumigil sila sa pagsunod. (Juan 6:60; Juan 6:53, 54; Juan 6:14)

ANS2 — Deeper

EN

This teaches me that faith based only on excitement can weaken when something is hard to understand. Those disciples had seen powerful evidence, but offense replaced loyalty. Jehovah wants our faith to go deeper than emotion. When I do not fully understand something, that is when I need humility, patience, and trust.

TL

Itinuturo nito na ang pananampalatayang nakabase lang sa excitement ay puwedeng humina kapag may hindi maintindihan. Nakakita na ang mga alagad na iyon ng matibay na ebidensiya, pero napalitan ng pagkatisod ang loyalty nila. Gusto ni Jehova na mas malalim ang faith natin kaysa emotion.

3. How did the apostles react to Jesus' words, and why? (See also picture.)

3. Ano ang naging reaksiyon ng mga apostol sa sinabi ni Jesus, at bakit? (Tingnan din ang larawan.)

ANS1 — Direct

EN

The apostles reacted with loyalty. Peter said: "Lord, whom shall we go away to? You have sayings of everlasting life." They may not have fully understood Jesus' words, but they stayed because they were convinced of who he was. Their faith rested on the truth that Jesus was the Christ of God and had Jehovah's support. (John 6:68, 69; Luke 9:20, 35)

TL

Iba ang naging reaksiyon ng mga apostol. Sinabi ni Pedro: "Panginoon, kanino kami pupunta? Nasa iyo ang mga salitang nagbibigay ng buhay na walang hanggan." Maaaring hindi pa nila lubusang naintindihan ang sinabi ni Jesus, pero nanatili sila dahil kumbinsido sila kung sino siya. Naniniwala silang si Jesus ang Kristo ng Diyos at sinusuportahan siya ni Jehova. (Juan 6:68, 69; Lucas 9:20, 35)

ANS2 — Deeper

EN

Peter's answer helps me see what loyalty looks like when understanding is incomplete. He did not say, "We understand everything." He said, in effect, "We know whom we trust." That draws me closer to Jehovah because he does not ask us to know every detail immediately. He asks us to trust what we already know is true.

TL

Tinutulungan ako ng sagot ni Pedro na makita kung ano ang loyalty kapag hindi pa kumpleto ang understanding. Hindi niya sinabing, "Naiintindihan namin ang lahat." Parang sinabi niya, "Alam namin kung kanino kami nagtitiwala." Hindi hinihiling ni Jehova na alam natin agad ang bawat detalye; gusto niyang magtiwala tayo sa alam nating totoo.

4. What tests might we face?

4. Ano ang mga posibleng sumubok sa katapatan natin kay Jehova?

ANS1 — Direct

EN

Our loyalty to Jehovah can be tested in several ways. We may learn of an adjustment in understanding or a change in how the disciple-making work is organized. We may struggle to understand why Jehovah allows a tragedy. Our faith can also be tested when a close friend hurts us or when someone we respect treats us unfairly.

TL

Puwedeng masubok ang katapatan natin kay Jehova sa iba't ibang paraan. Baka magkaroon ng adjustment sa pagkaunawa natin sa isang teksto o pagbabago sa paraan ng pangangaral. Baka mahirapan tayong maintindihan kung bakit hinayaan ni Jehova ang isang trahedya. Puwede ring masubok tayo kapag nasaktan tayo ng malapít na kaibigan o ng taong iginagalang natin.

ANS2 — Deeper

EN

This paragraph is realistic. It shows that tests of faith are not always persecution from outside. Sometimes the test is confusion, pain, disappointment, or hurt feelings. Since no scripture is cited in the paragraph, I would keep the point simple: Jehovah knows loyalty can be tested in very personal ways, and he can help us stay close to him through them.

TL

Realistic ang paragraph na ito. Ipinapakita nitong hindi lang persecution mula sa labas ang sumusubok sa faith. Minsan ang test ay confusion, pain, disappointment, o nasaktang damdamin. Dahil walang binanggit na teksto rito, simple ang punto: alam ni Jehova na personal ang ilang pagsubok, at kaya niya tayong tulungang manatiling malapít.

5. Illustrate why our faith needs to be built on a strong foundation. (Colossians 1:23)

5. Bakit dapat nating patibayin ang pananampalataya natin? Magbigay ng ilustrasyon. (Colosas 1:23)

ANS1 — Direct

EN

Our faith needs a strong foundation because tests of faith can shake us, just as earthquakes shake buildings. A building in an earthquake-prone area needs a solid foundation to survive. Similarly, if we keep strengthening the foundation of our faith, we can remain steadfast and loyal when trials come. (1 Corinthians 15:58; Colossians 1:23)

TL

Kailangang matibay ang pundasyon ng pananampalataya natin dahil puwede tayong mayanig ng mga pagsubok, gaya ng building na nayayanig ng lindol. Para hindi gumuho ang building, kailangan nito ng matibay na pundasyon. Sa katulad na paraan, kung patuloy nating pinatitibay ang faith natin, mananatili tayong matatag at tapat kapag may pagsubok. (1 Corinto 15:58; Colosas 1:23)

ANS2 — Deeper

EN

Colossians 1:23 teaches me that faith must be stable, not temporary. Jehovah is loving because he tells us to strengthen the foundation before the earthquake comes. Spiritual preparation is not fear; it is wisdom. When my faith is built on solid truths, trials may shake me emotionally, but they do not have to move me away from Jehovah.

TL

Itinuturo ng Colosas 1:23 na ang pananampalataya ay dapat matatag, hindi pansamantala. Maibigin si Jehova dahil sinasabi niyang patibayin ang pundasyon bago dumating ang lindol. Ang spiritual preparation ay hindi takot; wisdom ito. Kapag matibay ang foundation ng faith ko, puwede akong mayanig emotionally pero hindi kailangang lumayo kay Jehova.

Three truths helped the apostles remain loyal to Jesus. Tatalakayin din sa artikulo ang tatlong katotohanang tutulong sa atin.

ANS2 — Deeper

EN

This paragraph shows that loyalty grows from conviction. The apostles were not simply attached to a group; they were convinced about Jehovah, Jesus, and divine backing. Since no scripture is cited in the paragraph, the application is simple: when my loyalty is based on truth, not just routine, I can remain steady when emotions are tested.

TL

Ipinapakita nito na ang loyalty ay galing sa conviction. Hindi lang attached ang mga apostol sa isang grupo; kumbinsido sila tungkol kay Jehova, kay Jesus, at sa suporta ng Diyos. Dahil walang binanggit na teksto rito, simple ang application: kapag nakabase sa katotohanan ang loyalty ko, hindi lang routine, mananatili akong matatag.
