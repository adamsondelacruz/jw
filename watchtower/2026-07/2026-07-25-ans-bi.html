Make Wise Decisions Regarding Additional Education - Bilingual (EN / TL)

Every scripture below is taken only from the paragraph that the question covers. ANS1 = direct answer from the paragraph; ANS2 = a deeper spiritual insight drawn from that same paragraph.

1-2. (a) What decisions do young people face? (b) What is meant by the term "additional education"? (See "Expression Explained.")

1-2. (a) Anong mga desisyon ang kailangang gawin ng mga kabataan? (b) Ano ang ibig sabihin ng terminong "karagdagang edukasyon"? (Tingnan ang "Karagdagang Paliwanag.")

ANS1 — Direct

EN

Young people often need to think about what kind of work they will do in the future. The best career is full-time service, but they still need to support themselves financially. Additional education means secular education or training after basic required schooling, such as university, trade school, apprenticeships, vocational training, short courses, or continuing education. (2 Thessalonians 3:10)

TL

Kailangang pag-isipan ng mga kabataan kung anong trabaho ang gagawin nila sa future. Ang pinakamagandang career ay full-time service, pero kailangan pa rin nilang masuportahan ang sarili nila. Ang karagdagang edukasyon ay sekular na edukasyon o training pagkatapos ng basic schooling, gaya ng university, trade school, apprenticeship, vocational training, short course, o continuing education. (2 Tesalonica 3:10)

ANS2 — Deeper

EN

This paragraph shows Jehovah's balanced care. He wants young ones to aim high spiritually, but he also understands the practical need to work. 2 Thessalonians 3:10 reminds me that supporting ourselves is part of responsible Christian living. So planning for work is not unspiritual when it is kept in its proper place under our service to Jehovah.

TL

Ipinapakita ng paragraph na balanced ang malasakit ni Jehova. Gusto niyang mataas ang spiritual goals ng mga kabataan, pero naiintindihan din niya ang practical need na magtrabaho. Ipinaaalala ng 2 Tesalonica 3:10 na bahagi ng pagiging responsableng Kristiyano ang pagsuporta sa sarili. Kaya ang pagpaplano ng trabaho ay hindi unspiritual kung nasa tamang lugar ito sa ilalim ng paglilingkod kay Jehova.

Should You Obtain Additional Education? / Dapat Ka Bang Kumuha ng Karagdagang Edukasyon?

3. Why might some Christians consider obtaining additional education?

3. Bakit may mga Kristiyano na kumukuha ng karagdagang edukasyon?

ANS1 — Direct

EN

Some Christians consider additional education because conditions differ from place to place. In some areas, basic schooling may be enough to get suitable work. In other areas, more training may help a person find employment with better pay or better hours. That can allow a Christian to spend more time in the ministry or other theocratic activities, though the education may also require sacrifices.

TL

May mga Kristiyano na nag-iisip kumuha ng karagdagang edukasyon dahil iba-iba ang sitwasyon sa bawat lugar. Sa ilang lugar, sapat na ang basic schooling para makakuha ng maayos na trabaho. Sa iba naman, makakatulong ang karagdagang training para makahanap ng trabahong mas maganda ang kita o schedule. Puwede itong magbigay ng mas maraming panahon sa ministeryo o ibang paglilingkod, pero may kaakibat din itong sakripisyo.

ANS2 — Deeper

EN

This paragraph teaches me to avoid extreme thinking. Additional education is not automatically good or automatically bad. The real question is whether it will help or hinder my service to Jehovah. Since this paragraph cites no scripture, I would keep the deeper point simple: Jehovah wants us to think carefully about our local situation and our spiritual goals, not copy someone else's decision.

TL

Itinuturo ng paragraph na iwasan ang sobrang pag-iisip. Hindi automatic na mabuti o masama ang karagdagang edukasyon. Ang tanong ay kung makakatulong ba ito o magiging hadlang sa paglilingkod kay Jehova. Dahil walang binanggit na teksto ang paragraph, simple lang ang deeper point: gusto ni Jehova na pag-isipan natin ang sariling sitwasyon at spiritual goals, hindi basta gayahin ang desisyon ng iba.

4. Who should decide if a Christian will obtain additional education? (See also footnote.)

4. Sino ang dapat magpasiya para sa isang Kristiyano kung kukuha siya ng karagdagang edukasyon? (Tingnan din ang talababa.)

ANS1 — Direct

EN

Each adult Christian should make his or her own decision about additional education because Galatians 6:5 says each person carries his own load of responsibility. Parents are responsible for decisions about their minor child's education, and Ephesians 6:1 shows that children should listen to them. Parents can also train their children so they can make wise decisions later. (Galatians 6:5; Ephesians 6:1; Proverbs 22:6)

TL

Ang bawat adultong Kristiyano ang dapat magpasiya kung kukuha siya ng karagdagang edukasyon dahil sinasabi ng Galacia 6:5 na bawat isa ang magdadala ng sariling pananagutan. Responsibilidad naman ng mga magulang ang desisyon tungkol sa edukasyon ng menor de edad na anak, at ipinapakita ng Efeso 6:1 na dapat makinig ang mga anak sa kanila. Puwede rin nilang sanayin ang anak para makagawa ito ng matatalinong desisyon sa future. (Galacia 6:5; Efeso 6:1; Kawikaan 22:6)

ANS2 — Deeper

EN

Galatians 6:5 teaches personal responsibility, and that protects both freedom and peace. Jehovah does not ask other people to carry my conscience for me. At the same time, Proverbs 22:6 shows that parents can lovingly prepare a child to decide wisely later. So Jehovah's arrangement combines responsibility, training, and respect.

TL

Itinuturo ng Galacia 6:5 ang personal responsibility, at pinoprotektahan nito ang freedom at peace. Hindi hinihiling ni Jehova sa ibang tao na sila ang magdala ng konsensiya ko. Pero ipinapakita ng Kawikaan 22:6 na maibiging puwedeng ihanda ng mga magulang ang anak para makapagdesisyon nang tama sa future. Kaya pinagsasama ng kaayusan ni Jehova ang pananagutan, pagsasanay, at respeto.

5. When should a person begin thinking about additional education, and why? (See also picture.)

5. Kailan dapat pag-isipan ng isa ang tungkol sa karagdagang edukasyon, at bakit? (Tingnan din ang larawan.)

ANS1 — Direct

EN

A young person should start thinking about additional education well before finishing required schooling. That gives him and his parents time to research options and choose school subjects that may help with future education or employment. But a decision does not always have to be made before graduation. Proverbs 21:5 supports careful planning, and some first work and pioneer while they decide. (Proverbs 21:5)

TL

Dapat pag-isipan ng kabataan ang karagdagang edukasyon matagal bago matapos ang basic schooling. Magbibigay iyon ng panahon sa kaniya at sa mga magulang niya na mag-research ng options at pumili ng subjects na makakatulong sa future education o trabaho. Pero hindi laging kailangang magdesisyon bago graduation. Sinusuportahan ng Kawikaan 21:5 ang maingat na pagpaplano, at may ilan na nagtatrabaho muna at nagpapayunir habang nagdedesisyon. (Kawikaan 21:5)

ANS2 — Deeper

EN

Proverbs 21:5 helps me see Jehovah as a God who values thoughtful planning. He is not asking young people to panic or rush. He gives time for prayer, family discussion, and research. That makes me feel close to Jehovah because he cares about both our future work and our spiritual safety while we make decisions.

TL

Tinutulungan ako ng Kawikaan 21:5 na makita si Jehova bilang Diyos na nagpapahalaga sa maingat na pagpaplano. Hindi niya gustong mag-panic o magmadali ang mga kabataan. Binibigyan niya tayo ng panahon para manalangin, makipag-usap sa pamilya, at mag-research. Mas napapalapit ako kay Jehova dahil mahalaga sa kaniya ang future work natin at spiritual safety habang nagdedesisyon.

6. What can help someone to make a wise decision regarding additional education?

6. Ano ang makakatulong para makagawa ang isang Kristiyano ng matalinong desisyon tungkol sa karagdagang edukasyon?

ANS1 — Direct

EN

Prayer is the first help because James 1:5 says Jehovah gives wisdom. Then a Christian should honestly analyze his motives, as Psalm 26:2 indicates, and carefully analyze the education he is considering. Proverbs 14:15 says the shrewd one ponders each step, so wisdom means praying, checking the heart, and examining the course carefully. (James 1:5; Psalm 26:2; Proverbs 14:15)

TL

Ang unang makakatulong ay panalangin dahil sinasabi ng Santiago 1:5 na nagbibigay si Jehova ng karunungan. Pagkatapos, dapat suriin ng Kristiyano ang motibo niya, gaya ng ipinapakita ng Awit 26:2, at suriin nang mabuti ang edukasyong pinag-iisipan niyang kunin. Sinasabi ng Kawikaan 14:15 na pinag-iisipan ng marunong ang bawat hakbang, kaya kasama sa wisdom ang panalangin, pagsusuri ng puso, at maingat na pag-aaral sa course. (Santiago 1:5; Awit 26:2; Kawikaan 14:15)

ANS2 — Deeper

EN

James 1:5 teaches me that Jehovah does not leave us alone with complicated decisions. He invites us to ask for wisdom. Psalm 26:2 also shows that Jehovah can help us examine motives we may not see clearly. That draws me closer to him because he is not only interested in what decision I make but also in what is happening in my heart.

TL

Itinuturo ng Santiago 1:5 na hindi tayo pinababayaan ni Jehova sa komplikadong desisyon. Iniimbitahan niya tayong humingi ng karunungan. Ipinapakita rin ng Awit 26:2 na matutulungan tayo ni Jehova na makita ang motibong hindi natin malinaw na nakikita. Mas napapalapit ako sa kaniya dahil hindi lang siya interesado sa desisyon ko kundi pati sa nangyayari sa puso ko.

Analyze Your Motives / Suriin ang Motibo Mo

7. What care should Christians take when considering additional education?

7. Ano ang dapat tiyakin ng isang Kristiyano kung pinaplano niyang kumuha ng karagdagang edukasyon?

ANS1 — Direct

EN

A Christian should carefully examine why he wants the education. Wanting work that is enjoyable or better paid is not necessarily wrong, since 1 Timothy 5:8 shows we need to provide. But Proverbs 23:4, 5, 1 Timothy 6:8-10, and 1 John 2:17 warn against materialism, seeking prestige, or putting security in this system. Such motives can leave a person empty and pull him away from Jehovah.

TL

Dapat suriin ng Kristiyano kung bakit niya gusto ang edukasyong iyon. Hindi naman automatic na mali ang gusto ng trabahong enjoyable o mas maganda ang kita, dahil ipinapakita ng 1 Timoteo 5:8 na kailangan nating maglaan. Pero nagbababala ang Kawikaan 23:4, 5, 1 Timoteo 6:8-10, at 1 Juan 2:17 laban sa materialism, paghahangad ng prestige, o paglalagay ng security sa sistemang ito. Ang ganoong motibo ay puwedeng mag-iwan ng emptiness at magpalayo kay Jehova.

ANS2 — Deeper

EN

This paragraph teaches me that Jehovah looks beyond the course itself and reads the motive behind it. 1 John 2:17 reminds me that the world is passing away, so building my identity on wealth or status is unstable. Jehovah's warning is loving because he wants my education decisions to protect my friendship with him, not slowly replace it.

TL

Itinuturo nito na tinitingnan ni Jehova hindi lang ang course kundi ang motibo sa likod nito. Ipinaaalala ng 1 Juan 2:17 na lumilipas ang sanlibutan, kaya hindi stable kung ibabase ko ang identity ko sa pera o status. Maibigin ang babala ni Jehova dahil gusto niyang protektahan ng desisyon ko sa edukasyon ang friendship ko sa kaniya, hindi ito palitan.

8-9. (a) How should Christians view education? (Matthew 6:33) (b) What do you learn from the comments made by Josefina, Morine, and Iris?

8-9. (a) Ano ang dapat na maging tingin natin sa edukasyon? (Mateo 6:33) (b) Ano ang natutuhan mo sa mga sinabi nina Josefina, Morine, at Iris?

ANS1 — Direct

EN

Christians should view education as a tool, not as the center of life. Matthew 6:33 says to keep seeking first the Kingdom, and Matthew 22:37, 38 and Philippians 3:8 show that serving Jehovah must be most important. The comments teach that education should revolve around spiritual goals, help support service, and never be treated as the source of true success or happiness.

TL

Dapat tingnan ng mga Kristiyano ang edukasyon bilang tool, hindi sentro ng buhay. Sinasabi ng Mateo 6:33 na patuloy na unahin ang Kaharian, at ipinapakita ng Mateo 22:37, 38 at Filipos 3:8 na ang paglilingkod kay Jehova ang pinakamahalaga. Natutuhan natin sa mga komento na dapat umikot ang edukasyon sa spiritual goals, makatulong sa paglilingkod, at hindi ituring na pinagmumulan ng tunay na tagumpay o kaligayahan.

ANS2 — Deeper

EN

Matthew 6:33 helps me see Jehovah's loving priority system. He does not say we cannot think about work or education; he says the Kingdom must come first. That makes education safer because it becomes a servant, not a master. When Jehovah is the center, even practical decisions can support lasting happiness.

TL

Tinutulungan ako ng Mateo 6:33 na makita ang maibiging priority system ni Jehova. Hindi niya sinasabing bawal mag-isip tungkol sa trabaho o edukasyon; sinasabi niya na dapat mauna ang Kaharian. Nagiging mas ligtas ang edukasyon dahil nagiging servant ito, hindi master. Kapag si Jehova ang sentro, kahit practical decisions ay makakasuporta sa tunay na kaligayahan.

Analyze the Education / Suriin ang Edukasyong Gusto Mo

10. How can the principle found at Deuteronomy 32:29 be applied when deciding on additional education?

10. Paano makakatulong sa iyo ang prinsipyo sa Deuteronomio 32:29 sa pagdedesisyon mo tungkol sa karagdagang edukasyon?

ANS1 — Direct

EN

Deuteronomy 32:29 urges us to think about the outcome, so a Christian should compare the benefits and disadvantages before choosing education. He should consider other options, including shorter, different, online, or no additional education. Proverbs 18:17 also supports hearing more information before deciding, and 1 Corinthians 10:23 reminds us that not every option is advantageous. (Deuteronomy 32:29; Proverbs 18:17; 1 Corinthians 10:23)

TL

Hinihimok tayo ng Deuteronomio 32:29 na pag-isipan ang magiging resulta, kaya dapat timbangin ng Kristiyano ang pakinabang at hamon bago pumili ng edukasyon. Dapat niyang pag-isipan ang ibang options, gaya ng mas maikling course, ibang field, online course, o kahit hindi na kumuha ng karagdagang edukasyon. Sinusuportahan din ng Kawikaan 18:17 ang pagkuha ng mas maraming impormasyon, at ipinaaalala ng 1 Corinto 10:23 na hindi lahat ng option ay kapaki-pakinabang. (Deuteronomio 32:29; Kawikaan 18:17; 1 Corinto 10:23)

ANS2 — Deeper

EN

Deuteronomy 32:29 teaches me that Jehovah wants us to look ahead, not just at what feels attractive now. That is loving because many education decisions affect time, money, spiritual routine, and family life for years. Jehovah is helping us ask, "Where will this step take me spiritually?"

TL

Itinuturo ng Deuteronomio 32:29 na gusto ni Jehova na tumingin tayo sa future, hindi lang sa mukhang attractive ngayon. Maibigin iyon dahil maraming desisyon sa edukasyon ang makakaapekto sa oras, pera, spiritual routine, at pamilya nang maraming taon. Tinutulungan tayo ni Jehova na itanong, "Saan ako dadalhin ng hakbang na ito spiritually?"

11. Why is it important to consider the schedule of the education or training? (See also pictures.)

11. Bakit mahalagang pag-isipan ang magiging schedule ng pag-aaral o training? (Tingnan din ang mga larawan.)

ANS1 — Direct

EN

The schedule matters because education can take many hours for classes, training, and homework. A Christian needs to ask whether he will still have time for his spiritual routine and family responsibilities. Philippians 1:10 says to make sure of the more important things. Some courses may be too tiring spiritually, while others may allow a person to continue pioneering or serving well. (Philippians 1:10)

TL

Mahalaga ang schedule dahil maraming oras ang puwedeng kainin ng classes, training, at homework. Dapat itanong ng Kristiyano kung magkakaroon pa rin siya ng panahon para sa spiritual routine at responsibilidad sa pamilya. Sinasabi ng Filipos 1:10 na tiyakin ang mas mahahalagang bagay. May mga course na puwedeng sobrang makapagod spiritually, pero may iba namang puwedeng makasabay sa pagpapayunir o mahusay na paglilingkod. (Filipos 1:10)

ANS2 — Deeper

EN

Philippians 1:10 helps me see that Jehovah cares about what gets the best of my time and energy. A course may look useful, but if it consistently leaves me too tired for meetings, study, and ministry, the cost is spiritual. Jehovah lovingly teaches us to protect the things that keep us close to him.

TL

Tinutulungan ako ng Filipos 1:10 na makita na mahalaga kay Jehova kung saan napupunta ang best ng oras at lakas ko. Puwedeng mukhang useful ang course, pero kung lagi akong masyadong pagod para sa meetings, study, at ministry, spiritual ang cost. Maibiging tinuturuan tayo ni Jehova na protektahan ang mga bagay na nagpapanatili sa atin na malapit sa kaniya.

12. How can a person make the best use of his time with respect to education? (Ecclesiastes 12:1)

12. Pagdating sa edukasyon, paano magagamit ng isang Kristiyano sa pinakamabuting paraan ang oras niya? (Eclesiastes 12:1)

ANS1 — Direct

EN

A person can make the best use of time by considering how long the education will take and whether a shorter option could provide practical skills. Ephesians 5:15-17 says to use time wisely, and Ecclesiastes 12:1 urges young ones to remember Jehovah during youth. So the education should not needlessly consume years that could be used to give Jehovah one's best. (Ephesians 5:15-17; Ecclesiastes 12:1)

TL

Magagamit ng isang Kristiyano ang oras sa pinakamabuting paraan kung pag-iisipan niya kung gaano katagal ang edukasyon at kung may mas maikling option na makakapagbigay ng practical skills. Sinasabi ng Efeso 5:15-17 na gamitin nang may karunungan ang oras, at hinihimok ng Eclesiastes 12:1 ang mga kabataan na alalahanin si Jehova habang bata pa. Kaya hindi dapat kainin ng edukasyon ang maraming taon na puwedeng gamitin para ibigay kay Jehova ang best. (Efeso 5:15-17; Eclesiastes 12:1)

ANS2 — Deeper

EN

Ecclesiastes 12:1 gives youth a beautiful value. Jehovah does not view young years as throwaway years for building only a secular future. He wants young ones to remember him while they have strength and energy. That makes me see time as a sacred resource, not just a schedule to fill.

TL

Napakaganda ng halaga na ibinibigay ng Eclesiastes 12:1 sa kabataan. Hindi tinitingnan ni Jehova ang kabataan bilang panahong para lang bumuo ng secular future. Gusto niyang alalahanin siya ng mga kabataan habang malakas at energetic pa sila. Kaya nakikita ko ang oras bilang sagradong resource, hindi lang schedule na pupunuin.

13. How could a person be affected by where he lives while obtaining additional education?

13. Bakit dapat mong pag-isipan ang lokasyon kung saan mo pinaplanong mag-aral?

ANS1 — Direct

EN

Location can greatly affect a person's spirituality. If the course is far away, requires living on campus, or means rooming with unbelievers, that can create serious spiritual danger. Proverbs 22:3 says the shrewd one sees danger and conceals himself, and 1 Corinthians 15:33 warns that bad associations spoil useful habits. Staying near family or choosing online options may reduce risk. (Proverbs 22:3; 1 Corinthians 15:33)

TL

Malaki ang epekto ng lokasyon sa spirituality ng isang tao. Kung malayo ang course, kailangang tumira sa campus, o makasama sa bahay ang mga di-kapananampalataya, puwede itong magdulot ng seryosong spiritual danger. Sinasabi ng Kawikaan 22:3 na nakikita ng marunong ang panganib at umiiwas, at nagbababala ang 1 Corinto 15:33 na nasisira ng masasamang kasama ang kapaki-pakinabang na ugali. Baka mas ligtas kung malapit sa pamilya o online ang option. (Kawikaan 22:3; 1 Corinto 15:33)

ANS2 — Deeper

EN

Proverbs 22:3 shows Jehovah's care before danger becomes damage. He does not want us to test how strong we are around constant temptation. 1 Corinthians 15:33 reminds me that environment shapes habits. Jehovah's counsel helps me choose surroundings that protect my friendship with him.

TL

Ipinapakita ng Kawikaan 22:3 ang malasakit ni Jehova bago maging pinsala ang panganib. Ayaw niyang subukan natin kung gaano tayo katatag kapag lagi tayong nasa tukso. Ipinaaalala ng 1 Corinto 15:33 na hinuhubog ng environment ang habits. Tinutulungan ako ng payo ni Jehova na pumili ng surroundings na poprotekta sa friendship ko sa kaniya.

14. How can the principle found at Luke 14:28 help you when considering additional education?

14. Paano makakatulong sa iyo ang prinsipyo sa Lucas 14:28 kapag pinag-iisipan mong kumuha ng karagdagang edukasyon?

ANS1 — Direct

EN

Luke 14:28 teaches us to calculate the cost before beginning. When considering additional education, a Christian should ask how much it will cost, whether he or his family can afford it, whether there is a cheaper option, and whether any debt can realistically be repaid. Proverbs 22:7 also warns about the burden of debt. (Luke 14:28; Proverbs 22:7)

TL

Itinuturo ng Lucas 14:28 na kalkulahin muna ang gastos bago magsimula. Kapag pinag-iisipan ang karagdagang edukasyon, dapat itanong ng Kristiyano kung magkano ang gagastusin, kaya ba niya o ng pamilya niya, may mas mura bang option, at kaya bang bayaran kung kailangan mang umutang. Nagbababala rin ang Kawikaan 22:7 tungkol sa bigat ng utang. (Lucas 14:28; Kawikaan 22:7)

ANS2 — Deeper

EN

Luke 14:28 shows that Jehovah values realistic thinking. He does not want us to make emotional decisions and then suffer unnecessary pressure. Even finances can affect spirituality if debt becomes a heavy burden. Jehovah's counsel protects peace, family needs, and our ability to serve him freely.

TL

Ipinapakita ng Lucas 14:28 na pinapahalagahan ni Jehova ang realistic thinking. Ayaw niyang magdesisyon tayo dahil lang sa emotion at pagkatapos ay ma-pressure nang sobra. Kahit pera ay puwedeng makaapekto sa spirituality kung mabigat ang utang. Pinoprotektahan ng payo ni Jehova ang kapayapaan, pangangailangan ng pamilya, at kakayahan nating maglingkod nang malaya.

15. Why should you consider the work prospects before deciding on a particular type of education?

15. Bakit dapat mo ring isipin ang mga posibleng maging trabaho sa kursong gusto mong kunin?

ANS1 — Direct

EN

A person should consider work prospects because some education may not actually lead to suitable employment. Some courses are mostly theoretical, academic, or philosophical and may not provide practical skills employers need. Colossians 2:8 also warns against being taken captive by human philosophy. So a Christian should ask whether the course will truly help him find work in his area or where he plans to live. (Colossians 2:8)

TL

Dapat isipin ang work prospects dahil may edukasyon na hindi talaga humahantong sa angkop na trabaho. May mga course na puro theory, academic ideas, o philosophy at hindi nagbibigay ng practical skills na kailangan ng employers. Nagbababala rin ang Colosas 2:8 laban sa pagiging bihag ng pilosopiya ng tao. Kaya dapat itanong ng Kristiyano kung talagang makakatulong ang course para makahanap ng trabaho sa lugar niya o sa planong lipatan. (Colosas 2:8)

ANS2 — Deeper

EN

Colossians 2:8 helps me see that not all education is spiritually neutral. Some training may waste time, and some ideas may even weaken faith. Jehovah is not against learning; he is protecting our mind and future. A wise decision asks not only, "Can I study this?" but "Will this truly help me serve Jehovah?"

TL

Tinutulungan ako ng Colosas 2:8 na makita na hindi lahat ng edukasyon ay spiritually neutral. May training na puwedeng magsayang ng oras, at may ideas pa na puwedeng magpahina ng pananampalataya. Hindi laban si Jehova sa pag-aaral; pinoprotektahan niya ang isip at future natin. Itatanong ng matalinong desisyon hindi lang, "Puwede ko ba itong aralin?" kundi "Talaga bang makakatulong ito sa paglilingkod ko kay Jehova?"

16. Why is it worthwhile to consider the type of work that the education may lead to?

16. Bakit magandang pag-isipan ang klase ng trabahong makukuha mo sa kursong gusto mo?

ANS1 — Direct

EN

It is worthwhile because the education may affect the kind of work available later. A Christian should consider whether he will enjoy the work, what the environment is like, whether it is unsafe, stressful, or overly competitive, what it pays, and whether it will require ongoing education. Most important, he should ask whether the work will allow him to put Kingdom interests first. (Ecclesiastes 3:12, 13; 12:13)

TL

Magandang pag-isipan ito dahil makakaapekto ang edukasyon sa klase ng trabahong makukuha sa future. Dapat isipin ng Kristiyano kung mae-enjoy niya ang trabaho, ano ang environment, delikado ba, stressful, o sobrang competitive, magkano ang suweldo, at kailangan ba ng tuloy-tuloy na pag-aaral. Pinakamahalaga, dapat itanong kung makakatulong ang trabahong iyon na unahin ang Kaharian. (Eclesiastes 3:12, 13; 12:13)

ANS2 — Deeper

EN

Ecclesiastes 12:13 brings the decision back to the deepest purpose: fear God and obey him. Jehovah cares about more than whether a job pays. He cares whether the work allows us to remain spiritually healthy and useful to him. That makes employment a spiritual decision, not just an economic one.

TL

Ibinabalik ng Eclesiastes 12:13 ang desisyon sa pinakamalalim na purpose: matakot sa Diyos at sundin siya. Hindi lang mahalaga kay Jehova kung magkano ang sahod ng trabaho. Mahalaga sa kaniya kung mananatili tayong spiritually healthy at magagamit sa paglilingkod. Kaya ang trabaho ay spiritual decision din, hindi lang economic decision.

17. (a) How can a Christian find the information he needs to make a wise decision regarding education? (b) What Bible principles shed light on this decision? (See the box "Bible Principles to Consider.")

17. (a) Paano ka makakahanap ng impormasyong kailangan mo para makagawa ng matalinong desisyon tungkol sa edukasyon? (b) Anong mga prinsipyo sa Bibliya ang makakatulong sa iyo? (Tingnan ang kahong "Mga Prinsipyo sa Bibliya na Puwede Mong Pag-isipan.")

ANS1 — Direct
