# "The God of Truth" Always Fulfills His Purpose
**The Watchtower — April 2026 | June 8–14, 2026**
*Song 8 & 2 | "You have redeemed me, O Jehovah, the God of truth." — Psalm 31:5*

---

## Study Questions

> *<sub>**1.** Why can we be confident that we are safe from Satan's wicked lies?</sub>*

**Direct:** Because Jehovah, "the God of truth," is our refuge. As the psalmist sang in **Psalm 31:2-5**, when we entrust our spirit to him, he becomes our rock and stronghold against deception. We are safe not because we are smart enough to spot every lie, but because Jehovah himself shields us.

**Deeper:** This text teaches us something tender about Jehovah's title — "the God of TRUTH." (**Psalm 31:5**) In a world flooded with disinformation, our Father stands apart as the One whose word can ALWAYS be trusted. In ministry, this becomes our most reassuring offer: not just truth about a topic, but a relationship with Truth himself.

---

> *<sub>**2.** What will we discuss in this article?</sub>*

**Direct:** The article examines why we can trust in the God of truth, how Jehovah fulfills his purpose despite Satan's lies, how he is gathering all lovers of truth today, and why we can look to the future with absolute confidence.

**Deeper:** Notice how each section points back to Jehovah's character. He is not merely COMPETENT to fulfill his purpose — he IS truth itself. Every paragraph that follows is really an invitation to know him better. In the ministry, this is what we offer: not arguments, but introductions to a Father whose word never fails.

---

> *<sub>**3.** Why can we have absolute confidence in Jehovah?</sub>*

**Direct:** Because he is the Creator of everything (**Genesis 1:1**), the Source of life itself (**Psalm 36:9**), and worthy of all worship (**Revelation 4:11**). A God who designed the universe, sustains every breath we take, and acts only out of love can be trusted absolutely.

**Deeper:** **Revelation 4:11** calls him worthy "to receive the glory and the honor and the power" — not because he demands it, but because he earned it through faithful action. Our confidence is anchored in his TRACK RECORD. In ministry, we don't ask people to trust a stranger — we introduce them to the One whose works have stood for thousands of years.

---

> *<sub>**4.** How do we know that Jehovah loves us?</sub>*

**Direct:** He made humans in his image (**Genesis 1:26**), gave us the earth as our home (**Psalm 115:16**), purposed for us to fill it with joy (**Genesis 1:28; Genesis 2:15**), and his loyal love endures forever (**Psalm 103:17**). Every part of creation is a love letter.

**Deeper:** **Psalm 103:17** says his loyal love is "from everlasting to everlasting." His love is not a reaction to our behavior — it is his unchanging nature. In ministry, this overturns the common idea that God's love must be earned. We can confidently tell householders: he has loved you before you even knew his name.

---

> *<sub>**5(a-b).** How did Jehovah keep his word despite Devil's efforts? What promises has God fulfilled?</sub>*

**(a) Direct:** Despite Satan's lies in Eden, Jehovah declared that the earth would not be created in vain — it would be inhabited. (**Isaiah 45:18-19**) What he speaks, he performs. (**Isaiah 46:10-11**)

**(a) Deeper:** **Isaiah 46:10-11** shows Jehovah declaring "the end from the beginning." This teaches that no opposition can derail his purpose — not because he reacts to threats, but because he sees the end before the start. In ministry, this brings peace to anyone overwhelmed by world events.

**(b) Direct:** He sent his Son (**John 3:16**) to redeem mankind, fulfilling the very first prophecy spoken after Eden. Every promise he has made so far, he has kept.

**(b) Deeper:** **John 3:16** reveals the COST of his faithfulness: his only-begotten Son. Jehovah's promise-keeping isn't cheap words — it's measured in what he was willing to give. In ministry, we can offer this as the deepest proof of his love: a Father who paid the highest price to keep his word to us.

---

> *<sub>**6.** What did Jehovah foretell after the rebellion in Eden?</sub>*

**Direct:** He foretold the bruising of the serpent's head (**Genesis 3:15**) — pointing to the Messiah who would undo Satan's damage. Even in the darkest moment of human history, Jehovah immediately announced hope.

**Deeper:** Notice how quickly hope was given — in the same conversation where sin was exposed. (**Genesis 3:15**) Jehovah didn't wait centuries to comfort his hurting children; mercy came in real time. In ministry, this models how we approach struggling people: we don't lecture first — we point to hope first.

---

> *<sub>**7.** Who follow the Devil, and what will be their end?</sub>*

**Direct:** Those who oppose Jehovah's purpose follow Satan, but their end is certain destruction. **Daniel 2:44** — God's Kingdom "will crush and put an end to all these kingdoms." **Romans 16:20** — "the God who gives peace will crush Satan under your feet shortly."

**Deeper:** **Romans 16:20** promises the crushing of Satan UNDER THE FEET of God's people. This teaches that Jehovah does not merely defeat his enemy — he involves his servants in the victory. In ministry, this gives us dignity: we are not bystanders in this conflict but participants in a triumph already announced.

---

> *<sub>**8.** What action did Jesus take in 1914?</sub>*

**Direct:** In 1914, Jesus was enthroned as King and cast Satan out of heaven (**Revelation 12:7-9**). He rides forth "conquering and to complete his conquest." (**Revelation 6:2**) He acts with truth and righteousness (**Psalm 45:4-6**) so that all nations may know that Jehovah is God. (**Ezekiel 38:23**)

**Deeper:** **Ezekiel 38:23** gives the deepest reason for Jesus' actions: "I will certainly magnify myself and sanctify myself and make myself known to many nations." Every prophetic action — including 1914 — exists to make Jehovah's name known. In ministry, this is the heartbeat of our preaching: not just to save people, but to clear Jehovah's name.

---

> *<sub>**9.** Whom is Jehovah gathering today?</sub>*

**Direct:** He is gathering "all things together in the Christ" (**Ephesians 1:10**) — those who love truth, from every nation, into one unified family.

**Deeper:** **Ephesians 1:10** speaks of "an administration at the full limit of the appointed times." Jehovah is not gathering randomly — there is a deliberate, organized harvest happening right now. In ministry, we are not random witnesses; we are part of a divinely scheduled work. The person we'll meet next at the door may be the one Jehovah has been preparing all along.

---

> *<sub>**10.** How is Jehovah freeing people from false religion's lies?</sub>*

**Direct:** Through the angel's message to "fear God and give him glory" (**Revelation 14:6-7**), the call to "get out of her, my people" (**Revelation 18:2-4**), and the prophesied collapse of "Babylon the Great" — false religion will be hated and made desolate. (**Revelation 17:16**)

**Deeper:** **Revelation 18:4** records Jehovah's tender words: "my people." Even while still in false religion, he claims them as his own. (**Revelation 18:2-4**) This teaches that his love precedes their decision to leave. In ministry, we don't approach people of other faiths as enemies — we approach them as his people, still being called home.

---

> *<sub>**11.** How can we identify Jehovah's true worshippers?</sub>*

**Direct:** They form a "great crowd... out of all nations and tribes" who give credit for salvation to God and Christ (**Revelation 7:9-10**), they preach the good news to all nations (**Mark 13:10**), and they are distinguished from those who do not serve God (**Malachi 3:18**).

**Deeper:** **Malachi 3:18** promises that we will "see the distinction" between those who serve God and those who do not. The distinction is not in clothes, race, or ritual — it is in HEART. (**Mark 13:10**) In ministry, this reassures us: the genuine truth-lover will recognize the genuine message. Our job is to deliver it; Jehovah does the dividing.

---

> *<sub>**12.** How does the preaching work help accomplish Jehovah's purpose?</sub>*

**Direct:** The Kingdom good news must be preached in all the inhabited earth as a witness before the end comes. (**Matthew 24:14**) Jesus commissioned his disciples to make disciples of all nations. (**Matthew 28:18-20**) Our preaching is essential to fulfilling Jehovah's announced purpose.

**Deeper:** **Matthew 28:18** begins with "All authority has been given me." Our ministry rests on Jesus' authority, not our own ability. (**Matthew 28:18-20**) In moments of discouragement, this is liberating: results don't depend on our charm — they depend on the King who sent us. We are simply faithful messengers of a Sovereign whose orders cannot fail.

---

> *<sub>**13.** Who are Satan's special targets, and why?</sub>*

**Direct:** Satan is enraged and "knows he has a short period of time" (**Revelation 12:12-13**), so he wages war with "those who observe the commandments of God" — the anointed and their companions. (**Revelation 12:17; Revelation 14:12**) Those who endure prove their love for Jehovah.

**Deeper:** **Revelation 14:12** calls this endurance the mark of "the holy ones." Being a target is not a sign of God's disfavor — it is a sign of being IDENTIFIED with him. In ministry, when we face rejection or hardship, we can see it as confirmation: we are doing the very work Satan is most afraid of.

---

> *<sub>**14.** What will happen when Satan attacks Jehovah's people?</sub>*

**Direct:** Satan will gather "the kings of the entire inhabited earth" to Armageddon (**Revelation 16:13-14, 16**), but they will be defeated by Christ. (**Revelation 19:19-21**) The wicked will receive their part in the lake of fire — the second death. (**Revelation 21:8**)

**Deeper:** **Revelation 21:8** lists the cowardly first among those destroyed. This teaches that Jehovah's deliverance is not just for the brave — but cowardice IS a choice we are warned against. In ministry, this gives us courage now. Standing firm today is preparation for standing firm in the final attack — and Jehovah will fight on our behalf.

---

> *<sub>**15.** Why can we look to the future with confidence?</sub>*

**Direct:** Because Jehovah promises "a new heaven and a new earth" where past troubles will not be remembered (**Isaiah 65:16-17**), and nothing — not death, life, or any creature — can separate us from his love. (**Romans 8:38-39**)

**Deeper:** **Romans 8:38-39** lists everything that could threaten the relationship — death, angels, present things, future things — and declares them powerless. Our security isn't based on what's coming, but on WHO is with us. In ministry, this is the gospel in one sentence: God's love is unbreakable. Anyone who hears that with an open heart will want to know more.

---

> *<sub>**16.** Why do you trust in Jehovah with all your heart?</sub>*

**Direct:** Because every promise he has kept assures us he will keep the rest. The "little one" will become a thousand (**Isaiah 60:22**), people of all languages will come saying "we want to go with you" (**Zechariah 8:23**), and he alone does wondrous works. (**Psalm 72:18-19**)

**Deeper:** **Zechariah 8:23** paints a beautiful picture — ten men grasping a Jew's robe because "we have heard that God is with you." This teaches us that the strongest witness is the visible presence of Jehovah in our lives. In ministry, we don't need to be eloquent — we need to be authentic. People will reach for the truth when they see God is with us.
