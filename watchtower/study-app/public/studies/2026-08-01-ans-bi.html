Stay Spiritually Strong While Getting Additional Education - Bilingual (EN / TL)

Every scripture below is taken only from the paragraph that the question covers. ANS1 = direct answer from the paragraph; ANS2 = a deeper spiritual insight drawn from that same paragraph.

1-2. (a) If you choose to get additional education, what do you need to keep doing? (b) What does it mean to "go on walking orderly in this same course"? (Philippians 3:16)

1-2. (a) Kung nagpasiya kang kumuha ng karagdagang edukasyon, ano pa rin ang kailangan mong gawin? (b) Ano ang ibig sabihin ng "patuloy [na] lumakad nang maayos sa gayong landasin"? (Filipos 3:16)

ANS1 — Direct

EN

If someone chooses additional education, he needs to keep serving Jehovah faithfully and remain close to him. James 4:8a shows the goal is closeness to Jehovah. To "go on walking orderly in this same course" means to continue moving forward spiritually, like soldiers marching in order. So the student should not stand still or retreat spiritually while studying. (James 4:8a; Philippians 3:16)

TL

Kung pipiliin ng isa ang karagdagang edukasyon, kailangan pa rin niyang patuloy na maglingkod nang tapat kay Jehova at manatiling malapít sa kaniya. Ipinapakita ng Santiago 4:8a na ang goal ay closeness kay Jehova. Ang "patuloy na lumakad nang maayos" ay nangangahulugang patuloy na umabante spiritually, gaya ng mga sundalong nagmamartsa nang maayos. (Santiago 4:8a; Filipos 3:16)

ANS2 — Deeper

EN

Philippians 3:16 teaches me that Jehovah values steady spiritual movement. He does not want education, work, or any goal to freeze our progress. The military picture is strong: a Christian student keeps marching with Jehovah's people, not drifting away from the line. That helps me view education as something that must fit my walk with Jehovah, not interrupt it.

TL

Itinuturo ng Filipos 3:16 na mahalaga kay Jehova ang tuloy-tuloy na espirituwal na pagsulong. Ayaw niyang pahintuin ng edukasyon, trabaho, o ibang goal ang progress natin. Malakas ang larawan ng mga sundalo: ang Kristiyanong estudyante ay patuloy na nagmamartsa kasama ng bayan ni Jehova, hindi humihiwalay sa linya.

Maintain a Healthy Spiritual Routine / Huwag Pabayaan ang Espirituwal na Rutin Mo

3. As you are getting additional education, what challenge might you face with regard to your daily routine?

3. Habang kumukuha ka ng karagdagang edukasyon, anong hamon ang puwedeng mapaharap sa iyo dahil nagiging mas busy ka?

ANS1 — Direct

EN

The challenge is that additional education may place new demands on the daily routine. A student may naturally want to do well, but that could lead him to treat Jehovah's service as less important. He might begin to neglect meetings, ministry, personal Bible study, and prayer, which would be like losing the love he had at first. (Revelation 2:4)

TL

Ang hamon ay puwedeng maging mas busy ang routine dahil sa karagdagang edukasyon. Natural lang na gustong galingan ng estudyante, pero baka dahil doon, maging mas mababa na ang tingin niya sa paglilingkod kay Jehova. Baka mapabayaan niya ang pulong, ministeryo, personal study, at panalangin. (Apocalipsis 2:4)

ANS2 — Deeper

EN

Revelation 2:4 reminds me that Jehovah notices not only what we do but the love behind it. A student may still look active, yet the heart can slowly cool off if school becomes first. Jehovah's warning is loving because he wants us to protect our first love before the routine becomes empty.

TL

Ipinaaalala ng Apocalipsis 2:4 na napapansin ni Jehova hindi lang ang ginagawa natin kundi ang pag-ibig sa likod nito. Puwedeng mukhang active pa rin ang estudyante, pero unti-unting lumamig ang puso kapag nauna na ang school. Maibigin ang babala ni Jehova dahil gusto niyang maprotektahan natin ang first love natin.

4. What can help you to have "plenty to do in the work of the Lord"? (1 Corinthians 15:58)

4. Paano mo masusunod ang payo sa 1 Corinto 15:58?

ANS1 — Direct

EN

A student can stay spiritually upright by staying active in Jehovah's service, just as a bicycle stays upright when it keeps moving. First Corinthians 15:58 encourages having "plenty to do in the work of the Lord." That is about priorities, not just time. The student should view himself first as Jehovah's servant and second as a student. (1 Corinthians 15:58; Matthew 22:37)

TL

Mananatiling matatag spiritually ang estudyante kung patuloy siyang aktibo sa paglilingkod kay Jehova, gaya ng bisikletang nananatiling tuwid kapag patuloy na umaandar. Hinihimok ng 1 Corinto 15:58 na maging "maraming ginagawa para sa Panginoon." Tungkol ito sa priorities. Dapat una siyang lingkod ni Jehova, pangalawa lang ang pagiging estudyante. (1 Corinto 15:58; Mateo 22:37)

ANS2 — Deeper

EN

First Corinthians 15:58 connects spiritual activity with being firm and immovable. Jehovah knows that movement in his service keeps us stable. Matthew 22:37 adds the heart of the matter: we serve Jehovah with our whole heart. So spiritual routine is not a side schedule; it is how we keep our love strong.

TL

Iniuugnay ng 1 Corinto 15:58 ang pagiging aktibo sa paglilingkod sa pagiging matatag at di-natitinag. Alam ni Jehova na ang patuloy na pagkilos sa paglilingkod sa kaniya ay nagpapakatatag sa atin. At ipinapakita ng Mateo 22:37 na buong puso ang paglilingkod natin sa kaniya.

5. What can you do to stay active in Jehovah's service?

5. Ano ang puwede mong gawin para makapanatili kang aktibo sa paglilingkod kay Jehova?

ANS1 — Direct

EN

Before starting additional education, a student should make a clear plan to stay regular in meetings, ministry, and personal study. Joshua 1:8, Matthew 28:19, 20, and Hebrews 10:25 point to those priorities. He should also look back at previous school years, make needed adjustments, and be determined to give adequate attention to school without sacrificing worship. (Joshua 1:8; Matthew 28:19, 20; Hebrews 10:25; Matthew 6:24)

TL

Bago magsimula sa karagdagang edukasyon, dapat gumawa ng malinaw na plano para regular pa rin sa pulong, ministeryo, at personal study. Ipinapakita iyan ng Josue 1:8, Mateo 28:19, 20, at Hebreo 10:25. Dapat ding balikan ang dating school years, gumawa ng adjustment, at mag-aral nang mabuti nang hindi isinasakripisyo ang pagsamba. (Josue 1:8; Mateo 28:19, 20; Hebreo 10:25; Mateo 6:24)

ANS2 — Deeper

EN

Matthew 6:24 helps me see why a plan matters. Without a plan, education can quietly become the master. Jehovah kindly helps us decide in advance what will not be sacrificed: meetings, ministry, study, and prayer. A good schedule is really a protection for loyalty.

TL

Tinutulungan ako ng Mateo 6:24 na makita kung bakit mahalaga ang plano. Kapag walang plano, puwedeng unti-unting maging master ang edukasyon. Maibiging tinutulungan tayo ni Jehova na magdesisyon nang maaga kung ano ang hindi natin isasakripisyo: pulong, ministeryo, pag-aaral, at panalangin.

6. What can you consider while making a self-examination? (See also picture.)

6. Ano ang mga puwede mong pag-isipan para matiyak na inuuna mo pa rin si Jehova sa buhay mo? (Tingnan din ang larawan.)

ANS1 — Direct

EN

A student can examine himself regularly, perhaps monthly, by asking whether his priorities are still clear. Warning signs include skipping meetings, arriving late, thinking about schoolwork during meetings, attending only virtually, praying or reading the Bible less, or treating the ministry as a chore to finish quickly. If those signs appear, he should act quickly.

TL

Puwedeng regular na suriin ng estudyante ang sarili niya, halimbawa buwan-buwan, kung malinaw pa rin ang priorities niya. Warning signs ang madalas na pagliban o pag-late sa pulong, pag-iisip ng homework habang meeting, virtual na lang lagi, pagbawas ng panalangin o pagbabasa ng Bibliya, o ministeryong parang chore na lang.

ANS2 — Deeper

EN

This paragraph teaches me that spiritual decline often begins quietly. Jehovah lovingly helps us notice early warning signs before they become habits. Even without a scripture cited in the paragraph, the application is clear: regular self-examination is a way to let Jehovah help us protect our friendship with him.

TL

Itinuturo nito na kadalasan, tahimik lang nagsisimula ang spiritual decline. Maibiging tinutulungan tayo ni Jehova na mapansin ang early warning signs bago maging habits ang mga ito. Kahit walang binanggit na teksto sa paragraph, malinaw ang application: regular self-examination ang proteksiyon sa friendship natin kay Jehova.

"Guard Your Thinking Abilities" / "Bantayan ang Kakayahan Mong Mag-Isip"

7. As you are getting additional education, what challenges might you face with regard to your thinking?

7. Habang kumukuha ka ng karagdagang edukasyon, paano puwedeng maapektuhan ang kakayahan mong mag-isip?

ANS1 — Direct

EN

Depending on the courses, a student may be exposed to the world's philosophy, empty deception, atheism, evolution, or a spirit of self-reliance. Colossians 2:8 warns about being taken captive by such thinking. A student might be taught that success depends only on himself, which can make it harder to rely on Jehovah. (Colossians 2:8)

TL

Depende sa course, puwedeng ma-expose ang estudyante sa pilosopiya ng mundo, mapanlinlang na ideya, ateismo, ebolusyon, o sobrang pag-asa sa sarili. Nagbababala ang Colosas 2:8 laban sa pagiging bihag ng ganoong kaisipan. Baka ituro na ang success ay nakadepende lang sa sarili, at dahil dito, humina ang pag-asa kay Jehova. (Colosas 2:8)

ANS2 — Deeper

EN

Colossians 2:8 shows that Jehovah cares about what captures our mind. Some ideas may sound educated, but they can quietly remove Jehovah from the picture. This makes me appreciate Jehovah's protection. He wants students to learn useful skills without losing the thinking that keeps faith alive.

TL

Ipinapakita ng Colosas 2:8 na mahalaga kay Jehova kung ano ang bumibihag sa isip natin. May mga ideya na mukhang educated, pero unti-unting inaalis si Jehova sa picture. Pinapahalagahan ko ang proteksiyon ni Jehova: gusto niyang matuto tayo ng useful skills nang hindi nawawala ang pananampalataya.

8. Why is it important to "guard your thinking abilities"? (Proverbs 5:1, 2)

8. Bakit mahalagang "mabantayan mo ang kakayahan mong mag-isip"? (Kawikaan 5:1, 2)

ANS1 — Direct

EN

It is important because our thinking abilities are under attack. Proverbs 5:1, 2 tells us to guard them, and 1 Peter 5:8 reminds us that Satan is looking for someone to devour. The world's spirit can weaken faith in Bible morals, creation, and God's existence. What the world calls higher thinking is actually foolishness with God. (Proverbs 5:1, 2; 1 Peter 5:8; 1 Corinthians 3:18-20)

TL

Mahalaga ito dahil inaatake ang kakayahan nating mag-isip. Sinasabi ng Kawikaan 5:1, 2 na bantayan ito, at ipinaaalala ng 1 Pedro 5:8 na naghahanap si Satanas ng masisila. Puwedeng pahinain ng espiritu ng mundo ang pananampalataya sa moral ng Bibliya, paglalang, at pag-iral ng Diyos. (Kawikaan 5:1, 2; 1 Pedro 5:8; 1 Corinto 3:18-20)

ANS2 — Deeper

EN

Proverbs 5:1, 2 helps me see that Jehovah respects our ability to think. He does not ask us to stop thinking; he tells us to protect our thinking. That draws me closer to him because his guidance is not anti-education. It is protection against ideas that could damage faith and moral clarity.

TL

Tinutulungan ako ng Kawikaan 5:1, 2 na makita na nirerespeto ni Jehova ang kakayahan nating mag-isip. Hindi niya sinasabing huwag tayong mag-isip; sinasabi niyang protektahan ito. Hindi anti-education ang gabay niya. Proteksiyon ito laban sa ideyang sisira sa pananampalataya at moral clarity.

9. What can you do to guard your thinking abilities?

9. Ano ang puwede mong gawin para mabantayan ang kakayahan mong mag-isip?

ANS1 — Direct

EN

Before starting, a student should strengthen his Bible-based convictions. He can ask why he believes in God, why he is convinced the Bible is God's Word, and why he trusts Bible standards more than the world's standards. He should also review past school experiences and make needed adjustments to protect his thinking. (2 Timothy 2:16-18)

TL

Bago magsimula, dapat patibayin ng estudyante ang Bible-based convictions niya. Puwede niyang itanong kung bakit siya naniniwalang may Diyos, bakit siya kumbinsidong Salita ng Diyos ang Bibliya, at bakit mas nagtitiwala siya sa pamantayan ng Bibliya kaysa sa mundo. Dapat din niyang balikan ang dating karanasan sa school at gumawa ng adjustments. (2 Timoteo 2:16-18)

ANS2 — Deeper

EN

Second Timothy 2:16-18 shows that wrong talk and ideas can spread spiritually like disease. Jehovah's counsel is loving because prevention is easier than repair. If I strengthen my convictions before pressure comes, I am not just preparing for school; I am preparing to stay loyal to Jehovah.

TL

Ipinapakita ng 2 Timoteo 2:16-18 na ang maling usapan at ideya ay puwedeng kumalat na parang sakit spiritually. Maibigin ang payo ni Jehova dahil mas madaling umiwas kaysa mag-repair. Kapag pinatibay ko ang conviction bago dumating ang pressure, naghahanda akong manatiling loyal kay Jehova.

10. How can you continue to protect your thinking abilities?

10. Paano mo patuloy na maiingatan ang kakayahan mong mag-isip?

ANS1 — Direct

EN

A student can keep examining himself by asking whether he is being influenced by ungodly lifestyles, whether he can see through empty philosophies, and whether he is fully convinced that God's Kingdom is the only solution to mankind's problems. He should continue to study God's Word and meditate so his conviction remains strong. (1 Timothy 4:15)

TL

Patuloy niyang masusuri ang sarili niya kung tinatanggap na ba niya ang masasamang lifestyle, kung nakikita pa niya ang empty philosophies ng mundo, at kung kumbinsido pa rin siyang Kaharian lang ng Diyos ang solusyon sa problema ng tao. Dapat niyang patuloy na pag-aralan at bulay-bulayin ang Bibliya. (1 Timoteo 4:15)

ANS2 — Deeper

EN

First Timothy 4:15 says to ponder and be absorbed in spiritual things. That teaches me that conviction needs feeding. Jehovah gives us a way to keep truth real in our mind: steady study and meditation. When I meditate, I am letting Jehovah's thinking become stronger than the world's voice.

TL

Sinasabi ng 1 Timoteo 4:15 na pag-isipan at maging absorbed sa espirituwal na bagay. Itinuturo nito na kailangan pakainin ang conviction. Binibigyan tayo ni Jehova ng paraan para manatiling totoo sa isip natin ang katotohanan: regular study at meditation.

Manage Your Time Wisely / Gamitin Nang Tama ang Oras Mo

11. As you are getting additional education, what challenge might you face with regard to your use of time?

11. Habang kumukuha ka ng karagdagang edukasyon, bakit posibleng mahirapan kang balansehin ang oras mo?

ANS1 — Direct

EN

Additional education may place heavy demands on time, especially during exams or project deadlines. If a student does not manage time wisely, he may experience undue stress or even burnout. That is why he needs to care for his physical and emotional health, not only his school requirements.

TL

Puwedeng kainin ng karagdagang edukasyon ang maraming oras, lalo na kapag may exams o project deadlines. Kung hindi gagamitin nang tama ang oras, puwedeng ma-stress nang sobra o ma-burnout. Kaya kailangan ding alagaan ang physical at emotional health, hindi lang ang school requirements.

ANS2 — Deeper

EN

This paragraph reminds me that Jehovah is reasonable. Even though no scripture is cited here, the point is caring and practical: Jehovah does not want us to burn ourselves out. He wants us to serve him with balance, not with a schedule that slowly damages our health and joy.

TL

Ipinaaalala nito na makatuwiran si Jehova. Kahit walang binanggit na teksto rito, practical at caring ang punto: ayaw ni Jehovah na ma-burnout tayo. Gusto niyang maglingkod tayo nang balanced, hindi sa schedule na unti-unting sumisira sa health at joy natin.

12. What activities will you need to include as you make "the best use of your time"? (Ephesians 5:15, 16)

12. Ano ang mga kailangan mong isama sa schedule para magamit mo "sa pinakamabuting paraan ang oras [mo]"? (Efeso 5:15, 16)

ANS1 — Direct

EN

Ephesians 5:15, 16 says to make the best use of time. A student needs time for classes, but even more, for spiritual needs. He also needs wholesome association, family, possible work or family responsibilities, rest, and moderate exercise. Psalm 133:1, Matthew 6:33, Ecclesiastes 4:6, and 1 Timothy 4:8 show that balance matters. (Ephesians 5:15, 16; Psalm 133:1; Proverbs 18:1; Matthew 6:33; Ecclesiastes 4:6; 1 Timothy 4:8)

TL

Sinasabi ng Efeso 5:15, 16 na gamitin sa pinakamabuting paraan ang oras. Kailangan ng estudyante ng panahon para sa classes, pero mas mahalaga ang spiritual needs. Kailangan din niya ng wholesome association, family time, posibleng trabaho o responsibilidad, pahinga, at exercise. (Efeso 5:15, 16; Awit 133:1; Kawikaan 18:1; Mateo 6:33; Eclesiastes 4:6; 1 Timoteo 4:8)

ANS2 — Deeper

EN

Matthew 6:33 helps me see that time management is really priority management. Jehovah does not ask us to ignore health, family, or work, but he teaches us what comes first. His way is balanced. Ecclesiastes 4:6 shows that a peaceful handful is better than overloading ourselves and losing calm.

TL

Tinutulungan ako ng Mateo 6:33 na makita na ang time management ay priority management. Hindi sinasabi ni Jehova na balewalain ang health, family, o work, pero tinuturuan niya tayo kung ano ang una. Balanced ang paraan niya. Ipinapakita ng Eclesiastes 4:6 na mas mabuti ang kapayapaan kaysa sobrang daming ginagawa.

13. What can you do in order to make the best use of your time?

13. Ano ang puwede mong gawin para magamit mo sa pinakamabuting paraan ang oras mo?

ANS1 — Direct

EN

Before beginning additional education, a student should create a realistic schedule. Proverbs 21:5 says that planning leads to success. He can review how he managed time in previous school years and ask what adjustments are needed now. The goal is to control time instead of allowing time and deadlines to control him. (Proverbs 21:5)

TL

Bago magsimula sa karagdagang edukasyon, dapat gumawa ng realistic schedule. Sinasabi ng Kawikaan 21:5 na nagtatagumpay ang nagpaplano. Puwede niyang balikan kung paano niya ginamit ang oras noong dating school years at itanong kung anong adjustments ang kailangan ngayon. Ang goal ay kontrolin ang oras, hindi magpakontrol sa oras at deadlines. (Kawikaan 21:5)

ANS2 — Deeper

EN

Proverbs 21:5 shows Jehovah's wisdom in advance planning. He knows that a good schedule can protect peace, spiritual routine, and health. That makes planning feel spiritual, not just practical. When I plan well, I am cooperating with Jehovah's wise direction before stress builds up.

TL

Ipinapakita ng Kawikaan 21:5 ang karunungan ni Jehova sa advance planning. Alam niyang ang maayos na schedule ay makakaprotekta sa peace, spiritual routine, at health. Kaya ang planning ay spiritual din, hindi lang practical. Kapag nagpa-plano ako nang mabuti, sumusunod ako sa wise direction ni Jehovah.

14. What questions might help you to examine yourself?

14. Anong mga tanong ang puwede mong pag-isipan para masuri ang paggamit mo ng oras?

ANS1 — Direct

EN

A student can ask: Does my schedule allow sufficient rest and exercise? Do I still have time with fellow believers? Can I complete homework on time? If not, is it because I have too much to do or because I procrastinate? Would family and friends agree with my assessment? He may also seek advice from good time managers. (Proverbs 11:14)

TL

Puwedeng itanong ng estudyante: May sapat ba akong pahinga at exercise? May panahon pa ba ako para makasama ang mga kapatid? Natatapos ko ba ang homework on time? Kung hindi, sobra ba ang ginagawa ko o nagpapaliban lang ako? Sang-ayon ba ang family at friends sa assessment ko? Puwede rin siyang humingi ng payo. (Kawikaan 11:14)

ANS2 — Deeper

EN

Proverbs 11:14 teaches me that Jehovah often helps us through counsel. Time problems can be hard to see honestly, so mature advice can protect us from self-deception. That draws me closer to Jehovah because his guidance is not vague; he provides people who can help us make practical adjustments.

TL

Itinuturo ng Kawikaan 11:14 na madalas tayong tinutulungan ni Jehova sa pamamagitan ng payo. Mahirap makita nang honest ang time problems, kaya makakatulong ang mature advice. Mas napapalapit ako kay Jehova dahil practical ang tulong niya; gumagamit siya ng mga taong makakatulong sa adjustments.

Keep "Walking With the Wise" / Patuloy na "Lumakad na Kasama ng Marurunong"

15. As you are getting additional education, what challenge might you face?

15. Habang kumukuha ka ng karagdagang edukasyon, anong hamon ang puwedeng mapaharap sa iyo?

ANS1 — Direct

EN

The challenge is association. Classmates may invite the student into their social life, and he may feel he has much in common with them. But he does not share their values. First Corinthians 15:33 warns that bad associations spoil useful habits. The more time he spends with classmates, the more their thinking may affect him. (1 Corinthians 15:33)

TL

Ang hamon ay pakikisama. Baka yayain ng classmates ang estudyante sa social life nila, at baka maramdaman niyang marami silang pagkakapareho. Pero hindi sila pareho ng values. Nagbababala ang 1 Corinto 15:33 na sinisira ng masasamang kasama ang mabubuting ugali. Habang mas madalas silang kasama, mas maaapektuhan ang thinking niya. (1 Corinto 15:33)

ANS2 — Deeper

EN

First Corinthians 15:33 shows that Jehovah understands how influence works. He is not saying we cannot be kind to classmates; he is warning us not to let them shape our heart. Jehovah's counsel protects our values before bad speech, entertainment, or attitudes start to feel normal.

TL

Ipinapakita ng 1 Corinto 15:33 na naiintindihan ni Jehova kung paano gumagana ang influence. Hindi niya sinasabing hindi tayo magiging mabait sa classmates; binabalaan niya tayo na huwag hayaang hubugin nila ang puso natin. Pinoprotektahan ng payo ni Jehova ang values natin.

16. What does "walking with the wise" mean? (Proverbs 13:20)

16. Ano ang itinuturo sa atin ng Kawikaan 13:20?

ANS1 — Direct

EN

Proverbs 13:20 teaches that the people we choose to spend time with influence us either for good or for bad. Walking with the wise means choosing close friends who truly serve Jehovah and have our best interests at heart. Psalm 101:6, 7 and Psalm 119:63 point us toward loyal, spiritually minded companions. (Proverbs 13:20; Psalm 101:6, 7; Psalm 119:63)

TL

Itinuturo ng Kawikaan 13:20 na ang mga pinipili nating kasama ay nakakaimpluwensiya sa atin, mabuti man o masama. Ang paglakad kasama ng marurunong ay pagpili ng malalapít na kaibigang naglilingkod kay Jehova at gusto ang makakabuti sa atin. Ipinapakita ng Awit 101:6, 7 at Awit 119:63 ang halaga ng tapat at spiritual na kasama. (Kawikaan 13:20; Awit 101:6, 7; Awit 119:63)

ANS2 — Deeper

EN

Proverbs 13:20 shows Jehovah's loving realism: friendship forms us. He does not leave us guessing about who should be close to us. Psalm 119:63 helps me see that friends who fear Jehovah are a gift. They make it easier to keep walking in the same direction spiritually.

TL

Ipinapakita ng Kawikaan 13:20 ang maibiging realism ni Jehova: hinuhubog tayo ng friendship. Hindi niya tayo hinahayaang manghula kung sino ang dapat maging close sa atin. Ipinapakita ng Awit 119:63 na regalo ang mga kaibigang natatakot kay Jehova.

17. Ano ang puwede mong gawin para maiwasan ang masasamang kasama?

EN

He should review how he handled association in previous school years, make needed adjustments, and remain determined to walk with the wise and avoid those who do not love Jehovah.

TL

Bago magsimula, dapat magdesisyon ang estudyante kung anong limits ang itatakda niya sa classmates. Puwede siyang maging mabait pero hindi kailangang maging close friend o sumama pagkatapos ng klase. Dapat niyang balikan ang dating school years, gumawa ng adjustments, at manatiling determinadong lumakad kasama ng marurunong.

ANS2 — Deeper

EN

This paragraph helps me see that boundaries are not unkind; they are spiritual protection. Since no scripture is cited in the paragraph, the application stays simple: Jehovah wants us to be friendly, but he also wants our closest ties to strengthen our love for him, not weaken it.

TL

Tinutulungan ako nito na makita na ang boundaries ay hindi pagiging unkind; spiritual protection ito. Dahil walang binanggit na teksto sa paragraph, simple ang application: gusto ni Jehova na maging friendly tayo, pero ang closest ties natin ay dapat nagpapatibay ng pag-ibig natin sa kaniya.

18. Anong mga tanong ang puwede mong pag-isipan? (Tingnan din ang larawan.)

Tinutulungan siya ng Awit 1:1 na iwasan ang landas ng makakapinsala spiritually. Kung may negative changes, dapat kumilos agad. (Awit 1:1)

EN

Psalm 1:1 shows that Jehovah cares about direction. Association can slowly move us onto a path, even before we notice the destination. I appreciate that the paragraph also mentions witnessing to classmates. We can be kind and helpful to them without making them our closest friends.
