> *<sub>**1-2.** What happened as Jesus was teaching in Capernaum?</sub>*  
> *<sub>**1-2.** Ano ang nangyari noong nagtuturo si Jesus sa Capernaum?</sub>*

**ANS1 - Direct (EN):** While Jesus was teaching in Capernaum, many disciples were shocked by his words about eating his flesh and drinking his blood. They had previously been impressed by his works and even recognized him as the Prophet who was to come. But when they faced a test of faith, they were offended and stopped following him. (**John 6:60; John 6:53, 54; John 6:14**)

**ANS1 - Direct (TL):** Noong nagtuturo si Jesus sa Capernaum, marami sa mga alagad niya ang nagulat sa sinabi niya tungkol sa pagkain ng kaniyang laman at pag-inom ng kaniyang dugo. Dati, hanga sila sa ginawa ni Jesus at kinilala pa siyang Propeta na darating. Pero nang masubok ang pananampalataya nila, hindi nila ito natanggap at tumigil sila sa pagsunod. (**Juan 6:60; Juan 6:53, 54; Juan 6:14**)

**ANS2 - Deeper (EN):** This teaches me that faith based only on excitement can weaken when something is hard to understand. Those disciples had seen powerful evidence, but offense replaced loyalty. Jehovah wants our faith to go deeper than emotion. When I do not fully understand something, that is when I need humility, patience, and trust.

**ANS2 - Deeper (TL):** Itinuturo nito na ang pananampalatayang nakabase lang sa excitement ay puwedeng humina kapag may hindi maintindihan. Nakakita na ang mga alagad na iyon ng matibay na ebidensiya, pero napalitan ng pagkatisod ang loyalty nila. Gusto ni Jehova na mas malalim ang faith natin kaysa emotion.

**ANS2 - Deeper (TL):** Tinutulungan ako ng sagot ni Pedro na makita kung ano ang loyalty kapag hindi pa kumpleto ang understanding. Hindi niya sinabing, "Naiintindihan namin ang lahat." > *<sub>**5.** Illustrate why our faith needs to be built on a strong foundation. (Colossians 1:23)</sub>*

**ANS1 - Direct (EN):** Our faith needs a strong foundation because tests of faith can shake us, just as earthquakes shake buildings. A building in an earthquake-prone area needs a solid foundation to survive. Similarly, if we keep strengthening the foundation of our faith, we can remain steadfast and loyal when trials come. (**1 Corinthians 15:58; Colossians 1:23**)

**ANS1 - Direct (TL):** Kailangang matibay ang pundasyon ng pananampalataya natin dahil puwede tayong mayanig ng mga pagsubok, gaya ng building na nayayanig ng lindol. Para hindi gumuho ang building, kailangan nito ng matibay na pundasyon. Sa katulad na paraan, kung patuloy nating pinatitibay ang faith natin, mananatili tayong matatag at tapat kapag may pagsubok. (**1 Corinto 15:58; Colosas 1:23**)

**ANS2 - Deeper (EN):** Colossians 1:23 teaches me that faith must be stable, not temporary. Jehovah is loving because he tells us to strengthen the foundation before the earthquake comes. Spiritual preparation is not fear; it is wisdom. When my faith is built on solid truths, trials may shake me emotionally, but they do not have to move me away from Jehovah.

**ANS2 - Deeper (TL):** Itinuturo ng Colosas 1:23 na ang pananampalataya ay dapat matatag, hindi pansamantala. Maibigin si Jehova dahil sinasabi niyang patibayin ang pundasyon bago dumating ang lindol. Ang spiritual preparation ay hindi takot; wisdom ito. Kapag matibay ang foundation ng faith ko, puwede akong mayanig emotionally pero hindi kailangang lumayo kay Jehova.

**ANS2 - Deeper (TL):** Tinutulungan ako ng Apocalipsis 4:11 na iugnay ang paglalang sa pagtitiwala. Kung nilalang ni Jehova ang lahat, walang pagsubok na mas malaki kaysa kapangyarihan niya. Kahit hindi agad natin makita ang solusyon, mananatili tayong tapat kung kumbinsido tayo na si Jehova ang Maylalang, na mula sa kaniya ang Bibliya, at na ang mga Saksi ni Jehova ang bayan niya. (**2 Timoteo 3:1, 13**)

**ANS2 - Deeper (EN):** Second Timothy 3:1, 13 reminds me that difficult times should not surprise us. Jehovah told us what the last days would be like. That warning is loving because it prepares us emotionally. When a hard situation comes, I can say what Peter said: "Whom shall we go away to?" There is no better place than with Jehovah.

**ANS2 - Deeper (TL):** Ipinaaalala ng 2 Timoteo 3:1, 13 na hindi na tayo dapat magulat sa hirap ng mga huling araw. Sinabi na ito ni Jehova. Maibigin ang babala niya dahil inihahanda tayo emotionally. Kapag may mahirap na sitwasyon, masasabi natin gaya ni Pedro: "Kanino kami pupunta?" Wala nang mas mabuting lugar kaysa kay Jehova.

## How Can These Three Truths Help Us When Tests Arise? / Kapag May Pinagdadaanan Tayo, Paano Makakatulong sa Atin ang mga Katotohanang Ito?

> *<sub>Jehovah is the Creator</sub>*  
> *<sub>Si Jehova ang Maylalang</sub>*

**ANS1 - Direct (EN):** Knowing that Jehovah is the Creator helps us remain loyal because it proves he is worthy of our worship and powerful enough to help us. When we meditate on creation, we remember that Jehovah can deliver us from trials. That conviction protects us when Satan's world tries to make us doubt God's existence or care. (**Revelation 4:11; Hebrews 11:6**)

**ANS1 - Direct (TL):** Ang pagkaalam na si Jehova ang Maylalang ay tumutulong sa atin na manatiling tapat dahil pinatutunayan nito na karapat-dapat siyang sambahin at makapangyarihan siyang tumulong. Kapag binubulay-bulay natin ang creation, naaalala nating kaya ni Jehova na iligtas tayo sa pagsubok. Pinoprotektahan tayo ng conviction na iyan laban sa pagdududa. (**Apocalipsis 4:11; Hebreo 11:6**)

**ANS2 - Deeper (EN):** This truth draws me close to Jehovah because creation shows both power and personality. Revelation 4:11 reminds me that Jehovah made all things, and Hebrews 11:6 reminds me that he rewards those seeking him. So creation does not just tell me Jehovah is real; it helps me trust that he is powerful and caring.

**ANS2 - Deeper (TL):** Mas napapalapit ako kay Jehova dahil ipinapakita ng creation ang kapangyarihan at personalidad niya. Ipinaaalala ng Apocalipsis 4:11 na nilalang ni Jehova ang lahat, at ng Hebreo 11:6 na ginagantimpalaan niya ang humahanap sa kaniya. Kaya ang creation ay hindi lang ebidensiyang totoo siya; tumutulong din ito na magtiwala akong makapangyarihan at mapagmalasakit siya.

---

> *<sub>The Bible is God's Word</sub>*  
> *<sub>Mula sa Diyos ang Bibliya</sub>*

**ANS1 - Direct (EN):** Knowing that the Bible is God's Word helps us remain loyal because it gives us Jehovah's direction and answers life's important questions. Its accuracy, harmony, timeless advice, and fulfilled prophecy strengthen our confidence. When tests come, we can rely on the Bible as Jehovah's message, not merely human wisdom. (**2 Timothy 3:16, 17; Joshua 23:14**)

**ANS1 - Direct (TL):** Ang pagkaalam na mula sa Diyos ang Bibliya ay tumutulong sa atin na manatiling tapat dahil nagbibigay ito ng direksiyon ni Jehova at sagot sa mahahalagang tanong sa buhay. Pinapatibay ng accuracy, harmony, practical na payo, at natupad na hula ang tiwala natin. Kapag may pagsubok, makakaasa tayo sa Bibliya bilang mensahe ni Jehova. (**2 Timoteo 3:16, 17; Josue 23:14**)

**ANS2 - Deeper (EN):** This truth makes Jehovah feel close because the Bible is how he speaks to us. Second Timothy 3:16, 17 shows that his words can correct and equip us. Jehovah does not leave us guessing during tests of faith. He gives counsel that is reliable enough to build our life on.

**ANS2 - Deeper (TL):** Pinapalapit ako ng katotohanang ito kay Jehova dahil ang Bibliya ang paraan niya ng pakikipag-usap sa atin. Ipinapakita ng 2 Timoteo 3:16, 17 na ang kaniyang salita ay nagtutuwid at nagsasanay. Hindi tayo hinahayaan ni Jehova na manghula kapag may test of faith; nagbibigay siya ng maaasahang payo.

---

> *<sub>Jehovah is backing his people today</sub>*  
> *<sub>Ang mga Saksi ni Jehova ang bayan ng Diyos</sub>*

**ANS1 - Direct (EN):** Knowing that Jehovah is backing his people today helps us remain loyal because we see the evidence: Jehovah's Witnesses conform to Bible standards, preach the Kingdom worldwide, make Jehovah's name known, and show love and unity. These signs help us stay convinced that Jehovah is using his people to accomplish his will. (**Matthew 7:20, 21; Matthew 24:14; Isaiah 43:10; John 13:34, 35**)

**ANS1 - Direct (TL):** Ang pagkaalam na sinusuportahan ni Jehova ang bayan niya ngayon ay tumutulong sa atin na manatiling tapat dahil nakikita natin ang ebidensiya: sinusunod ng mga Saksi ni Jehova ang pamantayan ng Bibliya, ipinapangaral ang Kaharian sa buong lupa, ipinapakilala ang pangalan ni Jehova, at nagpapakita ng pag-ibig at pagkakaisa. (**Mateo 7:20, 21; Mateo 24:14; Isaias 43:10; Juan 13:34, 35**)

**ANS2 - Deeper (EN):** This truth gives me stability when something in the congregation or organization is hard to understand. Jehovah's backing is seen in the overall fruit, not in whether I understand every detail immediately. That helps me stay humble, patient, and loyal while I keep looking at the evidence of Jehovah's blessing.

**ANS2 - Deeper (TL):** Pinapatatag ako ng katotohanang ito kapag may bagay sa kongregasyon o organisasyon na hindi ko agad maintindihan. Nakikita ang suporta ni Jehova sa kabuuang bunga, hindi sa kung naiintindihan ko agad ang bawat detalye. Tinutulungan ako nitong manatiling humble, patient, at loyal habang tinitingnan ang ebidensiya ng pagpapala ni Jehova.
