# Respect the Decisions of Others - Bilingual (EN / TL)
**The Watchtower - May 2026 | August 3-9, 2026**  
*Song 113 & 89 | "Do not pass judgment on differing opinions." - Romans 14:1*  
*Awit Blg. 113 & 89 | "Huwag ninyo siyang hatulan dahil iba ang opinyon niya." - Roma 14:1*

> *Note: Every scripture below is taken only from the paragraph that the question covers. ANS1 = direct answer from the paragraph; ANS2 = a deeper spiritual insight drawn from that same paragraph.*

---

> *<sub>**1-2.** Why do our personal decisions sometimes differ from those of other Christians?</sub>*  
> *<sub>**1-2.** Bakit may mga desisyon tayo na iba sa desisyon ng mga kapatid?</sub>*

**ANS1 - Direct (EN):** Our personal decisions sometimes differ because we are not all the same. Each Christian has his own viewpoint, and our thinking is affected by our culture, family background, and personal experiences. So even faithful Christians may make different choices in personal matters. But those differences should not damage congregation peace and unity. (**Ephesians 4:3**)

**ANS1 - Direct (TL):** Minsan iba-iba ang personal na desisyon natin dahil magkakaiba tayo. May kani-kaniya tayong pananaw, at naaapektuhan tayo ng kultura, pamilya, at mga karanasan natin. Kaya kahit tapat na Kristiyano, puwedeng magkaiba ang piliin sa personal na bagay. Pero hindi dapat masira ng pagkakaibang iyon ang kapayapaan at pagkakaisa ng kongregasyon. (**Efeso 4:3**)

**ANS2 - Deeper (EN):** Ephesians 4:3 teaches me that Jehovah values unity even when personalities and backgrounds differ. He does not expect every servant to have the same preference or life experience. That draws me closer to Jehovah because his congregation has room for variety, but not for pride or division. Unity is something we actively protect.

**ANS2 - Deeper (TL):** Itinuturo ng Efeso 4:3 na mahalaga kay Jehova ang pagkakaisa kahit magkakaiba ang personalidad at background natin. Hindi niya inaasahang pare-pareho ang preference o experience ng mga lingkod niya. Mas napapalapit ako kay Jehova dahil may lugar sa kongregasyon niya ang variety, pero walang lugar ang pride at division.

---

> *<sub>**3.** What might cause us to judge a fellow Christian for a decision he has made?</sub>*  
> *<sub>**3.** Bakit posibleng mahirapan tayong igalang ang desisyon ng isang kapatid?</sub>*

**ANS1 - Direct (EN):** We might judge a fellow Christian because we love him and fear that his decision could hurt him spiritually. Proverbs 17:17 shows that real friends care. So our motive may be good. But even with good motives, we may wrongly feel that we must convince him, criticize his decision, or talk about our concerns to others.

**ANS1 - Direct (TL):** Posibleng mahusgahan natin ang kapatid dahil mahal natin siya at nag-aalala tayo na baka makasira sa kaniya spiritually ang desisyon niya. Ipinapakita ng Kawikaan 17:17 na nagmamalasakit ang tunay na kaibigan. Kaya maaaring maganda ang motive natin. Pero kahit maganda ang motive, baka mali nating isipin na kailangan natin siyang kumbinsihin o punahin sa iba.

**ANS2 - Deeper (EN):** Proverbs 17:17 reminds me that love is loyal, but love also needs wisdom. Jehovah does not want concern to turn into control. This helps me ask, "Am I truly helping my brother, or am I trying to make his decision match mine?" Love should protect, not pressure.

**ANS2 - Deeper (TL):** Ipinaaalala ng Kawikaan 17:17 na loyal ang pag-ibig, pero kailangan din nitong samahan ng karunungan. Ayaw ni Jehova na ang concern ay maging control. Kaya magandang itanong, "Tinutulungan ko ba talaga ang kapatid, o pinipilit ko lang siyang gayahin ang desisyon ko?" Ang pag-ibig ay proteksiyon, hindi pressure.

---

> *<sub>**4-5.** What should we do if a fellow Christian decides to do something that differs from what we would have done?</sub>*  
> *<sub>**4-5.** Ano ang dapat nating gawin kung hindi tayo sang-ayon sa desisyon ng isang kapatid?</sub>*

**ANS1 - Direct (EN):** If a Christian is about to violate a Bible command, love should move us to help him correct his thinking. But if his decision does not break God's law and is simply different from what we would do, we should not judge him. Romans 14:1 says not to pass judgment on differing opinions. (**Proverbs 27:5, 6; Romans 14:1**)

**ANS1 - Direct (TL):** Kung may malalabag na utos sa Bibliya ang isang kapatid, dapat tayong pakilusin ng pag-ibig na tulungan siyang ituwid ang kaisipan niya. Pero kung wala naman siyang nilalabag na utos ng Diyos at iba lang ang desisyon niya sa atin, hindi natin siya dapat hatulan. Sinasabi ng Roma 14:1 na huwag humatol dahil lang iba ang opinyon niya. (**Kawikaan 27:5, 6; Roma 14:1**)

**ANS2 - Deeper (EN):** Romans 14:1 teaches me that Jehovah makes a difference between Bible commands and personal opinions. That protects the peace of the congregation. Jehovah gives clear standards, but he does not make rules for every preference. Respecting that helps me imitate his reasonableness.

**ANS2 - Deeper (TL):** Itinuturo ng Roma 14:1 na may pagkakaiba sa paningin ni Jehova ang utos sa Bibliya at personal na opinyon. Pinoprotektahan nito ang kapayapaan ng kongregasyon. Nagbibigay si Jehova ng malinaw na pamantayan, pero hindi siya gumagawa ng rules para sa bawat preference. Kapag iginagalang ko iyan, natutularan ko ang pagiging makatuwiran niya.

## What Personal Decisions Could Cause Us to Judge Others? / Personal na mga Desisyon ng Iba na Posible Nating Mahusgahan

> *<sub>**6-7.** Describe scenarios in which we might judge others for their decisions.</sub>*  
> *<sub>**6-7.** Magbigay ng mga senaryo kung saan posible nating mahusgahan ang desisyon ng iba.</sub>*

**ANS1 - Direct (EN):** The paragraphs give several examples: drinking alcohol at a gathering, choosing medical treatment, attending a funeral in a church, wearing a beard or dress slacks in formal theocratic settings, and seeking additional education. In each case, a person's background or past experience could make him feel strongly and lead him to judge another Christian's personal decision.

**ANS1 - Direct (TL):** Binanggit ng mga paragraph ang ilang halimbawa: pag-inom ng alak sa gathering, pagpili ng paraan ng pagpapagamot, pagpunta sa libing sa simbahan, pagpapabalbas o pagsusuot ng slacks sa formal na teokratikong gawain, at pagkuha ng karagdagang edukasyon. Sa bawat sitwasyon, puwedeng maapektuhan ng background o dating karanasan ang pananaw ng isang kapatid.

**ANS2 - Deeper (EN):** These examples help me see that a strong feeling is not always the same as a Bible rule. Since these paragraphs cite no scriptures, I would keep the lesson simple: Jehovah knows our backgrounds can affect us, but he wants us to be careful not to turn personal pain or preference into a standard for everyone else.

**ANS2 - Deeper (TL):** Tinutulungan ako ng mga halimbawang ito na makita na hindi porke malakas ang feeling ko, rule na iyon sa Bibliya. Dahil walang binanggit na teksto ang paragraphs, simple ang lesson: alam ni Jehova na naaapektuhan tayo ng background natin, pero ayaw niyang gawing standard para sa lahat ang personal pain o preference natin.

> *<sub>**8.** (a) What could cause a parent to become judgmental of other parents? (b) How might our having a critical attitude toward others affect the congregation?</sub>*  
> *<sub>**8.** (a) Sa anong sitwasyon posibleng mahusgahan ng isang magulang ang ibang mga magulang? (b) Ano ang puwedeng maging epekto sa kongregasyon kung hinuhusgahan natin ang ginagawa ng mga kapatid?</sub>*

**ANS1 - Direct (EN):** A parent might judge other parents if they seem more permissive, such as allowing a later curfew, nonviolent video games, or a cell phone at a younger age. Ephesians 6:4 shows parents must raise children in Jehovah's discipline, but parents may apply that responsibility differently. A critical attitude can make our opinions feel more important than congregation unity. (**Ephesians 6:4**)

**ANS1 - Direct (TL):** Puwedeng mahusgahan ng isang magulang ang ibang magulang kung parang mas maluwag sila, halimbawa sa curfew, nonviolent video games, o cellphone habang bata pa ang anak. Ipinapakita ng Efeso 6:4 na kailangang palakihin ng magulang ang anak ayon sa patnubay ni Jehova, pero puwedeng magkaiba ang paraan ng pag-a-apply. Ang pagiging critical ay puwedeng makasira sa pagkakaisa. (**Efeso 6:4**)

**ANS2 - Deeper (EN):** Ephesians 6:4 reminds me that Jehovah gives parents a serious assignment, but he does not tell every family to make identical house rules. That helps me respect another parent's role before Jehovah. I can be firm with my own child without becoming negative toward parents who make different personal decisions.

**ANS2 - Deeper (TL):** Ipinaaalala ng Efeso 6:4 na seryoso ang atas na ibinigay ni Jehova sa mga magulang, pero hindi niya sinabing pare-pareho dapat ang house rules ng lahat. Tinutulungan ako nito na respetuhin ang role ng ibang magulang sa harap ni Jehova. Puwede akong maging firm sa anak ko nang hindi nagiging negative sa ibang magulang.

> *<sub>**9.** What should we remember? (See also picture.)</sub>*  
> *<sub>**9.** Ano ang dapat nating tandaan? (Tingnan din ang larawan.)</sub>*

**ANS1 - Direct (EN):** We should remember that different personal decisions do not always mean one person is right and the other is wrong. Christians agree on Jehovah's standards, but we do not have to think alike in every personal matter. Like travelers choosing different routes to the same destination, Christians may make different choices while having the same goal: to please Jehovah. (**Romans 14:5; 2 Corinthians 13:11; Matthew 7:1; 1 Thessalonians 4:11**)

**ANS1 - Direct (TL):** Dapat nating tandaan na kapag magkaiba ang personal na desisyon ng dalawang Kristiyano, hindi ibig sabihin na tama ang isa at mali ang isa. Nagkakaisa tayo sa pamantayan ni Jehova, pero hindi kailangang pare-pareho ang pananaw natin sa lahat ng personal na bagay. Gaya ng iba't ibang ruta papunta sa iisang lugar, iisa ang goal natin: mapasaya si Jehova. (**Roma 14:5; 2 Corinto 13:11; Mateo 7:1; 1 Tesalonica 4:11**)

**ANS2 - Deeper (EN):** Matthew 7:1 and 1 Thessalonians 4:11 help me see that Jehovah wants me to respect the space he gives others. The route illustration is kind and practical. Jehovah is not asking everyone to have identical preferences; he is asking all of us to move toward the same destination, pleasing him.

**ANS2 - Deeper (TL):** Tinutulungan ako ng Mateo 7:1 at 1 Tesalonica 4:11 na makita na gusto ni Jehova na respetuhin ko ang space na ibinibigay niya sa iba. Practical ang illustration ng ruta. Hindi hinihiling ni Jehova na pare-pareho ang preferences natin; hinihiling niyang pare-pareho tayong patungo sa goal na mapasaya siya.

## Why Respect the Personal Decisions of Others? / Bakit Dapat Nating Igalang ang Personal na mga Desisyon ng Iba?

> *<sub>**10.** According to James 4:12, what do we not have the authority to do, and why?</sub>*  
> *<sub>**10.** Ayon sa Santiago 4:12, wala tayong awtoridad na gawin ang ano, at bakit?</sub>*

**ANS1 - Direct (EN):** We do not have the authority to judge others in personal matters. James 4:12 shows that Jehovah is the Lawgiver and Judge. He alone has the right to set standards and rules for life. Our brothers and sisters are accountable to Jehovah, not to us, so we should not criticize their personal decisions based on our own standards. (**James 4:12; Romans 14:10**)

**ANS1 - Direct (TL):** Wala tayong awtoridad na hatulan ang iba pagdating sa personal na bagay. Ipinapakita ng Santiago 4:12 na si Jehova ang Tagapagbigay-Batas at Hukom. Siya lang ang may karapatang magtakda ng pamantayan sa buhay. Sa kaniya mananagot ang mga kapatid, hindi sa atin, kaya hindi natin sila dapat punahin base sa sarili nating standard. (**Santiago 4:12; Roma 14:10**)

**ANS2 - Deeper (EN):** James 4:12 teaches me humility. Jehovah does not share his position as Lawgiver and Judge with me. That makes me feel safer too, because I would not want imperfect people judging every personal choice I make. Jehovah's authority is righteous, balanced, and loving.

**ANS2 - Deeper (TL):** Itinuturo ng Santiago 4:12 ang humility. Hindi ibinabahagi ni Jehova sa akin ang posisyon niya bilang Tagapagbigay-Batas at Hukom. Nakakapagpanatag din iyon, kasi ayoko ring husgahan ng imperfect na tao ang bawat personal choice ko. Ang awtoridad ni Jehova ay matuwid, balanced, at maibigin.

> *<sub>**11.** How can we contribute to the unity of the congregation? (See also picture.)</sub>*  
> *<sub>**11.** Ano ang puwede nating gawin para mapanatili ang pagkakaisa ng kongregasyon? (Tingnan din ang larawan.)</sub>*

**ANS1 - Direct (EN):** We contribute to unity by valuing peace more than our opinions and preferences. Jehovah wants unity, not uniformity. He created variety, so he does not want us to be copies of one another. Romans 14:19 shows that we should pursue the things that make for peace and build one another up. (**Romans 14:19**)

**ANS1 - Direct (TL):** Makakatulong tayo sa pagkakaisa kung mas papahalagahan natin ang kapayapaan kaysa sa sariling opinyon at preference. Gusto ni Jehova ng unity, hindi uniformity. Siya ang lumalang sa atin na magkakaiba, kaya ayaw niyang maging carbon copy tayo ng isa't isa. Sinasabi ng Roma 14:19 na itaguyod ang kapayapaan at patibayin ang isa't isa. (**Roma 14:19**)

**ANS2 - Deeper (EN):** Romans 14:19 shows what Jehovah wants us to pursue: peace and upbuilding. I like that the paragraph says Jehovah loves variety. That means respecting differences is not merely tolerating them; it reflects appreciation for the way Jehovah made his people. Unity becomes more beautiful because we are different.

**ANS2 - Deeper (TL):** Ipinapakita ng Roma 14:19 ang gusto ni Jehova na itaguyod natin: kapayapaan at pagpapatibay. Maganda rin na sinabi ng paragraph na gusto ni Jehova ang variety. Ibig sabihin, ang paggalang sa pagkakaiba ay hindi lang pagtitiis dito; pagpapahalaga ito sa paraan ng paglalang ni Jehova sa bayan niya.

## How to Respect the Viewpoints of Others / Paano Natin Maipapakitang Iginagalang Natin ang Desisyon ng Iba?

> *<sub>**12-13.** What should we keep in mind if we feel that someone has taken "a false step"? (Galatians 6:1; see also the box "When You Disagree.")</sub>*  
> *<sub>**12-13.** Ano ang dapat nating tandaan kung sa tingin natin, nakagawa ng "maling hakbang" ang isang kapatid? (Galacia 6:1; tingnan din ang kahong "Kapag Hindi Ka Sang-ayon.")</sub>*

**ANS1 - Direct (EN):** We should ask whether the person has truly taken "a false step" or simply a different step from what we would take. If he is violating a Bible command and we are qualified to help, Galatians 6:1 says to readjust him in a spirit of mildness. But if his decision is personal, we should not speak negatively or impose our opinion. (**Galatians 6:1; Romans 14:2-4; Galatians 6:5**)

**ANS1 - Direct (TL):** Dapat nating itanong kung talagang nakagawa siya ng "maling hakbang" o iba lang ang hakbang niya sa gagawin natin. Kung may nilalabag siyang utos sa Bibliya at kuwalipikado tayong tumulong, sinasabi ng Galacia 6:1 na ituwid siya sa mahinahong paraan. Pero kung personal decision lang iyon, hindi tayo dapat magsalita nang negatibo o ipilit ang opinyon natin. (**Galacia 6:1; Roma 14:2-4; Galacia 6:5**)

**ANS2 - Deeper (EN):** Galatians 6:1 shows Jehovah's balance. He wants help to be given when it is truly needed, but in mildness, not superiority. The restaurant illustration helps me too. Jehovah gives room for personal choice, and I should not act as if my preference is the only acceptable "meal."

**ANS2 - Deeper (TL):** Ipinapakita ng Galacia 6:1 ang balance ni Jehova. Gusto niyang tumulong tayo kapag kailangan talaga, pero sa mahinahong paraan, hindi may superiority. Nakakatulong din ang restaurant illustration. Nagbibigay si Jehova ng room para sa personal choice, kaya hindi ko dapat isipin na preference ko lang ang katanggap-tanggap.

> *<sub>**14.** How can you promote unity when making personal decisions? (1 Corinthians 8:12, 13)</sub>*  
> *<sub>**14.** Ano ang puwede mong gawin para hindi masira ang pagkakaisa sa kongregasyon kapag gumagawa ka ng personal na mga desisyon? (1 Corinto 8:12, 13)</sub>*

**ANS1 - Direct (EN):** We promote unity by trying not to stumble others. Even if a decision is lawful, 1 Corinthians 8:12, 13 and 1 Corinthians 10:23, 24 show that we should ask whether it is advantageous and loving. Rather than insisting on our rights, we may choose to put our preferences aside so we can maintain peace. (**1 Corinthians 8:12, 13; 1 Corinthians 10:23, 24; Romans 15:1; Romans 12:18**)

**ANS1 - Direct (TL):** Mapapanatili natin ang pagkakaisa kung sisikapin nating hindi makatisod sa iba. Kahit pinapayagan ang isang desisyon, ipinapakita ng 1 Corinto 8:12, 13 at 1 Corinto 10:23, 24 na dapat itanong kung kapaki-pakinabang at maibigin ba iyon. Imbes na ipilit ang karapatan natin, puwede nating isantabi ang preference para sa kapayapaan. (**1 Corinto 8:12, 13; 1 Corinto 10:23, 24; Roma 15:1; Roma 12:18**)

**ANS2 - Deeper (EN):** Romans 12:18 helps me see that peace may require initiative from me. Jehovah does not only want me to defend my freedom; he wants me to use freedom lovingly. That draws me closer to him because he teaches us to think beyond "Can I?" and ask, "Will this build peace?"

**ANS2 - Deeper (TL):** Tinutulungan ako ng Roma 12:18 na makita na minsan kailangan kong magkusa para sa kapayapaan. Hindi lang gusto ni Jehova na ipagtanggol ko ang freedom ko; gusto niyang gamitin ko ito nang may pag-ibig. Tinuturuan niya akong mag-isip lampas sa "Puwede ba?" at itanong, "Makakatulong ba ito sa kapayapaan?"

> *<sub>**15.** How can elders contribute to the unity of the congregation? (1 Corinthians 4:6)</sub>*  
> *<sub>**15.** Ano ang puwedeng gawin ng mga elder para maingatan ang pagkakaisa sa kongregasyon? (1 Corinto 4:6)</sub>*

**ANS1 - Direct (EN):** Elders contribute to unity by not making rules on personal matters and by not going beyond what is written. First Corinthians 4:6 shows that they should stay within the Bible and Bible-based publications. When giving advice, elders should not rely mainly on personal experience but should base their counsel on God's Word. (**1 Corinthians 4:6; Isaiah 48:17, 18**)

**ANS1 - Direct (TL):** Makakatulong ang mga elder sa pagkakaisa kung hindi sila gagawa ng rules sa personal na bagay at hindi lalampas sa nakasulat. Ipinapakita ng 1 Corinto 4:6 na dapat silang manatili sa sinasabi ng Bibliya at Bible-based publications. Kapag nagbibigay ng payo, hindi dapat personal experience ang pangunahing basehan kundi Salita ng Diyos. (**1 Corinto 4:6; Isaias 48:17, 18**)

**ANS2 - Deeper (EN):** Isaiah 48:17, 18 reminds me that Jehovah is the One teaching us to benefit ourselves. Elders best reflect Jehovah when they guide people to his Word rather than to personal opinions. This protects the flock because Jehovah's counsel is always wiser, safer, and kinder than human preference.

**ANS2 - Deeper (TL):** Ipinaaalala ng Isaias 48:17, 18 na si Jehova ang nagtuturo para makinabang tayo. Pinakamabuting natutularan ng elders si Jehova kapag inaakay nila ang mga kapatid sa Salita niya, hindi sa personal opinion. Pinoprotektahan nito ang kawan dahil mas marunong, mas ligtas, at mas maibigin ang payo ni Jehova.

> *<sub>**16.** How can an elder show respect for the decisions made by the body of elders?</sub>*  
> *<sub>**16.** Paano maipapakita ng isang elder na iginagalang niya ang mga desisyong ginawa ng lupon ng matatanda?</sub>*

**ANS1 - Direct (EN):** An elder shows respect by supporting the collective decision of the body of elders, even if his personal view was not adopted. The body prays for holy spirit, reviews Scriptural direction, and reaches a decision. Ephesians 5:17 shows the need to perceive Jehovah's will. An elder should also avoid reading more into direction than is really there. (**Ephesians 5:17**)

**ANS1 - Direct (TL):** Maipapakita ng isang elder ang respeto kung susuportahan niya ang desisyon ng lupon kahit hindi iyon ang personal view niya. Nananalangin ang lupon para sa banal na espiritu, sinusuri ang Scriptural direction, at saka nagdedesisyon. Ipinapakita ng Efeso 5:17 na kailangang unawain ang kalooban ni Jehova. Dapat ding iwasan ng elder na lagyan ng sariling interpretasyon ang tagubilin. (**Efeso 5:17**)

**ANS2 - Deeper (EN):** Ephesians 5:17 teaches that the issue is not whose opinion wins but what Jehovah's will is. That protects elders from pride and protects the congregation from confusion. I see Jehovah's wisdom in using a body of elders, not just one man's viewpoint, to care for the congregation.

**ANS2 - Deeper (TL):** Itinuturo ng Efeso 5:17 na hindi mahalaga kung kaninong opinyon ang nanalo; ang mahalaga ay ang kalooban ni Jehova. Pinoprotektahan nito ang elders laban sa pride at ang kongregasyon laban sa kalituhan. Nakikita ko ang karunungan ni Jehova sa paggamit ng lupon ng matatanda, hindi pananaw lang ng isang tao.

> *<sub>**17.** Ano ang magandang resulta kung igagalang natin ang mga desisyon ng isa't isa?</sub>*

**ANS1 - Direct (EN):** When we respect the decisions of others, our differences make the congregation warm and inviting rather than divided. We promote peace, avoid stumbling others, and enjoy a happy, united congregation. Psalm 133:1 shows how good and pleasant unity is, and Matthew 5:9 shows that peacemakers are happy and blessed. (**Psalm 133:1; Matthew 5:9**)

**ANS1 - Direct (TL):** Kapag iginagalang natin ang desisyon ng isa't isa, ang pagkakaiba-iba natin ay nagpapasaya sa kongregasyon imbes na magdulot ng pagkakabaha-bahagi. Itinataguyod natin ang kapayapaan, iniiwasang makatisod, at nagiging masaya at nagkakaisa ang kongregasyon. Ipinapakita ng Awit 133:1 at Mateo 5:9 ang ganda ng pagkakaisa at kapayapaan. (**Awit 133:1; Mateo 5:9**)

**ANS2 - Deeper (EN):** Psalm 133:1 helps me feel how Jehovah views unity: it is good and pleasant. He is not asking us to erase differences, but to handle them with peace. Matthew 5:9 makes it personal. When I respect others, I become a peacemaker, someone who reflects Jehovah's own peaceful spirit.

**ANS2 - Deeper (TL):** Tinutulungan ako ng Awit 133:1 na maramdaman kung paano tinitingnan ni Jehova ang unity: mabuti at kaaya-aya ito. Hindi niya hinihiling na burahin ang differences, kundi harapin ito nang may kapayapaan. Ginagawang personal ng Mateo 5:9 ang punto: kapag nirerespeto ko ang iba, nagiging tagapamayapa ako.

## How Can the Following Scriptures Help Us to Respect the Viewpoints and Decisions of Others? / Paano Makakatulong ang Sumusunod na mga Teksto Para Maigalang Natin ang Pananaw at Desisyon ng Iba?

> *<sub>James 4:12</sub>*  
> *<sub>Santiago 4:12</sub>*

**ANS1 - Direct (EN):** James 4:12 helps us remember that Jehovah alone is the Lawgiver and Judge. So we should not act as if our personal standards have the same authority as his standards. When a decision is personal and does not violate Bible law, the brother or sister is accountable to Jehovah, not to us.

**ANS1 - Direct (TL):** Tinutulungan tayo ng Santiago 4:12 na tandaan na si Jehova lang ang Tagapagbigay-Batas at Hukom. Kaya hindi natin dapat ituring na kapantay ng pamantayan niya ang personal standard natin. Kung personal ang desisyon at walang nilalabag na utos sa Bibliya, kay Jehova mananagot ang kapatid, hindi sa atin.

**ANS2 - Deeper (EN):** This verse makes me more humble and more peaceful. Jehovah's judgment is perfect, but mine is limited. When I leave judging to Jehovah, I show trust in his authority and I protect my relationship with my brothers and sisters.

**ANS2 - Deeper (TL):** Ginagawa akong mas humble at mas mapayapa ng tekstong ito. Perpekto ang paghatol ni Jehova, pero limitado ang akin. Kapag hinayaan kong si Jehova ang humatol, ipinapakita kong nagtitiwala ako sa awtoridad niya at pinoprotektahan ko ang kaugnayan ko sa mga kapatid.

---

> *<sub>Galatians 6:1</sub>*  
> *<sub>Galacia 6:1</sub>*

**ANS1 - Direct (EN):** Galatians 6:1 helps us distinguish between a real false step and a different personal choice. If someone is truly taking a false step and we are qualified to help, we should try to readjust him with mildness. But the verse does not give us permission to correct everyone whose decision differs from ours.

**ANS1 - Direct (TL):** Tinutulungan tayo ng Galacia 6:1 na makita ang pagkakaiba ng tunay na maling hakbang at personal choice lang na iba sa atin. Kung may ginagawa talagang maling hakbang ang isa at kuwalipikado tayong tumulong, dapat natin siyang tulungan nang may kahinahunan. Pero hindi ibig sabihin nito na puwede nating ituwid ang lahat ng hindi kapareho ng desisyon natin.

**ANS2 - Deeper (EN):** Galatians 6:1 shows Jehovah's tenderness. Even correction, when needed, must be mild. That teaches me that Jehovah is not harsh or controlling. If I ever need to help someone, my spirit should make Jehovah's care easier to feel, not harder.

**ANS2 - Deeper (TL):** Ipinapakita ng Galacia 6:1 ang lambing ni Jehova. Kahit correction, kapag kailangan, dapat mahinahon. Itinuturo nito na hindi harsh o controlling si Jehovah. Kung kailangan kong tumulong sa iba, dapat maramdaman nila sa approach ko ang malasakit ni Jehova.

> *<sub>1 Corinthians 4:6; 8:12, 13</sub>*  
> *<sub>1 Corinto 4:6; 8:12, 13</sub>*

**ANS1 - Direct (EN):** First Corinthians 4:6 helps us avoid going beyond what is written by making rules Jehovah has not made. First Corinthians 8:12, 13 helps us use our freedom with love, avoiding decisions that could stumble others. Together, these scriptures teach balance: do not impose personal rules, and do not use personal freedom selfishly.

**ANS1 - Direct (TL):** Tinutulungan tayo ng 1 Corinto 4:6 na huwag lumampas sa nakasulat sa pamamagitan ng paggawa ng rules na hindi ginawa ni Jehova. Tinutulungan naman tayo ng 1 Corinto 8:12, 13 na gamitin ang freedom nang may pag-ibig at iwasang makatisod. Magkasama, itinuturo ng mga tekstong ito ang balance: huwag mag-impose ng personal rules, at huwag gamitin ang freedom nang selfishly.

**ANS2 - Deeper (EN):** These verses show Jehovah's beautiful balance. He protects personal conscience by saying, "Do not go beyond what is written," and he protects congregation peace by teaching us not to stumble others. Jehovah's way is neither controlling nor careless; it is loving and wise.

**ANS2 - Deeper (TL):** Ipinapakita ng mga tekstong ito ang magandang balance ni Jehova. Pinoprotektahan niya ang personal conscience sa pagsasabing huwag lumampas sa nakasulat, at pinoprotektahan niya ang kapayapaan ng kongregasyon sa pagtuturong huwag makatisod. Hindi controlling o careless ang paraan ni Jehova; maibigin at marunong ito.
