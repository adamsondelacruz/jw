RequirementsREQUIREMENTS FOR FORMING A NEW CONGREGATION
S-50 par. 22. All the requirements listed below must be met before the circuit overseer submits to the
Service Department a recommendation to form a new congregation, whether the new congregation is being formed from a group or from a congregation.
S-50 par. 2(1)(1) A significant population—perhaps at least a few thousand individuals—is dependent
on the language of the proposed congregation and lives in close proximity to the
Kingdom Hall. Thus, it should not be necessary to travel long distances or to spend
many hours in search work. If the proposal involves forming a congregation in a foreign or an indigenous language, a significant population in the language community
should, in most cases, have little or no understanding of the language of an already
established congregation in the area.
S-50 par. 2(2)(2) Either there is a significant number of publishers who depend on the language for
their spiritual food or Bible students in the territory who depend on and prefer the
language are attending meetings and progressing toward baptism.
S-50 par. 2(3)(3) A solid core of spiritually mature publishers is fluent in the language.
S-50 par. 2(4)(4) There is a sufficient number of appointed men (including at least one elder) to provide
shepherding, handle meeting parts, and take the lead in the ministry. (Eph. 4:11, 12; 1
Pet. 5:1-3) In order to provide such spiritual oversight, the majority of the appointed
men should be fluent in the language.—1 Cor. 14:9.
S-50 par. 2(5)(5) The new congregation is able to conduct all weekly meetings without depending on
JW Stream. Local publishers are able to regularly care for meeting parts. With the
circuit overseer’s approval, the congregation may view recorded meetings or portions
of meetings on JW Stream on occasion. However, the congregation should not be
dependent on this arrangement.—sfl chap. 20 par. 25; chap. 24 pars. 13-14.
S-50 par. 2(6)(6) The study edition of The Watchtower and the Life and Ministry Meeting Workbook is
available in the language of the congregation or in another language in which the
majority of publishers are literate. (If these publications are not available in the language of the congregation, the brothers would participate in the field ministry, give
comments and talks, and pray in the language of the congregation but use publications in the other language during their meetings.)
S-50 par. 2(7)(7) A Kingdom Hall or another appropriate meeting place is available for the congregation’s use.

ConsiderationsADDITIONAL CONSIDERATIONS
S-50 par. 33. When considering whether to recommend a new congregation, circuit overseers and elders should keep in mind that each publisher or family head must decide which congregation
he and his family will attend. No one should be pressured to support a certain language field
or congregation.—1 Cor. 11:3; Gal. 6:4, 5; w17.05 p. 10 pars. 10-13.
S-50 par. 44. The examples listed below illustrate when a new congregation should not be recommended.
Of course, these are merely examples; each situation must be considered individually.
S-50 par. 4(1)(1) After one or two years as a group, there are no Bible students attending meetings or
progressing toward baptism. Although the majority of the publishers are fluent in the
language, only a few or perhaps none depend on it for their spiritual food.
S-50 par. 4(2)(2) A group holds a weekly Life and Ministry Meeting and a monthly public talk in the
language. Progressive Bible students are attending meetings. There is a solid core
of spiritually mature publishers who are fluent in the language. However, there is only
one appointed man, an elder, taking the lead in the group.
S-50 par. 4(3)(3) Progressive Bible students are attending a group’s weekly public talk. Although there
is a solid core of spiritually mature publishers, most are not fluent in the language and
none are dependent on the language. There is an insufficient number of appointed
men to provide spiritual oversight.
SubmittingSUBMITTING A RECOMMENDATION
S-50 par. 55. If it is decided to recommend that a new congregation be formed, the circuit overseer will
verify that the information listed below has been prepared accurately before sending it to the
Service Department for consideration. Leave the space for the congregation number blank
on all forms for the new congregation.
S-50 par. 5(1)(1) Congregation Application/Information (S-51) Form: Provide a completed form for
the new congregation and for each existing congregation involved in the proposal.
S-50 par. 5(2)(2) Coordinator of the Body of Elders/Secretary Change of Address (S-29) Form:
Provide a completed form for the new congregation and for any existing congregations in which the coordinator of the body of elders or secretary is changing.
S-50 par. 5(3)(3) Kingdom Hall Information (S-5) Form: Provide a completed form for the new congregation and for any existing congregations for which the Kingdom Hall information
is changing.
S-50 par. 5(4)(4) Periodical Request Form (M-202): Provide a completed form for the new congregation and for any existing congregations that are changing their magazine or Life and
Ministry Meeting Workbook request. Or each congregation may enter its new or revised requests on jw.org when the congregation is informed that the new congregation is approved.
S-50 par. 5(5)(5) Shipping Information (S-36) Form: Provide a completed form for the new congregation and for any existing congregations that are changing their shipping address.
Post office boxes should not be used if at all possible. The ship-to congregation in
the Kingdom Hall where the new congregation will be meeting should also complete
a copy of this form. All updated information, including the shipping address and all
congregations in the Kingdom Hall Literature Group, should be listed. This form is
required even if literature shipments will normally be sent to a Regional Literature Depot.
S-50 par. 5(6)(6) Territory Adjustment Request (S-6) Form: Provide a completed form for the new
congregation and for each existing congregation in which the territory boundaries are
affected, carefully following the steps outlined on the form. Since congregation names
