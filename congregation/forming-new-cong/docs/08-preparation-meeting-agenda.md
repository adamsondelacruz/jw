**Suggested duration:** 90 minutes<br>
**Purpose:** Prepare the Ashburton Tagalog Congregation to begin functioning on 1 November 2026 by identifying proposed role assignments, assigning preparation work and surfacing decisions that require formal approval or Branch direction.

<blockquote class="notice warning">

**Meeting boundary:** This is a preparation meeting, not a body-of-elders meeting. Record proposed names and action owners. Formal appointments, congregation decisions, shared-hall decisions and external legal or banking actions must be made or confirmed by the authorised brothers or body under current direction.

</blockquote>

## What this meeting should produce

- Proposed names or follow-up owners for every priority responsibility.
- Owners and deadlines for the first month of meetings, ministry, records and Kingdom Hall arrangements.
- A clear list of matters requiring formal approval or outside direction.
- One action owner for the Branch Accounting/Legal inquiry; no unauthorised charity, tax or banking action.

## Questions to work through

- ☐ Which roles absolutely need a named brother before the first meeting, and which can be covered temporarily?
- ☐ Do the proposed brothers have the availability, ability and eligibility indicated in the organisation chart?
- ☐ Who has formal authority to confirm each proposal, and by what date should that confirmation be obtained?
- ☐ How many field service groups should begin on 1 November, and who will care for each group?
- ☐ Who will prepare and check the first four weeks of meeting, ministry and Kingdom Hall rosters?
- ☐ What records, access, supplies or information must come from Ashburton or other former congregations?
- ☐ What must be agreed with the other bodies using the Kingdom Hall?
- ☐ Who will contact Branch Accounting/Legal for the legal-entity and bank-account instructions?

## Optional discussion — charity, tax, bank and accounts

<blockquote class="notice info">

Discuss only enough to assign preparation work. Do not create an entity, register with Charities Services, apply for an IRD number or open an account under an assumed name before Branch Accounting/Legal identifies the approved arrangement. Obtain the current S-27 before designing the accounting workflow.

</blockquote>

Suggested discussion only:

1. Who will ask Branch Accounting/Legal which legal entity, official account name, charity/IRD identifiers and bank process apply to congregation 3814?
2. Who will obtain the current S-27 and accounting tutorials?
3. Who can prepare—but not yet submit—the likely bank documents, identity evidence and draft minutes?
4. If the Branch directs a separate registration, who will coordinate the approved governing documents, officers and statutory calendar?
