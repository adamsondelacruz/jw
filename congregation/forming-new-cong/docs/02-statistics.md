## Calculations

| Calculation | Result | Formula | Caution |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Active rate | **88.6 percent** | `31 / 35 × 100` | 35 is the total listed population, not automatically the S-51 Regular field. |
| Active publishers per appointed brother | **4.4 publishers per appointed brother** | `31 / (5 + 2)` | A ratio does not prove language fluency or availability. |
| Bible studies per active publisher | **1.48 studies per active publisher** | `46 / 31` | The numerator and denominator come from different reporting dates. |
| Progressive share of reported studies | **73.9 percent** | `34 / 46 × 100` | Use only after confirming the two figures cover the same reporting period. |
| Publisher growth from June representation to August list | **6.1 percent** | `(35 - 33) / 33 × 100` | Compares represented/listed totals, not necessarily average active publishers. |

**Population evidence:** the 1,605 census figure is for Filipino ethnicity in Ashburton District; the 2.17% figure is language ability; the approximately 3,000 figure is an informed local estimate for the wider Mid Canterbury area. They are useful together only when their different measures and geographies remain explicit.
