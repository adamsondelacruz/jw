## The short version

Before 1 November, complete the announcement, leadership, JW Hub, accounting, shared-hall, meeting and ministry setup. On or shortly after 1 November, transfer people and records, confirm pioneers and appointed brothers, and begin congregation records. After November, submit the first field service report and operate the monthly accounting and compliance routines. Charity registration is conditional on branch direction; bank-account preparation may begin now, but the legal account holder must be confirmed first.

Use the [persistent master checklist](../02-checklist.html) to tick these tasks and save notes. This page is the meeting-friendly launch view; the branch letter and current official direction remain controlling.

## New Zealand official references

These are decision support for a structure approved by the branch—not authority to create a separate entity independently.

- [Charities Services — About the Charities Register](https://www.charities.govt.nz/charities-in-new-zealand/the-charities-register/about-the-charities-register) — Confirms registration is voluntary and describes public information on the register.
- [Charities Services — Online registration application](https://register.charities.govt.nz/Home/RegisterOnline) — Lists the entity, rules, officers, activities and documents required if separate registration is directed.
- [Charities Services — Register search and processing notice](https://register.charities.govt.nz/AdvancedSearch) — Current service notice says registration applications may take up to 20 weeks.
- [Charities Services — Annual returns](https://www.charities.govt.nz/reporting-standards/annual-return) — Registered charities file an annual return within six months after balance date.
- [Inland Revenue — IRD number for a not-for-profit](https://www.ird.govt.nz/roles/not-for-profits-and-charities/before-you-start/ird-number) — Explains entity sequencing and that intended Charities Services applicants should not separately apply for an IRD number.
- [Companies Office — Charitable Trusts Register](https://www.ct-register.companiesoffice.govt.nz/) — Incorporation path only if that legal structure is directed.
- [BNZ — Opening an account for a group or society](https://www.bnz.co.nz/business-banking/support/opening-a-non-personal-account/group-or-society) — Illustrates current bank evidence, minutes, key-person ID and tax-residency requirements; use the selected bank’s own checklist.
- [DIA — AML/CFT customer due diligence](https://www.dia.govt.nz/AML-CFT-Frequently-Asked-Questions-for-DIA-Reporting-Entities) — Explains why banks identify the entity, beneficial owners/controllers and people acting for it.
- [Christian Congregation of Jehovah’s Witnesses of New Zealand — CC36365](https://register.charities.govt.nz/Charity/CC36365) — Public evidence that a nationwide entity is registered; it does not by itself establish the legal arrangement for congregation 3814.
