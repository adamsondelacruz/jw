Purpose: Prepare the Ashburton Tagalog Congregation to begin functioning on 1 November 2026 by identifying proposed role assignments, assigning preparation work and surfacing decisions that require formal approval or Branch direction.

What this meeting should produce

  • Proposed names or follow-up owners for every priority responsibility.
