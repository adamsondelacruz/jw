Important for S-51 point 6: proximity alone does not make a congregation “directly involved.” The form asks only for congregations:

  • whose territory boundaries will be affected; or
  • hosting the group being recommended.

Based on what we currently know, the likely entries are:

  1. Ashburton (1016) — the host congregation.
  2. Christchurch Tagalog–Christchurch (3756) — if its existing Tagalog territory will be divided or adjusted to form the Ashburton Tagalog congregation.

The other fourteen should not be entered merely because they fall inside the 100 km radius. Their inclusion depends on the proposed territory adjustment confirmed by the CO. I have therefore not filled point 6 in the PDF yet.
