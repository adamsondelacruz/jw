Extemporaneous Delivery Sample

Talk: How Can You Make a Good Name With God?
Purpose: Show how to use the full draft and v0 outline without reading word-for-word.

How to Use This

Do not memorize full sentences. Memorize:

  • the anchor sentence
  • the flow of thought
  • the illustration
  • the application question

Then speak naturally from the prompts.

Sample Section: Introduction

Anchor Sentence

Our name carries something, just like a fragrance can make an impression before we see the flower.

Flow

  1. Start with the garden/fragrance picture.
  2. Connect fragrance to reputation.
  3. Mention that people’s opinions can change.
  4. Raise the bigger question: What does my name mean to Jehovah?
  5. State the theme question.

v0 Prompt

Have you ever walked past a garden and noticed a pleasant fragrance before you even saw the flower? Hidden flower; fragrance already made impression.

How to Deliver It

Have you ever walked past a garden and noticed a pleasant fragrance before you even saw the flower?

Maybe you could not see the flower right away. It may have been small, or hidden among the leaves. But its fragrance had already reached you. It had already made an impression.

What Not to Do

Do not try to remember every word from the draft.

Instead of thinking:

What exact sentence comes next?

Think:

Flower hidden. Fragrance noticed. Name also carries something.

Next Prompt

In a similar way, our name carries something. Honest; kind; trusted; people remember unusual things; plate at gathering.

How to Deliver It

In a similar way, our name carries something.

When people hear our name, they may immediately think of certain qualities. Maybe they think, “That brother is dependable,” or “That sister is kind,” or “That person is honest.”

Of course, people can also remember unusual things about us. Sometimes we try for years to build a good reputation, and someone still remembers the one awkward moment when we dropped something or tripped while carrying a plate at a gathering.

Speaker Note

Use a slight smile on the light comment. Then quickly return to the serious point.

Next Prompt

But there is one Person whose view of our name matters more than anyone else’s: Jehovah.

How to Deliver It

But there is one Person whose view of our name matters more than anyone else’s.

That Person is Jehovah.

Pause.

Next Prompt

So today we will consider this important question: How can you make a good name with God? Not only longtime servants; new ones; still learning; can start now.

How to Deliver It

So today, we will consider this important question: How can you make a good name with God?

And that question is not only for those who have served Jehovah for many years. It is also for newer ones, for Bible students, and for those still learning. All of us can begin, or continue, building a good name with Jehovah now.

Sample Section: First Scripture

Anchor Sentence

A good name is more valuable than wealth because Jehovah’s approval lasts.

REIA

Read: Proverbs 22:1
Explain: A good name means a trusted reputation.
Illustrate: Human approval is like morning mist.
Apply: Ask, “What kind of name am I making with Jehovah?”

v0 Prompt

Please turn to Proverbs 22:1.

How to Deliver It

Let us see how the Bible describes the value of a good name. Please turn with me to Proverbs 22:1.

Read the scripture.

Pause.

v0 Prompt

A good name is more valuable than great wealth. People chase money; career; popularity; social media; coffee photo; coffee getting cold.

How to Deliver It

This verse says that a good name is more valuable than great wealth.

That is interesting because many people today spend so much energy trying to make a name for themselves. Some pursue money. Others pursue career success. Others pursue popularity.

And today, some try to make a name on social media. A person can spend twenty minutes arranging a photo of a cup of coffee so that strangers will think, “What a meaningful life.” Meanwhile, the coffee is getting cold.

Speaker Note

Use light humor here. Do not pause too long for a laugh. Keep moving.

v0 Prompt

Human reputation has value, but it is not the highest goal. People’s opinions change; praise today, forgotten tomorrow; morning mist.

How to Deliver It

Now, having a good reputation with people is not wrong. Christians should be known as honest, kind, and dependable.

But people’s opinions can change very quickly. Someone may be praised today and forgotten tomorrow. Human approval can be like morning mist. It may look impressive for a little while, but soon it disappears.

v0 Prompt

Jesus warned us to store up treasures in heaven. Matthew 6:19, 20; Jehovah’s approval; safest investment.

How to Deliver It

That is why Jesus warned us not to build our life around treasures that do not last. At Matthew 6:19, 20, he encouraged us to store up treasures in heaven.

In other words, Jehovah’s approval is the safest place to invest our life.

v0 Prompt

The question is not only, “Do people like me?” What kind of name am I making with Jehovah?

How to Deliver It

So the important question is not just, “Do people like me?”

The more important question is, “What kind of name am I making with Jehovah?”

Pause.

Practice Method

Use this method for the rest of the talk:

  1. Read one v0 prompt.
  2. Cover the draft.
  3. Say the thought in your own words.
  4. Check the full draft only if you lose the idea.
  5. Practice the transition into the next prompt.

Personal Delivery Rule

Never ask, “What exact words come next?”

Ask:

What is the next thought I want the audience to feel, understand, or do?
