Public Talk No. 55: How Can You Make a Good Name With God?
Use with: draft-talk-v3.md

Main memory path: Fragrance -> Remembered -> Know -> Obey -> Imitate -> Pray -> Act Now

One Sentence

A good name with Jehovah is worth more than any name people can give.

Delivery Rule

  • Do not read paragraphs.
  • See the cue.
  • Look up.
  • Say the thought naturally.
  • Return to the next cue.

Introduction (05) 2 min

Picture: garden fragrance before flower.

  • Hidden flower -> fragrance noticed.
  • Our name carries something.
  • Honest. Kind. Trusted.
  • Light humor: tripped carrying food.
  • Jehovah's view matters most.
  • Theme: How can you make a good name with God?
  • New ones / young ones / rebuilding habits -> can start now.

Bridge: What makes a name valuable?

A Good Name Is Valuable (07) 3 min

READ: Proverbs 22:1

  • Good name > wealth.
  • World chases name: money, career, education, popularity, social media.
  • Coffee photo -> coffee gets cold.
  • Human approval changes quickly.
  • Jehovah sees motive. Jehovah remembers.
  • Matthew 6:19, 20 -> safest investment: heaven.
  • Flower again: value not in being seen, but what it gives off.
  • Good name with Jehovah comes from inside.
  • Psalm 15:1, 2 -> integrity, right conduct, truth in heart.

Key line: What kind of name am I making with Jehovah?

Bridge: Why should we want that name?

Why Make a Good Name With God? (10) 5 min

READ: Malachi 3:16

  • Jehovah pays attention.
  • Jehovah listens.
  • Book of remembrance -> warm thought.
  • New ones: not speed, sincerity.
  • Finding Bible books / table of contents.
  • Careful gardener sees tiny shoot.
  • Jehovah notices sincere beginnings.
  • Hebrews 11:6 -> rewards seekers.
  • One short comment -> Jehovah sees courage and prayer.
  • Meeting attendance -> Jehovah sees effort.
  • Motive: love, not mere obligation.
  • Revelation 4:11 -> Creator.
  • Psalm 116:12 -> How can I respond?

Key line: Jehovah notices what others may never see.

Bridge: Four ways to build that good name.

How Can You Make a Good Name With God? (15) 16 min

1. Learn About Jehovah (15) 4 min

READ: John 17:3

  • Everlasting life -> coming to know Jehovah and Jesus.
  • More than facts.
  • Relationship.
  • Flower facts vs fragrance appreciation.
  • Bible study: not just identify information.
  • Ask:
    • What does this teach about Jehovah?
    • What does this teach about me?
    • What should I do?
  • Psalm 119:97 -> love + meditation.
  • New/rebuilding: friendship grows by regular attention.

Key line: Learning about Jehovah is the beginning of friendship.

Bridge: Knowing Jehovah naturally leads to obedience.

2. Obey Jehovah's Commandments (19) 4 min

READ: 1 John 5:3

Experience: David Meyers

  • May 22, 1985, Awake!
  • Ask: heard of David Meyers?
  • Ask: heard of Kareem Abdul-Jabbar?
  • Kareem -> one of NBA's greatest.
  • David Meyers:
    • UCLA national champion.
    • 1975 Lakers draft: 2nd overall.
    • Contract: $1.6M / 5 years.
    • Today's comparison: roughly $70M / 5 years for modern 2nd pick.
    • Included in trade for Kareem.
  • Clarify: not equal to Kareem.
  • But trade shows value / potential / what could have been.
  • Walked away from NBA.
  • Bigger offers / bigger payday possible.
  • Values changed:
    • Jehovah.
    • study.
    • meetings.
    • preaching.
    • family.
  • Basketball conflicted with spiritual responsibilities.
  • He chose obedience.
  • Obedience shaped by love.

Key line: He could build a name in sports, but chose a name with Jehovah.

Personal landing: What choice now shows Jehovah I want a good name with him?

3. Imitate Jehovah's Qualities (23) 3 min

READ: Psalm 25:4

  • David's request: teach me your path.
  • Not: bless my chosen path.
  • Imitation = thinking and feeling closer to Jehovah.
  • Amos 5:15 -> hate bad, love good.
  • Look at Jesus.
  • John 14:9 -> Jesus reflects Father.
  • Jesus:
    • compassion.
    • courage.
    • patience.
    • loyalty.
  • Ask:
    • How did Jesus treat weak people?
    • How did he respond to pressure?
    • How can I imitate today?

Key line: A good name is not only avoiding wrong; it is becoming someone Jehovah is pleased to remember.

4. Pray to Jehovah (26) 5 min

READ: Philippians 4:6

  • Jehovah speaks through Bible.
  • We speak through prayer.
  • Friendship cannot grow if only one speaks.
  • Voice messages but never reply.
  • Prayer = real communication.
  • Not performance.
  • Not impressive vocabulary.
  • Sincere heart.
  • Child to loving father.
  • 1 Peter 5:7 -> Jehovah cares.
  • Psalm 55:22 -> throw burden.
  • Pray specifically:
    • control speech today.
    • patience with this person.
    • wisdom for this decision.
  • Four steps together:
    • learn = listen.
    • obey = trust.
    • imitate = love his ways.
    • pray = rely on him.
  • Garden picture: light, protection, growth, life.

Key line: Prayer keeps the relationship alive.

Act Now (31) 4 min

READ: Isaiah 55:6

  • Search while he may be found.
  • Do not delay.
  • "When life is less busy" -> life stays busy.
  • Mistakes? not ruined.
  • Isaiah 1:18 -> set matters straight.
  • Repentant person can take one clean step today.
  • Return to garden:
    • small prayer.
    • study session.
    • obedience choice.
    • quiet honesty.
    • repentance.
    • meeting when tired.
  • People may not notice.
  • Jehovah does.

Final questions:

  • What does my name mean to Jehovah right now?
  • What step this week will make that name better?

Final line: A good name with Jehovah is worth more than anything this world can offer.
