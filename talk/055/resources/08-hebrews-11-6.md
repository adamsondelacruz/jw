# Hebrews 11:6

WOL: <https://wol.jw.org/en/wol/b/r1/lp-e/nwtsty/58/11#v=58:11:6>

## Outline Use

Supports the point that Jehovah rewards those earnestly seeking him.

## Key Thought

Faith includes confidence that Jehovah exists and that he responds to sincere seekers.

## REIA Prep

- Read: Optional supporting text.
- Explain: A good name with God begins with faith that moves a person to seek him.
- Illustrate: Roots move toward water even before anything is visible above ground.
- Apply: Encourage action: study, prayer, meetings, and obedience show that we are seeking Jehovah.
