# Watchtower 2007 11/15 p. 5 par. 5

WOL search: <https://wol.jw.org/en/wol/s/r1/lp-e?q=w07+11%2F15+5&p=par&r=occ&st=e>

## Outline Use

Supports the point that pursuing wealth, fame, or popularity does not bring true happiness or lasting meaning.

## Key Thought

A person can gain public recognition and still feel empty if life is centered on self-promotion rather than pleasing God.

## REIA Prep

- Read: Use only if needed; the outline does not require reading this reference aloud.
- Explain: The issue is not having money or being known. The danger is making those things the measure of a successful life.
- Illustrate: A tree with shiny leaves but shallow roots may look healthy until dry weather comes.
- Apply: Help listeners compare short-lived reputation with people to lasting approval from Jehovah.
