# Psalm 119:97

WOL: <https://wol.jw.org/en/wol/b/r1/lp-e/nwtsty/19/119#v=19:119:97>

## Outline Use

Supports regular study and meditation on God's Word.

## Key Thought

Love for Jehovah's law leads a person to think deeply about it.

## REIA Prep

- Read: Optional supporting text.
- Explain: Meditation lets Bible truth move from the page into the heart.
- Illustrate: Rain that soaks slowly into soil helps roots more than water that runs off the surface.
- Apply: After reading, ask, "What does this teach me about Jehovah?"
