# Malachi 3:10

WOL: <https://wol.jw.org/en/wol/b/r1/lp-e/nwtsty/39/3#v=39:3:10>

## Outline Use

Supports the point that Jehovah generously rewards those who take steps to gain his favor.

## Key Thought

Jehovah blesses those who trust him and put worship first.

## REIA Prep

- Read: Optional supporting text.
- Explain: Jehovah's generosity is greater than anything we give up to serve him.
- Illustrate: Clouds that look heavy do not take from the earth permanently; they return water in rain.
- Apply: Encourage listeners to test Jehovah's goodness by taking concrete spiritual steps.
