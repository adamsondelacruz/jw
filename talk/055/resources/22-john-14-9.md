# John 14:9

WOL: <https://wol.jw.org/en/wol/b/r1/lp-e/nwtsty/43/14#v=43:14:9>

## Outline Use

Supports imitating Jesus, who perfectly reflected Jehovah's qualities.

## Key Thought

Jesus shows us what Jehovah is like in human life.

## REIA Prep

- Read: Optional supporting text.
- Explain: To know Jehovah's qualities clearly, watch how Jesus treated people and obeyed his Father.
- Illustrate: Still water can reflect the sky clearly; Jesus reflected Jehovah without distortion.
- Apply: When unsure how to imitate Jehovah, study Jesus' words and reactions.
