# James 4:8

WOL: <https://wol.jw.org/en/wol/b/r1/lp-e/nwtsty/59/4#v=59:4:8>

## Outline Use

Supports the point that learning about Jehovah draws us closer to him.

## Key Thought

Jehovah responds when we take steps toward him.

## REIA Prep

- Read: Optional supporting text.
- Explain: Friendship with Jehovah grows by movement in his direction.
- Illustrate: Two people on opposite sides of a garden path become nearer when one starts walking forward.
- Apply: A person can draw close through prayer, study, clean conduct, and association with Jehovah's people.
