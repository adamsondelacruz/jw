# 1 Peter 5:7

WOL: <https://wol.jw.org/en/wol/b/r1/lp-e/nwtsty/60/5#v=60:5:7>

## Outline Use

Supports the point that prayer is real communication with God, who cares for us.

## Key Thought

Jehovah wants us to throw our anxieties on him because he cares.

## REIA Prep

- Read: Optional supporting text.
- Explain: Prayer is not ceremony; it is trustful dependence.
- Illustrate: A branch weighed down by snow bends until the weight slides off; prayer helps us unload weight onto Jehovah.
- Apply: Invite listeners to name anxieties honestly in prayer.
