# Psalm 55:22

WOL: <https://wol.jw.org/en/wol/b/r1/lp-e/nwtsty/19/55#v=19:55:22>

## Outline Use

Supports relying on Jehovah rather than ourselves.

## Key Thought

Jehovah can sustain those who throw their burden on him.

## REIA Prep

- Read: Optional supporting text.
- Explain: Relying on Jehovah means handing him the weight, not merely describing it.
- Illustrate: A young tree survives wind when tied to a firm stake.
- Apply: Prayer should be followed by trust in Jehovah's direction.
