# Psalm 25:4

WOL: <https://wol.jw.org/en/wol/b/r1/lp-e/nwtsty/19/25#v=19:25:4>

## Outline Use

Read in section: **Imitate Jehovah's qualities**.

## Key Thought

We can ask Jehovah to teach us his ways.

## REIA Prep

- Read: Psalm 25:4.
- Explain: Making a good name with Jehovah includes learning his path, not inventing our own.
- Illustrate: A hiker in mist needs a marked trail, not just enthusiasm.
- Apply: Before decisions, ask, "What path would Jehovah teach me to take?"
