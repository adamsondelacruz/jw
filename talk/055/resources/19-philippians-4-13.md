# Philippians 4:13

WOL: <https://wol.jw.org/en/wol/b/r1/lp-e/nwtsty/50/4#v=50:4:13>

## Outline Use

Supports the assurance that Jehovah will not disappoint those who ask for help.

## Key Thought

Jehovah can give strength to endure and obey.

## REIA Prep

- Read: Optional supporting text.
- Explain: The strength is not self-confidence; it comes through Jehovah's support.
- Illustrate: A vine can carry fruit because it is supported by a strong frame.
- Apply: Identify one area of obedience where prayer for strength is needed.
