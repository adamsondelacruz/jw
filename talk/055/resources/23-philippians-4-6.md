# Philippians 4:6

WOL: <https://wol.jw.org/en/wol/b/r1/lp-e/nwtsty/50/4#v=50:4:6>

## Outline Use

Read in section: **Pray to Jehovah**.

## Key Thought

Jehovah invites us to bring our concerns to him in prayer.

## REIA Prep

- Read: Philippians 4:6.
- Explain: Prayer includes requests, supplication, and thanks.
- Illustrate: A leaf releases water through tiny openings to stay healthy; prayer lets pressure out before anxiety overwhelms the heart.
- Apply: Encourage specific prayers, not only general ones.
