# Watchtower 2012 12/1 p. 11

## Outline Use

Supports Malachi 3:16 and Jehovah's personal interest in those who honor his name.

## Key Thought

Jehovah's remembrance is warm and purposeful, not mechanical.

## REIA Prep

- Read: Background reference.
- Explain: The "book of remembrance" idea highlights Jehovah's approval and intention to reward faithfulness.
- Illustrate: A family keeps treasured names and memories because those people matter.
- Apply: When a person begins to respect Jehovah's name, Jehovah does not overlook it.
