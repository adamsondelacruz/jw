# Matthew 6:19, 20

WOL: <https://wol.jw.org/en/wol/b/r1/lp-e/nwtsty/40/6#v=40:6:19-40:6:20>

## Outline Use

Supports the contrast between temporary human achievements and treasures with God.

## Key Thought

Spiritual treasure lasts; earthly treasure can decay, disappear, or disappoint.

## REIA Prep

- Read: Optional supporting text.
- Explain: Jesus redirects ambition from accumulating things to gaining Jehovah's approval.
- Illustrate: Morning dew can sparkle beautifully, but it is gone when the sun rises.
- Apply: When choosing goals, ask whether the result will still matter to Jehovah.
