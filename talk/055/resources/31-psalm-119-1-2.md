# Psalm 119:1, 2

WOL: <https://wol.jw.org/en/wol/b/r1/lp-e/nwtsty/19/119#v=19:119:1-19:119:2>

## Outline Use

Supports the concluding point that a good name with God leads to genuine happiness.

## Key Thought

Those who follow Jehovah's reminders and seek him wholeheartedly are truly happy.

## REIA Prep

- Read: Optional supporting text.
- Explain: Happiness is connected with a clean course and wholehearted seeking of Jehovah.
- Illustrate: A healthy plant is not happy because of one sunny day, but because it keeps receiving light, water, and good soil.
- Apply: Close with confidence: a good name with Jehovah is worth pursuing now.
