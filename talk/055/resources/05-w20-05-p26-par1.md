# Watchtower 2020.05 p. 26 par. 1

WOL search: <https://wol.jw.org/en/wol/s/r1/lp-e?q=w20.05+26&p=par&r=occ&st=e>

## Outline Use

Supports the point that the Bible encourages us to make a good name with God.

## Key Thought

The safest and most meaningful reputation is the one Jehovah recognizes.

## REIA Prep

- Read: Use as background, not as a main reading.
- Explain: Jehovah's memory is perfect and his approval is not shallow.
- Illustrate: A seed hidden in soil may be unseen by people, but the Creator knows its life and growth.
- Apply: Encourage listeners to value quiet acts of faithfulness that Jehovah notices.
