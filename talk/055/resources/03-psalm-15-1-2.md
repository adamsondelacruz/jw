# Psalm 15:1, 2

WOL: <https://wol.jw.org/en/wol/b/r1/lp-e/nwtsty/19/15#v=19:15:1-19:15:2>

## Outline Use

Supports the point that the Bible encourages us to make a good name with God.

## Key Thought

Jehovah welcomes those who live with integrity, practice righteousness, and speak truthfully.

## REIA Prep

- Read: Optional supporting text.
- Explain: A good name with Jehovah is built by the pattern of daily conduct.
- Illustrate: A bird returns to a safe nesting place because it is dependable. Jehovah's friendship is for those who choose a dependable course.
- Apply: Integrity at home, work, school, and congregation life all contribute to our name before Jehovah.
