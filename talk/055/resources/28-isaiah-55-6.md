# Isaiah 55:6

WOL: <https://wol.jw.org/en/wol/b/r1/lp-e/nwtsty/23/55#v=23:55:6>

## Outline Use

Supports the appeal to act now while there is still time.

## Key Thought

Jehovah invites people to seek him while the opportunity is open.

## REIA Prep

- Read: Optional supporting text.
- Explain: Delay can be spiritually dangerous because opportunity is not endless.
- Illustrate: A season for planting does not stay open all year; wise farmers act at the right time.
- Apply: Encourage immediate steps: study, prayer, meetings, repentance, and obedience.
