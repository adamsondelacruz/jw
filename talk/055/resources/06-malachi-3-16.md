# Malachi 3:16

WOL: <https://wol.jw.org/en/wol/b/r1/lp-e/nwtsty/39/3#v=39:3:16>

## Outline Use

Read in section: **Why make a good name with God?**

## Key Thought

Jehovah notices those who fear him and meditate appreciatively on his name.

## REIA Prep

- Read: Malachi 3:16.
- Explain: Jehovah pays attention to sincere spiritual conversations and thoughts.
- Illustrate: A careful gardener labels and remembers each young plant, even before it bears fruit.
- Apply: New ones can be assured that Jehovah notices sincere steps, even small ones.
