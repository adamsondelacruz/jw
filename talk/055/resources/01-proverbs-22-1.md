# Proverbs 22:1

WOL: <https://wol.jw.org/en/wol/b/r1/lp-e/nwtsty/20/22#v=20:22:1>

## Outline Use

Read in section: **A good name is valuable**.

## Key Thought

A good name, or reputation, is more valuable than material wealth.

## REIA Prep

- Read: Proverbs 22:1.
- Explain: Reputation is not just what people call us; it is the trust attached to our name.
- Illustrate: A clear spring is valued because people know the water is safe. A good name is similar: it refreshes confidence before we even speak.
- Apply: Ask, "Whose view of my name matters most: people's approval, or Jehovah's?"
