# 1 John 5:3

WOL: <https://wol.jw.org/en/wol/b/r1/lp-e/nwtsty/62/5#v=62:5:3>

## Outline Use

Read in section: **Obey Jehovah's commandments**.

## Key Thought

Love for God is shown by obedience, and his commandments are not oppressive.

## REIA Prep

- Read: 1 John 5:3.
- Explain: Jehovah's commands protect us and help us flourish.
- Illustrate: Riverbanks do not imprison a river; they guide it so it can flow with strength.
- Apply: When a command feels hard, view it as Jehovah's loving guidance, not a burden.
