# Luke 11:11-13

WOL: <https://wol.jw.org/en/wol/b/r1/lp-e/nwtsty/42/11#v=42:11:11-42:11:13>

## Outline Use

Supports asking Jehovah for help to obey him.

## Key Thought

Jehovah gives good help to those who ask sincerely.

## REIA Prep

- Read: Optional supporting text.
- Explain: Jesus reasons from imperfect human parents to Jehovah's perfect generosity.
- Illustrate: A healthy tree supplies sap to a branch that remains connected.
- Apply: Prayer for holy spirit is practical when obedience feels beyond our own strength.
