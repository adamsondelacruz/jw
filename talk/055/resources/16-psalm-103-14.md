# Psalm 103:14

WOL: <https://wol.jw.org/en/wol/b/r1/lp-e/nwtsty/19/103#v=19:103:14>

## Outline Use

Supports the point that Jehovah is patient and understands human limitations.

## Key Thought

Jehovah remembers our frailty and deals with us compassionately.

## REIA Prep

- Read: Optional supporting text.
- Explain: Jehovah does not expect perfection from imperfect people.
- Illustrate: A skilled gardener supports a weak young stem instead of snapping it off.
- Apply: Struggling to obey should move us to seek help, not to give up.
