# John 17:3

WOL: <https://wol.jw.org/en/wol/b/r1/lp-e/nwtsty/43/17#v=43:17:3>

## Outline Use

Read in section: **Learn about Jehovah**.

## Key Thought

Coming to know Jehovah and Jesus leads to everlasting life.

## REIA Prep

- Read: John 17:3.
- Explain: "Know" means more than facts. It means developing a real relationship.
- Illustrate: Recognizing a tree by one leaf is different from knowing its fruit, shade, seasons, and strength.
- Apply: Bible study should help a person know Jehovah's thinking, feelings, and ways.
