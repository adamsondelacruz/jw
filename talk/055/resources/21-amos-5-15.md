# Amos 5:15

WOL: <https://wol.jw.org/en/wol/b/r1/lp-e/nwtsty/30/5#v=30:5:15>

## Outline Use

Supports learning to love what Jehovah loves and hate what he hates.

## Key Thought

Jehovah wants moral feeling, not just outward compliance.

## REIA Prep

- Read: Optional supporting text.
- Explain: Imitating Jehovah means training our likes and dislikes by his standards.
- Illustrate: A healthy body learns to reject spoiled food because it protects life.
- Apply: Ask, "Am I training my heart to enjoy what Jehovah calls good?"
