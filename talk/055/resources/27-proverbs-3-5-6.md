# Proverbs 3:5, 6

WOL: <https://wol.jw.org/en/wol/b/r1/lp-e/nwtsty/20/3#v=20:3:5-20:3:6>

## Outline Use

Supports relying on Jehovah rather than ourselves.

## Key Thought

Trust in Jehovah means including him in decisions and not leaning on our own understanding.

## REIA Prep

- Read: Optional supporting text.
- Explain: A good name with God grows when we let Jehovah direct our paths.
- Illustrate: Migrating birds survive long journeys by following reliable guidance built into creation, not by guessing.
- Apply: Ask before decisions, "Have I acknowledged Jehovah in this?"
