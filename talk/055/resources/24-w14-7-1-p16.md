## Outline Use

Supports the point that Jehovah speaks to us through the Bible and we speak to him through prayer.

## Key Thought

Communication with Jehovah is two-way: we listen through Scripture and respond in prayer.

## REIA Prep

- Read: Background reference.
- Explain: A relationship grows by listening and speaking.
- Illustrate: Birds do not navigate by only making sound; they also listen and respond to signals around them.
- Apply: Pair Bible reading with prayer so study becomes personal communication.
