# Revelation 4:11

WOL: <https://wol.jw.org/en/wol/b/r1/lp-e/nwtsty/66/4#v=66:4:11>

## Outline Use

Supports the point that we owe Jehovah worship and obedience because he created us.

## Key Thought

Jehovah deserves honor because all life exists by his will.

## REIA Prep

- Read: Optional supporting text.
- Explain: Worship is reasonable because Jehovah is the Source of life.
- Illustrate: A river depends on its spring; without the source, the river stops.
- Apply: Obedience is not blind control. It is respect for the One who gave us life.
