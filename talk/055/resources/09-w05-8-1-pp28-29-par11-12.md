# Watchtower 2005 8/1 pp. 28-29 pars. 11-12

## Outline Use

Supports Hebrews 11:6 and Jehovah's reward for those who earnestly seek him.

## Key Thought

Jehovah is approachable, and he rewards sincere effort to know and please him.

## REIA Prep

- Read: Background reference.
- Explain: Earnest seeking is active, not casual curiosity.
- Illustrate: A bee does not drift randomly; it searches carefully for nectar and returns to what gives life.
- Apply: Help listeners see that regular spiritual habits are evidence of earnest seeking.
