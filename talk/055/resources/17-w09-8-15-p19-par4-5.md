# Watchtower 2009 8/15 p. 19 pars. 4-5

## Outline Use

Supports Jehovah's patience and understanding of human limitations.

## Key Thought

Jehovah knows both our desire to please him and the weakness we battle.

## REIA Prep

- Read: Background reference.
- Explain: Jehovah's patience should not make us casual; it should make us courageous.
- Illustrate: A parent helping a child learn to walk expects stumbles, but still encourages progress.
- Apply: Encourage listeners to keep taking the next obedient step.
