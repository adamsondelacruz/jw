# Isaiah 1:18

WOL: <https://wol.jw.org/en/wol/b/r1/lp-e/nwtsty/23/1#v=23:1:18>

## Outline Use

Supports the point that even those who made serious mistakes can set matters straight with Jehovah.

## Key Thought

Jehovah is willing to forgive and cleanse repentant ones.

## REIA Prep

- Read: Optional supporting text.
- Explain: A damaged reputation with God can be repaired through repentance and Jehovah's mercy.
- Illustrate: Rain can wash dust from leaves so they can breathe and receive light again.
- Apply: Do not let past mistakes stop present action toward Jehovah.
